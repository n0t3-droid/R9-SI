# Weißstadt — the owner's reference pictures for villages and towns (9 Oct 2026)

Two reference pictures the owner made (`hall_full.jpg`: a vast white vaulted hall with crystals, a grand staircase,
balustrades, market stalls under striped awnings, lanterns, barrels and crates, people in robes and armour;
`innenstadt_full.jpg` and the video frames `video/`: a white-stone town street under the same hall — shops with
storefront windows, shop signs, striped awnings, street lamps, a central fountain, cobblestones, flower boxes,
hanging vines, café chairs, people with shopping bags) and the owner's segment crops of them (`hall/`, `innenstadt/`,
one folder per element: arched windows, balustrade, crystal formations, grand staircase, hanging lantern, market
stall, potted plant, stained glass, striped awning, vines, wooden barrel / beams / crate / table, storefront window,
shop sign, street lamp, central fountain, cobblestone street, flower box, shopping bag …).

The owner's words: the villages are too small and too cramped for their many villagers, and their houses should look
like these pictures — and "keine Lego-Villagers, wir wollen schon Realismus": the people keep our own realistic
proportions (folkmodel.js), never figure-toy proportions.

The owner's two corrections (9 Oct, noon): (1) the town in the pictures is INDOORS — the whole street with its shops,
fountain and cafés stands inside the vast white vaulted hall; our villages were outdoors, that is wrong for this
style: the Weißstadt is a hall city. (2) NO CRYSTALS anywhere — "ich mag die nicht, ob groß oder klein": no crystal
formations, clusters, chandeliers or spires in the town, the hall or the world; where the references show crystals,
we use lanterns, gold trims, glass and light.

These pictures are design references only: nothing in them is copied into the game. Everything the game shows is
generated by our own code with our own names (no "Innenstadt"/"Inciers" signs: the shop signs are our own words).
