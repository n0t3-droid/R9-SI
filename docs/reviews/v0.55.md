## Fun and wonder — per player type, honestly

- **Builder / dreamer — the richest ten minutes so far, but less calm.** A sign, a musician, a well, kites, a caravan, a
  traveller, a map scrap: ten moments in five minutes, families who move into the house you build, nine festivals. The
  wonder is real at the 180-s vista and the festival nights. What loses him: moments that flash by tiny and in pairs, a
  54-s hole over the Sky Isles, the flock-soot at dusk. Session **7/11** (v0.54.1 10/11 — the drop is spam and variety,
  not emptiness).
- **RPG / adventurer — still no payoff.** The fairy adventures are sweet but finished in seconds; the outpost, the
  Abenteuer's promise, is not freed by the walker for the third review running, and the walk then pushes through dark
  leaves for 75 s. Session **4/11** (6/11). This is the type the release served least.
- **Explorer / space — the wonder is back.** The Leuchtpfad is now a true 3-D journey (ring giant 3.4) and chapter 9's
  nebula with Flimmi's birth is the most beautiful thing the game has shown (3.8, and as rich on Very Low). The arrival
  on Opal is still a white page and ×8 is still unproven.
- **Child / fairy-tale — warm.** Riddles, a cloud bridge, a lost lamb-light brought home, a story by the fire, a dance
  invitation; gentle and age-right. Missing: seeing the little folk themselves.
