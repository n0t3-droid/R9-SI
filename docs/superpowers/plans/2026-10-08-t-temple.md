# Trailer shots D and E · Gate Temple approach and Hangar Hall · Implementation Plan

**Goal:** make shot D (the Gate Temple approach: the portal veil, the landing strip, the shuttle flying in with its
thrusters and dust, the palm city seen through a portal) and shot E (the Hangar Hall: radar screens, blinking consoles,
pulsing wall strips, the shuttle over the pad, the two heroes with blasters, the hex corridor to the city) true in the
game, judged by pictures taken from the places the frames were shot from. Everything animated sits behind **Animierte
Anzeigen** (`settings.anim`), which is off on Very Low, where it costs nothing.

**Spec:** `TRAILER.md` §4 (shots D and E) and the fidelity brief `docs/trailer/README.md`. Frames: `docs/trailer/frames/`.

**History:** a first pass (8 Oct) built the veil texture, the hall crew, the holo radar, the pulsing strips, the console
blinks, the runway bars, the hangar shuttle's thrusters and the 7-high corridor; the machine restarted before its browser
run finished and the coordinator saved it as the WIP commit 2fad5c3. The second pass (9 Oct, this document's Results)
ran the browser test on that WIP (run 1), found the veil never reached its shader, and added the shuttle's loop, the
crew's walk, the corridor's veil and the runway lights; run 3 is the proof.

## Method

1. The frames are read and described below (what I see).
2. The game is photographed from the same places by `test/t_temple.mjs` (one browser run frames every shot; the shuttle
   and the crew are put at the moment of their loops each shot needs with `jump()`): `TAG=base` (git HEAD of the first
   pass), `TAG=after` (run 1, the WIP), `TAG=final` (run 3, this pass). Pictures: `test/shots/trailer_<shot>_<TAG>.png`.
   Each shot prints a `diag` line (the crew and where it walks, the hangar shuttle, the flight's leg, position and draws).
3. The differences are listed in order of how much they change the impression, fixed from the top, and the pictures are
   taken again.
4. `test/t_temple.js` (node) pins the geometry, the veil's mesher route, the spawns, the shuttle's path, the crew's walk
   and the gating; `test/t_temple.mjs` ends with a verdict (no page errors, crew, shuttles built, drawn, walking).

## Shot D · Gate Temple approach

### What I see (t1_10, t1_11, t3_03, t3_04)
- **t1_10:** from low over the plaza, the shuttle (white and orange, blue canopy, two engines throwing blue-white flames)
  flies at the temple; the octagonal portal glows amber-white; slate-blue tiers with amber windows, saucer towers, a
  planet in a dusk sky.
- **t1_11:** closer, from behind the shuttle as it reaches the portal: the portal's inner frames glow orange, the hall
  lies behind it.
- **t3_03:** the plaza looking up the runway: the shuttle comes in low, its thrusters throwing long orange-white flames;
  amber windows, cyan slits, orange strips, violet crystals, palms and hedges.
- **t3_04:** the portal as a window: a dark octagonal frame, an orange-white rim, blue-white energy crackling along the
  rim's inside, and through the clear middle the palm city (villas, palms, a starry dusk sky); a road with lane marks;
  amber lights on the walls at the sides. Our city lies north, behind the back door and the hex corridor, so this view
  is the corridor's city end, not the plaza gate.

### What the game showed (run 1, the WIP: `test/shots/trailer_<shot>_after.png`)
- `approach`: the temple from the far end of the plaza; the saucer towers are there; cream-lit slate tiers, amber
  windows; the runway's "light bars" are grey tiles (the light bar block's top face is plain stone). No shuttle flies.
- `portal`, `veil`: the gate's opening is a tiled light-blue sheet, the same pattern repeated in every block, with the
  hall faint behind it. **Cause:** the WIP routed the veil to the PORTAL kind only for model-shaped blocks; the veil is a
  cube, so all its faces went through the glass branch (texture × light), and the veil shader never ran.

### Ordered diffs and what was done
1. **The veil** (t3_04's colour and see-through). The mesher sends every veil face as `KIND.PORTAL` and hands each corner
   its distance to the veil's rim (`u` = distance × 16: the nearest non-veil cell in the face's plane, within 8 blocks of
   the padded section; a cell beyond the section counts as veil, so a gate cut by a chunk edge keeps the rim of its other
   half). The shader draws an orange-white rim on that distance, blue-white energy with crackling filaments a few blocks
   in, and a clear middle (alpha 0.05–0.13) through which the hall or the city shows; animation off: one texel read
   instead of the noise (`mesher.js` emitVeilQuad, `shaders.js` TRANS_FS).
2. **The portal as a window** (vgen 6): the corridor's city end carries a veil cut to a 7 × 7 octagon, framed by orange
   light bars; from inside the corridor the palm city shows through it (`gen_temple.js`).
3. **The shuttle flies in** (t3_03, t1_10, t1_11): `hangarflight.js` (below, shot E).
4. **The landing strip** (vgen 6): amber neon bars three blocks wide across the runway's centre line every 8 blocks
   (their tops glow; the WIP's light bars were dark from above).

Unchanged: the tiers, windows, slits, strips, crystals, plaza and palms; the stone's slate texture (`SLATE` in
`tex_recipes4.js` is already slate-blue; it reads cream under the low golden sun — a light/sky matter, see gaps).

### What the game showed (run 1)
- `hall`, `pad`: the octagon floor lines, the cyan ceiling ring, the console rows with blue stools, the walls of holo
  screens; the hangar shuttle (full size) parked on the pad, its tail and three glowing thrusters toward the camera, a
  tan dust cloud; the heroes small at the sides.
- The holo walls read as a grid of small framed screens (Klassisch frames every holo block; only Nahtlos joins them into
  one pane, `voxtech.js` ctxHook.holo) — the WIP's radar is drawn on the screens' content pixels only, so no radar circle
  reads.
- `backdoor`: the 7 × 7 corridor with dark panels and cyan strips, the city at its end; no heroes there.

### Ordered diffs and what was done
1. **The shuttle comes and goes** (t1_19, t1_13/16/17; t3_03, t1_10/11): `hangarflight.js`, a runtime actor like the sky
   traffic, owned by `hall.js`. One shuttle (the `shuttle` archetype in the white-and-orange Starforge livery, drawn at
   0.62 so its 9.3 m span passes the 13 m mouth) flies a 76 s loop: low down the runway from the canyon (6 m over the
   plaza on its last 40 m), in through the portal braking to a hover over the pad, 3 s settling, 5 s on the pad with its
   gear down, a lift and a half turn on the spot, out through the mouth and a teardrop over the canyon (≥ 16 m over the
   highest mesa of five seeds). Engine plumes (blue-white in the air, orange in the hall), lift jets under the hull and
   dust (a cool haze inside) while it is low. While it flies, the hangar's parked shuttle is "out" (hidden, not solid, not
   targetable); when another ship stands on the pad (an older world's pad ship, a player's ship) it lands on the runway in
   front of the mouth instead and hides nothing. Any world whose Gate Temple stands (vgen ≥ 3); no block, no save.
2. **The heroes walk out to the city** (t2_20, t1_21, t1_12): a 125 s round — 20 s at the consoles, along the wall clear
   of the parked shuttle's wings, through the back door and the corridor, 15 s at its city end looking out, and back
   (1.6 m/s, the stride from the metres walked, blasters up). The blue hero's console spot is now the orange one's mirror
   image about the hall's axis (x −3.9; it stood half in a stool's cell).
3. **Radar screens and the hall's tone** (t1_16/17): not changed in this pass — see gaps.

## Very Low and cost

Very Low is the `potato` preset, `anim: false`. With the animation off: the shuttle's loop builds nothing, moves nothing
and draws nothing (node test: no design request, no engine, no particle; the parked shuttle stays on the pad); the crew
stands at the consoles; the veil reads one texel instead of the noise (cheaper than the glass branch it took before:
texture × light, fresnel, sky reflection). The new mesher work (the rim distance) runs in the workers, only for veil
faces. Measured in the browser test: the hall view, 1296 × 704, swiftshader on the shared 2-CPU machine (load 7–15), five
blocks of six frames after three warm-up frames; the minimum is the cost. Numbers in Results.

## Known gaps

- **Radar screens / navy tone (t1_16, t1_17):** in Klassisch every holo block is its own framed screen with its own
  widget; the frame's two big radar displays per wall need the screens joined into one pane (what Nahtlos does) and the
  radar drawn on the pane, not only on the widget pixels. That is a look decision for all holo screens (villages, civic
  buildings), not done here. The hall's tone is close (navy walls, cyan strips and ring) but brighter and more lilac at the
  far end than the frames.
- **The stone colour:** the slate texture reads cream under the low golden sun; the frames are at dusk. The dusk shots
  (`*_dusk`) show the hour of the frames. Light and sky belong to f-light.
- **The shuttle's size:** the flying shuttle is drawn at 0.62 (it must pass the 13 m mouth and turn in the 21 m hall);
  the parked hangar shuttle (shown when the animation is off) is full size.
- **t1_21's second shuttle** flying over the city, t2_20's shuttle in the city: not done (the sky traffic may pass).
- **Starships and villages in the start-up race** (`game.js`): unchanged from HEAD (fixed for the hall only, first pass).
