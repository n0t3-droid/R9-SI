# Trailer shots D and E · Gate Temple approach and Hangar Hall · Implementation Plan

**Goal:** make shot D (the Gate Temple approach: the portal veil, the landing strip, the hangar shuttle with its thrusters
and dust, the palm city seen through the portal) and shot E (the Hangar Hall: radar screens, blinking consoles, pulsing wall
strips, the two heroes with blasters, the hex corridor to the city) true in the game, judged by pictures taken from the
places the frames were shot from. The animation sits behind **Animierte Anzeigen** (`settings.anim`), which is off on
Very Low, where it costs nothing.

**Spec:** `TRAILER.md` §4 (shots D and E) and the fidelity brief `docs/trailer/README.md`. Frames: `docs/trailer/frames/`.

## Frame mapping (a note on the brief)

The brief's frame list was corrected on 8 Oct (evening; its first list was off by one row). With the corrected list, shot D
is t1_10–11 (the Gate Temple), t3_03 (the approach and landing) and t3_04 (the portal as a window); shot E is t1_12–13,
t1_16–17 (the pad view), t1_19 (the hall) and the corridor (t2_20, t1_21); the palm city (t1_21, t1_22, t1_24) is shot F.
This plan takes t1_16/17 as shot E's pad view (`pad` in `test/t_temple.mjs`) and t3_03/t3_04 as shot D. t1_12/t1_13 are
listed under E by the corrected brief; they were not re-read for this pass. The brief itself is not edited here.

## Method

1. The frames are read and described below (what I see).
2. The game is photographed from the same places by `test/t_temple.mjs`: `TAG=base` (git HEAD, built into a scratch
   folder) and `TAG=after` (this branch). Pictures: `test/shots/trailer_<shot>_<TAG>.png` for `approach`, `portal`, `veil`
   (D), `hall`, `pad`, `backdoor`, `city` (E), and `verylow_hall*` (Very Low). Each shot also prints a `diag` line: the hall's
   crew, the hall's draw calls, and the pad shuttle's state, GPU upload and draw calls.
3. The differences are listed in order of how much they change the impression, fixed from the top, and the pictures are
   taken again.
4. `test/t_temple.js` (node) pins the geometry, the spawns, the vgen gating and the animation gating.

## Shot D · Gate Temple approach

### What I see (t3_03, t3_04)
- **t3_03:** the plaza, looking up the runway at the temple. A shuttle (white hull, orange stripes, blue canopy) comes in
  low, its two engines throwing long orange-white flames. Above it the stepped temple has amber windows, cyan slits and
  orange light strips; violet crystals stand at the sides; palms and hedges line the plaza.
- **t3_04:** through the octagonal portal a blue-white energy veil fills the opening, its rim glowing orange-white. Beyond
  it is the palm city (stepped villas with turquoise glass bands, palms, a starry dusk sky with a planet), and on the road
  a red-white shuttle with orange thrusters.

### What the game shows (base)
- `test/shots/trailer_approach_base.png`: the temple from the far end of the plaza. Cream-white tiers, amber windows, two
  beacon towers, cyan-edged flagstones, palms and lanterns. No shuttle, no light bars on the runway.
- `test/shots/trailer_portal_base.png`, `test/shots/trailer_veil_base.png`: the gate's opening is a flat orange sheet (an
  opaque emissive texture). Nothing shows through it: no hall, no palm city.
- The stone reads cream and light; the frame's temple is slate-blue (see Known gaps).

### Ordered diffs
1. **The portal veil** was an opaque, glowing sheet. Now: the texture is one soft, seamless sheet (`tex_recipes4.js`; the
   old radial term per block drew a grid of circles over the veil, and is gone); the mesher sends the veil faces to the
   translucent pass as the PORTAL kind (`mesher.js`, `meta.veil`); the shader's `TRANS_FS` draws a deeper, dimmer blue
   with two drifting noise layers in world space when the animation is on (`shaders.js`), and a flat swirl when it is off.
   The same block is the station's portal ring (`gen_station.js`), which therefore changes too.
2. **The landing strip:** orange light bars down the runway's centre line, every 8 blocks (`gen_temple.js`, vgen 6).
3. **The hangar shuttle** on the pad is a parked shuttle (23 m, the `shuttle` archetype), flagged `hangar` (vgen 6). With the
   animation on and within 48 blocks of the player its thrusters burn (engine trails at low thrust) and dust is kicked up
   from the pad under the nozzles (`starships.js` `hangarFx`: spawned at pad level, 30% of frames per nozzle, rising only
   slightly). The first version spawned the dust at nozzle height and let it climb: it covered the lower hull in the
   pictures, so it was lowered.

Unchanged from HEAD: the temple's stepped tiers, amber windows, cyan slits, orange strips, crystals, the plaza and palms.

## Shot E · Hangar Hall

### What I see (t1_16, t1_17, t1_19; the corridor t2_20 / t1_21; the city t1_22 / t1_24, listed under F by the brief)
- **t1_16 / t1_17 (the pad view):** a navy-cyan hall. A white-grey shuttle sits on the pad inside the octagon rings, flames
  under it (t1_17). Holo screens with radar rings and readouts fill both walls; consoles with button panels and stools
  stand in front of them. Two heroes (blue suit with orange trim, orange hair on the second) hold blasters at the consoles.
- **t1_19:** from the pad end, the shuttle hangs in the portal opening at the back of the hall, thrust glowing; the two
  heroes stand in the foreground, blasters down.
- **the corridor (t2_20, t1_21):** out through the hexagonal corridor. Hex panels with cyan icons line both walls, a cyan hex
  light row runs along the ceiling, the palm city lies beyond. The two heroes stand at the corridor's exit; in t1_21 a
  shuttle flies at the right with orange thrust.
- **the city (t1_22, t1_24):** the palm city close up: stepped villas with cyan window bands, palms, cyan light lines on the path.

### What the game shows (base)
- `test/shots/trailer_pad_base.png`, `test/shots/trailer_hall_base.png`: the octagon floor lines, the holo screens, the
  consoles with blue stools and the hex-light ceiling are all there, static. No heroes stand in the hall. The ship on the
  pad is a black-violet pod with a lilac canopy (HEAD spawns the pad ship with a seed-chosen archetype), still, no exhaust.
- `test/shots/trailer_backdoor_base.png`: through the back door the palm city shows (white villas, palms), between the
  cyan frame walls; the way out is a plain 3-block path, not a hexagonal corridor.
- `test/shots/trailer_city_base.png`: the palm city from the back door's end.

### Ordered diffs
1. **Two heroes** in blue and orange armour stand in front of the console rows with blasters up (`hall.js`, `TEMPLE.crew`
   in `gen_temple.js`). They come with the chunks that hold them (`hallcrew` spawns, vgen 6), so a chunk that loads again
   does not add them twice. With the animation on, they turn their heads now and then.
   **The start-up race (`game.js`):** the hall is now created before the first `await` of a world start. Before, the
   columns of the start area could arrive during that await, and their crew spawns went to no hall (the browser run showed
   an empty crew list while the pad ship, from a later chunk, was spawned).
2. **Holo screens animate** (`shaders.js`, the holo branch): one radar per 3 × 3 block panel (a sweep that turns, two range
   rings, a crosshair, blips) and scrolling readout bars on the rest of the screen.
3. **Wall strips pulse:** a light runs along the walls (`u_pulseA`, `u_pulseB` = the layers of `vx_tech_hex` and
   `vx_tech_dark_strip`).
4. **Console tops blink** (`hall.js`): about one light in five, every 0.12 s, cyan with a few amber, only within 36 blocks
   of the hall and only with the animation on.
5. **The hex corridor** from the back door is 7 wide and 7 high (it was 3 high): hex panels down both sides, a cyan hex
   light row in the ceiling, grey chamfers (`gen_temple.js`, vgen 6).
6. **The pad shuttle** (as shot D): thrusters and dust.

Unchanged from HEAD: the octagon floor lines, the holo screens, the consoles and the hex-light ceiling of the hall.

## Very Low and cost

Very Low is the `potato` preset, which now has `anim: false`. Nothing animated runs there: the hall's ticks return early,
the hangar's thrusters and dust are skipped, the holo and pulse branches of the shader are skipped by a uniform test, and
the veil's swirl is flat (no noise is evaluated). What stays: the two hero models (static, within 70 blocks), the veil's
translucent pass (static), and the hangar shuttle itself (as in HEAD).

Measured with `VERYLOW=1` on the hall view (`hall`, 1296 × 704, swiftshader, 2 CPUs): three blocks of six frames each,
each after three warm-up frames; the minimum is the cost, because the machine is shared (its load was 7–15 during the runs).
Numbers are in Results.

## Known gaps

- **t3_03:** the shuttle flying in along the runway, banking, with its thrusters and the dust it kicks up. The game has one
  parked shuttle on the pad; the sky traffic does not fly this approach.
- **t1_19:** a shuttle flying through the hangar mouth into the hall. The game has only the parked one.
- **t2_20 / t1_21:** the two heroes at the corridor's exit. The game keeps them at the consoles.
- **t1_22 / t1_24 (shot F):** the palm city close up is shot F's content and is not changed here.
- **The approach's saucer towers** (x ±40, z 8, about 125 m out) are missing from the approach picture; the chunk data is the
  same as in the base run, so this looks like far-detail (LOD) state at the shot. Not investigated further.
- **The stone** reads cream under the dusk sun, not the frame's slate-blue. Not changed.
- **Starships and villages in the start-up race:** `game.js` creates the starships and villages after the same `await`;
  a ship or village spawn in the first columns of a world start can be lost the same way the hall's crew was. Fixed for the
  hall only (the hall is small and self-contained); the others are unchanged from HEAD.
- The colours are judged by eye against the frames; the suits use the existing hero palette, not the exact frame colours.
