# ✨ Wunder der Welt — plan (wave 2, W-A)

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-08-new-era-wave2-design.md` § W-A. Branch `wonders`.

## Decisions (where the code showed a better way than the spec's sketch)

1. **Sites need the terrain, so they live with the generator.** `VR.Wonders.site(g, i, j, spawn)` reads `g.surfaceY`,
   `g.climateAt`, the village sites (`VG.site`), the sky rails between them, outposts, citadels, sky islands and the
   Heartland's design zones — so it runs in the worker like `villages` (a new job `{type:'wonders', x, z, r, spawn}`).
   The spawn comes from the caller (`meta.worldSpawn`, which a guest gets from its host): computing it in the worker
   would plan the whole home town. Cells of 640, 60 %, up to 8 tries per cell; the spawn's cell searches the 250–450
   ring around the spawn (best gentle spot), so every new world has its first wonder within a short walk.
2. **Spacing is from what the generator actually builds, not only from village centres.** A village keeps
   `max(160, rad + 96)` (modern towns reach 230 blocks), rails and viaducts 56, outposts and their rails, citadels,
   islands overhead, the Heartland's temple (160) and its designed zones, the spawn 160, other sites ~300.
3. **One hidden Forge type.** The eight generators register as Forge type `wonder` (the kind is its style:
   `wonder:sternwarte#1a2b`) and are taken out of `Forge.types` right after, so the Studio's 🏭 chips and the Forge
   tests do not list them, while `Forge.parse/build/meta/job` — the worker job, the levelled lot, `/bau` plans — work
   unchanged. No change in `forge.js`.
4. **The rise is our own slicing over the forge placer's list.** `blue.forgePiece` (worker build, cached) and
   `blue.planAt` (levelled lot + verge) give the cells; `wonders_game.js` lays the clearing and filling down at once,
   then the structure row band by row band over ~3.5 s with the light deferred (`world.bulkSet(list, notify, true)` —
   one relight of the touched columns at the end instead of one per slice), glows along the rising edge and a
   synthesized chime. No undo entry: a wonder is the world's, not a player edit.
5. **"Player edits" = differences from the generator, minus nature.** Columns never saved and never changed are
   pristine. A changed column is compared with a fresh `gen` of it (like `mp.columnDiff`), from just under the lot's
   ground up to the wonder's top; grass↔dirt, plants, leaves, fluids, snow and ice changing on their own do not count.
   Anything else → `blocked` for good.
6. **Multiplayer:** only the host plans and builds; its cells go to the guests as live block changes; every page
   discovers on its own (the sites are the same: seed + the host's world spawn).
7. **Hearts are existing glowing blocks** (beacon, sunstone, crystals, pearl light …) — no new block id.
