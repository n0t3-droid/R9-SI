# 🐾 Begleiter (companion animals) · Implementation Plan

**Goal:** three original companion species — Glimmerfuchs, Mooskätzchen, Kristallwelpe — live wild in meadows and near villages (rare, peaceful). A treat tames one (right-click), it follows, sits, sleeps by the bed at night, teleports back when lost, is saved with the world, and is visible to friends in multiplayer. Up to three per player.

**Architecture:** the pure rules live in `src/shared/pets.js` (`VR.Pets`: species, names, the follow / sit / sleep / teleport decision, the save format) and are tested in node. Companions are ordinary `Mob` entities (`src/main/entities.js`, `m.pet` record, `def.companion`) so they reuse the mob physics, rays, drawing and the voxel rig. `src/main/companions.js` (`VR.Companions`) is the game glue: taming, budgeted pathing, teleport spots, poses, the 🐾 HUD chip, the rename box, Pfeifen, save and multiplayer presence.

**Tech stack:** plain ES2019 IIFE style, node tests (`test/load_shared.js`), Playwright for `test/pets.mjs`.

## Global constraints

- No new block ids. Items are saved by name, so the spawn eggs (`egg_glimmer_fox` …) and no other new items.
- Pets never take damage, never despawn, never flee; wild ones are peaceful.
- Low end first: pets think at 1–5 Hz (by distance), at most two path searches per frame, no per-frame allocation in the pose code (the pose arrays are reused).
- Saved worlds stay valid: `meta.companions` is optional; old worlds load unchanged.
- Every player-facing text through `VR.I18N.tx` / `t` with keys in `lang_de.js` and `lang_en.js` (German first).
- Own names and shapes only.

## Tasks

1. **Rules (node first):** `test/pets_logic.js` fails until `src/shared/pets.js` exists. Covers the species table, names, teleport at > 24 blocks, stuck teleport, sit / follow toggle, night bed and wake, thinking intervals, moods, the save format (round trip, bad input dropped, cap of three).
2. **Models:** `M.glimmer_fox`, `M.moss_cat`, `M.crystal_pup` in `src/main/voxmodels.js` with parts `tail`, `ear_l`, `ear_r`, legs and head; the node test meshes them.
3. **Mob integration:** `MOBS` entries (`companion: true`, peaceful, `drops: []`), rare wild spawns in `trySpawn`, `hurtMob` refuses companions, left-click pets them, right-click tames or toggles, pose hook, far-removal skipped for pets, spawn eggs and egg textures.
4. **Glue:** `src/main/companions.js` — follow steering with budgeted paths, teleport, jump-along, sleep at the bed, save and load through `meta.companions`, presence `pl.co` for friends, the HUD chip (`#petchip`), the rename box (`#petname`), `/pfeifen`, the pause button `🐾 Pfeifen`.
5. **Language:** keys for every new text in both tables (test/lang.js).
6. **Browser:** `test/pets.mjs` (de-DE, creative flat world): spawn a wild Glimmerfuchs, tame it with its treat, walk 40 blocks (follows / teleports), right-click sits, reload the world (still yours, same name). Screenshots `test/shots/pets_1..3.png` of all three species.
7. **Regression:** node set (`REGRESS_ONLY=node bash test/regress.sh .tmp_tests/regress_node.txt`), and the browser tests touched (`smoke.mjs`, `mp.mjs`, `pets.mjs`).

## Review focus

1. A pet 30 blocks away teleports next to its player; a sitting pet never moves on its own.
2. A saved world with no `companions` key loads exactly as before.
3. A bad save record (unknown kind, NaN position, 40-character name, a fourth pet) is dropped, not thrown.
4. Right-click with the wrong item on a wild companion explains what it likes, takes nothing.
5. Pets never lose hp, never die in lava or a fall (they teleport first).
