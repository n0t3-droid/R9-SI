# 🚀 T-Shuttle — the trailer's shuttles, shot by shot · Plan and results

> Branch `t-shuttle` (merge of `shuttle` at v0.38 + the trailer wave). Method from `docs/trailer/README.md`:
> (1) open the frame and describe it, (2) put the game's camera at the same place and take the game's picture
> first (`test/trailer_shuttle.mjs`, `R9_TAG=before`), (3) write the differences ordered by how much they change
> the impression, (4) fix from the top, (5) take the picture again and look at both side by side.

**Goal:** the shuttle (flight, look, sky traffic, docking, cabin) matches the trailer frames t1_01, t1_03, t1_12,
t1_16, t1_17 and t3_03: a white hull with orange stripes and a blue canopy, twin engines with a cyan-white glow,
long luminous trails that fade into white contrails with a sparkle stream, a thruster flare on boost, dust on
landing; sky traffic in loose formations of 2–4 that docks in the Gate Temple's hangar mouth and takes off again;
the player's shuttle can land on the same apron (glowing strip); the cabin is furnished and walkable.

## Frames and what the trailer shows

### A · Canyon flight (t1_01, t1_03)

- **t1_01:** one shuttle seen from behind and above, flying left to right over a crystal canyon. White hull, orange
  band along the flank and on the wing tips, dark cockpit glass. Two engine flames at the back burn white-cyan with a
  hot core, and a long luminous trail (white-blue) runs out behind them and thins into a white contrail. Below:
  magenta/cyan crystal clusters, rail viaducts with trams, glowing lava cracks; above: a ringed gas giant, moons,
  a pink-blue nebula.
- **t1_03:** the formation at distance. A white shuttle with an orange-hot exhaust flame crosses the upper centre; a
  second craft at the right throws an orange flare straight at the camera; a white smoke trail curls across the
  sky. Stone spires, steam puffs from the vents and the ringed planet fill the frame.

### D · Gate Temple approach (t3_03; shot D in the trailer README; t1_16, t1_17 for the apron)

- **t3_03:** eye level on the plaza, the temple's slate-blue tiers rising ahead with amber windows, cyan slits and
  orange light strips; the octagonal portal glows orange-white. A shuttle (white hull, cyan square badge on the nose,
  blue canopy) comes straight down the plaza toward the camera. Both thrusters flare orange-white with streaked
  flames, the air is heated, and the fringes of the frame are motion-blurred. Purple crystals, palms and lawns line
  the flagstones.
- **t1_16 / t1_17:** the hangar side of the same temple. A boxy dark-grey shuttle (checkered roof band, orange lights
  on the nose) hovers over a glowing hex pad with two orange-white thruster flames. This is the hangar variant; the
  flight shuttle is the white one.

### E · Hangar hall (t1_12, t1_16, t1_17)

Octagonal navy hall lit cyan, holo screens with radar sweeps on both walls, button consoles with blinking lights,
two heroes in orange/blue armour with blasters at the consoles, the shuttle centred on a glowing pad.
**Not built in this wave** (see Known gaps): the trailer's hall is a separate interior; the nearest game space is
the cabin's cargo bay (`src/main/cabin.js`).

## Plan and changes (in this wave)

Ordered by impact on the impression. Each item names the code it touches.

1. **Docking on the temple apron** (`src/main/traffic.js`): a sky shuttle (or dropship) enters over the plaza on a
   Bezier curve, settles on the apron in front of the portal (`dockPoint()`: the temple's apron, one metre above the
   deck), idles 9–15 s with its wingmen beside it, then lifts off and leaves the way it came (`dockLeave()`). Engine
   dust (22 particles, `dockDust()`) blows off the deck on arrival and on lift-off. Only one dock craft at a time.
   On the apron the craft turns round to face the plaza (about 3 s, `flyDock()`), so it lifts off nose first with the
   portal behind it, as in shot D. Before this change its heading snapped 180° at lift-off. Its wingmen rise 6 m while
   the leader turns and close up beside it (their trail shrinks to nothing), so none parks behind the leader in the
   temple's hall, as the first browser picture showed them.
   Verified in `test/dock_unit.js` (ALL OK): parks within 0.25 m of the hover point, turns to face the plaza, lifts off
   with no snap (largest turn in one frame 0.000 rad), leaves, dies; wingmen wait.
2. **Formations of 2–4** (`src/main/traffic.js`): the formation table now includes the shuttle and the dropship
   (`rng.irange(2, 4)`), loose V offsets, the same Bezier paths for the whole group.
3. **Exhaust by speed** (`src/main/traffic.js`, `exhaust()`): cruise is cyan-white with a sparkle stream; a craft
   near hover (speed < 6 m/s) burns orange, like the frames' hover flames.
4. **Boost and landing in the flight model** (`src/main/flight.js`): boost kicks the FOV (0.09, relaxing to 0.05);
   the plume is cyan, orange when hovering. The landing flare (glide sink `-clamp(0.7·a + 0.5, 0.5, 10)`, pitch
   up over the last 9 m) brings the touchdown from −1.50 m/s to −0.54 m/s (`flight_unit.js`).
5. **The shuttle's colours** (`src/shared/starship.js`): shuttles use the Starforge palette (white hull, orange
   stripes, blue badge, cyan glow).
6. **Landmarks and instruments** (`src/main/cockpit.js`): the temple, the isles, the citadel and the station are
   marked on the radar and the navigation tile; the mode line (LANDED, LANDING, AUTOPILOT, BOOST, HOVER) and the
   labels are translated.
7. **Autopilot docks at the temple** (`src/main/flight.js`, `autoTargets()`/`startAuto()`): the temple apron is one
   of the autopilot's sites, with its exact spot (`A.refined = true`).
8. **Cabin** (Step 3, `src/main/cabin.js`, `src/shared/cabinkit.js`, `src/shared/shipcabin.js`,
   `src/shared/cockpitkit.js`): furnished cargo bay and cockpit behind the seats, the cockpit door, the ramp and the
   side stair; the cabin test (`test/cabin.mjs`) checks the walk.
9. **Translations**: `g.fl.*` (18 keys), `g.cock.*`, `g.ship.parked`, `g.ship.hint` in both tables with the same key set;
   `test/lang.js` ALL OK.

## Known gaps

- The hangar hall (shot E) is not built: no hall interior with holo screens and consoles, no dark-grey hangar
  shuttle variant, no hero stance at the consoles.
- The shuttle's canopy is the palette's navy glass; the frames' canopy is a brighter blue.
- The player's own docking is by autopilot (G) and the assisted V landing onto the apron, not a separate hangar
  mechanic.
- The frame list in `docs/trailer/README.md` has an off-by-one correction (trailer 1 t1_01–05 canyon, t1_12–19 the hangar
  hall). It was made in this worktree and is not committed: README edits are out of this wave's scope. The patch is in
  the session scratchpad (`trailer_readme_frame_fix.diff`) for the owner to apply.
- The sky is the trailer's world-environment work, not this wave: the trailer's crystal canyon, ringed gas giant and
  pink nebula are not in the game's sky over the canyon (shot A's background stays the game's).
- `src/main/starships.js`: the shipyard and helm strings beyond the two translated here still need T() keys.
- Trailer shots B, C, F and the portal's energy veil are not in this wave.
