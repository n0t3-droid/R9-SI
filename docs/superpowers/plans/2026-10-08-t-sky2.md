# t-sky2: the sky, perfected — space and nebula by day, the haze band, sparse clouds, shaded planets, a soft sun

Branch `t-sky2` (base `e45b233`, v042 with v0.44: t-sky merged). The owner, playing v0.44: "der Himmel ist immer noch
nicht perfektioniert"; earlier: "hellblau unten, oben dunkel — extrem exakt wie im Trailer". t-sky (merged; plan
`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-08-t-sky.md` in the t-sky worktree) brought the day sky's band means within ΔE 7–11 of the
frames (white-cyan horizon, blue, a navy top). What is still wrong is the look of the top half — the space — and the
things in the sky. The night is close already and stays as it is (only the shared nebula structure gets richer).

Method as before, measured, not felt: `test/sky_ref.js` (node) measures the frames, `test/trailer_skycurve.mjs` (browser)
the game's pictures with the same functions; the shader is tuned offline first (the real `SKY_FS` rendered with Mesa,
surfaceless EGL, through the composite's tone path; the scripts in the session scratchpad `sky2/`: `uni.js`, `render.py`,
`curve.mjs`), the Very Low cost measured offline with SwiftShader through ANGLE (`bench_ss.py`, the browser tests' own GL)
and in the browser test. Browser runs: at most four.

What the game shows by day (proof pictures, city noon / canyon noon / isles noon, and the owner's v0.44 pictures): the
right gradient in the band means (L* 74–77 at the horizon to 17–22 at the top), but

1. **the space is muted**: the top is a smooth navy-violet (L* 18–30 everywhere) with sparse, small stars and the nebula
   as a faint violet-pink smear only where its band crosses the view (canyon, isles); in the city's view (north-north-east)
   the band is out of view and the top is plain navy. Space by day is held to 0.85 × 0.9 × 0.55 × 0.55 of the night's
   veil (`u_skyMagic`, `u_skySpace.z`, `nebDay`, `u_nebula`) and only fully from dir.y 0.7.
2. **the haze**: the game's white-cyan fades slowly (L* > 60 up to ~30 % of the sky height; the frames: ~20 %), its bottom
   is a little dimmer (L* 76 vs the frames' ~81), so the blue and the navy start too high.
3. **the clouds**: big puffy cumulus (a cell of 56 blocks in five holds one, radius 22–36) in a ring at mid-height
   covering the planets and the nebula (Very Low: blocky slabs over a third of the top); the frames have small cumulus low
   at the edges (t1_05), cirrus streaks only in the golden isles frames.
4. **the planets are flat**: the city and canyon views look away from the sun, so both planets are fully lit discs (the
   giant's shade side kept at 0.3); no terminator, no ring shadow on the giant, a hard edge.
5. **the sun** is a hard HDR disc with a ×3 pow(900) core and bloom and rays round it (canyon / isles at 15:00: a white
   glare over a third of the picture); the frames' sun is a soft glow with a warm halo.

## Ordered differences (largest first) and the plan

1. **Space by day** (`shaders.js` skyColor, `env.js` VR.Sky): above the blue band (dir.y > ~0.35) the cosmic layer shows
   fully by day — `u_skyMagic` at its default 0.8 counts as full (×1.25), `u_skySpace` = [0.18, 0.40, 1.0, nebDay] — on a
   darker navy (the zenith stop refitted so the band means stay at the frames' while the gaps are as dark as theirs).
   The nebula gets structure (our own noise, nothing copied): the gas as 4 octaves of the existing value-noise fbm in a
   domain stretched along the band and warped by a low-frequency hue noise; a ridged term (1 − |2n − 1|) whose crests
   are the dark lanes and whose shoulders are the lit edges; three hue zones (pink-orange, violet, a cyan tint) and white
   cores; off the band a veil over the whole upper sky by day (the band alone misses the city's view). The stars denser
   (two layers) with a brightness spread and a few bright ones. Very Low (LITE): 2 octaves and the ridged term from the
   same noise, no more noise calls than today; High: fewer calls than today's 16 (the dust octaves go).
   `u_skySpace.w` (the nebula's brightness by day) fitted to the frames' measured chroma.
2. **The haze band** (`env.js` stops, the band term in skyColor, `test/sky_stops.js`): the white-cyan haze as measured in
   the frames (its brightness at the bottom and the height where it falls under L* 60), then the clean saturated blue.
3. **Clouds** (`clouds.js`, `renderer.js` box clouds, the cirrus in skyColor): in clear weather one cumulus cell in 12 at
   most, smaller (×0.65), and by day high ones thin out (the puffs fade above ~25° elevation in clear weather, so the near
   ones overhead do not cover the nebula and the visible ones sit low and far); the Very Low box clouds as sparse; the
   cirrus as few thin wisps at ~0.5 alpha in the blue band, the nebula seen through them.
4. **Planets** (skyColor's gasGiant / rockyPlanet): a terminator from the sun's side (the line-of-sight part of the light
   damped, so a planet opposite the sun still shows a lit gibbous with a soft terminator), the ring's shadow on the giant,
   limb darkening and a soft atmospheric limb.
5. **The sun**: a soft glow — a Gaussian core and a warm halo instead of the hard disc and the pow(900) spike.

Measures (step a): per day frame the star density (local maxima of the luma whose contrast to the ring two pixels away
is ≥ 14, per 1000 sky pixels, in each of the top three bands) and the nebula chroma (the mean CIELAB chroma C* of the
5 × 5-blurred sky in the top three bands and its variance), and the haze (the bottom's L* and the height where the
profile falls under L* 60); the targets the medians over the day frames, appended to `docs/trailer/sky_ref.json`.
Browser checks (step f): by day the top band's stars ≥ 60 % of the frames', the chroma (mean and variance) ≥ 70 %.

No block, generator, mesher or light change (gen_same, mesh_same, light_same untouched); no new player-facing text.
