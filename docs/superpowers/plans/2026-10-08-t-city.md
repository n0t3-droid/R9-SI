# Tropical Tech City (t-city) — trailer shots F and G in the game, and the terraced hills

> **For agentic workers:** the pictures are taken by `test/trailer_fg.mjs` (a showcase, not a check); the numbers by
> `test/trailer_city.js`. Test first, commit small. Worlds made before this branch (vgen 5) keep their chunks.

**Goal:** the game shows trailer shot F (Palm City Nova Haven) and shot G (the white interior) at the frames' dusk,
and the green hills behind the city are cut into terraces with white villas on the treads (shot F, t3_07–t3_09).

**Status at the end of this branch: partly met.** Done: tall palms with fronds (F1, F6); glass villas with cove strips
and glass walls (F4, G1, G2); terraced hills with a few villas on the treads (H2). Open: the stepped villas and the
curved avenue (F1, F3); the framing of F2 and F4; the floor colour, the round pad and the stairs in G1; the staircase
in G2; the terraces in F5 and H1, which do not read from the rooftop cameras; planting on the terraces.

## Method

1. Open each frame (`docs/trailer/frames/<id>.jpg`) and say what it shows.
2. Take the game's picture at the same place and light before writing the plan. `before` is the build of `a482675`
   (the build from before any change on this branch); `after` is this checkout's `dist/`. Each shot names the game
   picture first, then the frame.
3. List the differences in order of how much they show.
4. Change the generator (vgen 6 only), rebuild, take the `after` picture, and compare: `test/shots/side_<shot>.png`
   holds the before picture (left) and the after picture (right).

The camera is `p.teleport(x, 82.2, z)` with `p.flying = true; p.noclip = true`, `yaw` 0 looking along −z, dusk at
tick 13300 with the time frozen, the seed 4242, the preset High, no HUD.

## Shot by shot

Pictures (git-ignored, in `test/shots/`): `trailer_<shot>_before.png` is the build before this branch,
`trailer_<shot>_after2.png` this branch at its final code, and `side_<shot>.png` the two side by side (before left,
after right). Regenerate with `BEFORE_DIST=<snapshot>/index.html TAG=after2 bash test/wt_run.sh trailer_fg.mjs 2400`;
one shot only: add `ONLY=F5` (or another shot name).

### F1 · avenue (frames t3_05, t2_20)

**Game, before** (`trailer_F1_avenue_before.png`): a straight avenue between tall blue-white glass towers with orange
window bands; single-block palms with small crowns; a dark green lawn with a cyan dashed line down the middle; green
cube hedges; a pink-violet dusk sky.

**Game, after** (`side_F1_avenue.png`, right): the same avenue. The palms have full crowns with long fronds; the
towers, the road and the hedges are unchanged.

**The frame:** a curved avenue with cyan light lines in the paving, planted curbs, palms in a row, stepped villas in
grey stone with turquoise glass bands in orange frames and white balcony rails, a starry dusk, a pale blue planet.

**Differences, in order of how much they show:**
1. Architecture: stepped villas with set-back roof gardens, against flat towers. Open.
2. Road: a curved avenue with cyan light lines along the edges, against a straight lane. Open.
3. Palms: full crowns with long fronds, against single-block palms. Done for vgen 6 (`VG.palmTall`: four cardinal
   frond chains and four diagonal fronds on a trunk of 7–12 blocks).
4. Sky: stars and a pale planet. Faint at this camera height. Open.

**Plan and status:** palms done. The architecture (a stepped villa variant for the Palm City lots) and the curved
avenue are not started on this branch; both need new generator work.
