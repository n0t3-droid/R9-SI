# 💡 Erfinderwerkstatt — put two cards together · Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** 💡 a child puts two picture cards together and sees what it invented — big picture, name, one friendly line, confetti, a jingle — and the invention becomes a new card. 167 inventions from six start cards (Holz, Stein, Feuer, Wasser, Erde, Luft) up to the Modern Living pieces and a Sternenkern, in German first and English, without reading a recipe.

**Architecture:** `src/main/inventions.js` is the data and the rules (no DOM): the six starter cards, the inventions (`key`, existing item/block/VX id, unordered pair, category, alternative pairs), the profile's progress in `localStorage r9si.inventions`, `combine`, `tip`, `reward`, `grant`, `recipeFor`. `src/main/invent.js` + `src/ui/invent.css` are the screen (`#invent`, a normal `.screen` of `VR.UI`): a two-slot workbench, the card tray, the reveal, the 📖 book, 💡 Tipp, ⌫ reset. It hooks into the game by wrapping (`UI.prototype.act` / `pause`, `ST.renderMeins`, one `keydown` listener for I and Esc), so `start.js`, `game.js` and `ui.js` stay untouched. Texts are `erf.*` keys and one `erf.i.<key>` = `[name, line]` per card in `lang_de.js` / `lang_en.js`. `codex.js` gets a 💡 category whose entries read the profile's progress.

**Tech Stack:** plain ES2019 in the project's IIFE style, node tests (`test/load_shared.js`, `test/dom_stub.js`), Playwright for the browser test.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-08-new-era-wave2-design.md` §W-B.

## Where the plan refines the spec (and why)

1. **A two-slot workbench** (`[A] + [B] = [?]`) next to "drag one card onto another". The spec's drag-only picture cannot do Luft + Luft (a card cannot be dropped on itself), a phone user does not want to drag, and 💡 Tipp has to show its pair somewhere. Tap two cards, drag a card onto a slot, or drag a card onto another card — all end in the same `combine(a, b)`.
2. **Every card is an invention with an existing id**, also the ideas (Licht = Sonnenstein, Strom = Volt Dust, Stoff = carpet, Pflanze = sapling, Wolke = white wool, Zeit-as-Uhr = clock). A card without a picture cannot be a picture card, and the hard rule is no new ids. Texts are therefore keyed by the card (`erf.i.<key>`), not by the item id: the card says "Licht", the game's item says "Sunstone".
3. **Every pair of the six start cards gives something** (21 pairs: 15 different cards + 6 doubles). The first minute never ends in "nothing happens"; the first-run hint pulses Feuer and Stein.
4. **Alternative pairs** (33) for the guesses a child makes anyway (Stock + Feuer = Fackel, Sand + Lagerfeuer = Glas): the result stays one invention, the recipe is shown as the main pair in the book.
5. **Hooks by wrapping, not by editing.** Eight other agents work on `start.js`, `game.js`, `ui.js`, the lang tables. The pause menu button, the 🎒 Meins button and card, key I and Esc are added at run time from `invent.js`. Sounds use the public `audio.tone()` (no `audio.js` edit). The new lang block sits in the middle of the tables (before „Werft“), not at their end, so appended keys from other agents do not collide.
6. **Codex entries read the profile, not the world.** The codex is saved per world, the inventions per profile ("survives worlds"); the `i:<key>` entries have a live `found`.
7. **💡 Tipp fills the two slots and lights the `=` pad**; the child still does the last tap, so the discovery is its own.
8. **Survival shows the recipe where the item has one** (`VR.Crafting.recipes`, tags expanded to a picture), and says that the codex remembers; Creative / Zen put one stack into the inventory (hotbar first) with the „💡 Neu im Inventar“ toast; a guest in a friend's world gets the codex only.

## Global Constraints

- No new block or item ids; every invention is an existing item, block or VM model id (checked by `test/inventions.js` against the registry).
- No server, no account: nothing leaves the browser. All progress in `r9si.inventions`, every `localStorage` access in try/catch; the page works with storage refused.
- Low-end PCs first: the screen opens instantly (no icon is painted before it shows), icons are painted lazily (≈ 6 ms per frame, `game.icons` cache), confetti is 36 DOM particles with CSS transitions (none with `prefers-reduced-motion`).
- Every player-facing text through `VR.I18N` with keys in both tables; `test/lang.js` stays ALL OK. German first: short, friendly, pictures before words; names ≤ 22, lines ≤ 70 characters.
- 44 px targets, Tab / Enter / Space / arrows / Esc work, `:focus-visible` ring from `start.css`, works at 360 px wide.
- No edit of README.md / ROADMAP.md.

## Review Focus

1. Storage refused / full / corrupt JSON: the screen still works for the session, nothing throws.
2. Two quick taps while the merge animation runs, a language switch while the reveal is open, Esc in every layer (reveal → book detail → book → screen → where it came from).
3. A pair of two unfound cards cannot happen (only known cards are on the tray) but `combine` must refuse it anyway.
4. Survival and guests never get an item; Creative / Zen with a full inventory get a message, not a lost invention.
5. Key I never fires while typing (chat, rename field), with a menu open, in photo mode, dead, or with the Studio overlay up.
