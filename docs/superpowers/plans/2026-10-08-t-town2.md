## Per shot: the reference, the game (run 4 `_final` and the node preview of the same build), the differences

**P1 / P2 (innenstadt_full.jpg, v3_01–06)** — reference: three-storey shop houses of warm white stone whose fronts are
all relief: pilasters between the bays, deep window reveals under stepped round arches with keystones, wood-brown window
frames with warm light behind, a projecting cornice at every storey, a balcony with a balustrade, long flower boxes
overflowing with flowers and vines hanging down the cornices, the ground floor a wood-framed storefront with goods
behind the glass, a striped awning over it and a dark board sign with gold letters above the awning.
Game: the same street and fountain, but every façade is one flat plane: white wall, a gold line at the storey, four
narrow arched slits per storey (1 wide), window boxes, two awnings and a small pictogram sign. Differences, by weight:
1. no relief at all (no pilaster, no cornice, no arch, no reveal) — the façades read as cardboard;
2. the windows are slits, cold blue, no wood frames, no warmth behind them;
3. the signs have no words (pictograms), so the shops do not read as shops;
4. too few flowers and vines; the gable roofs sit on a façade without a crowning cornice;
5. the ground floor's storefront is right, but its door is off-centre and nothing frames it.

**P3 (hall_full.jpg, left)** — reference: big timber market stalls (posts, braces, a cream/brown striped canopy with a
scalloped edge), goods piled on the counters (bread, baskets, crates, flowers), lanterns hanging from the frames, barrels
beside, a crowd. Game: a long empty marble aisle, a stall at every pier line (10 apart) far away, plain loggia fronts.
Differences: 1. density — one stall per 10 blocks on one side, nothing between; 2. no lanterns at the stalls; 3. the
aisle vault is bare (the reference has wood-brown beams/ribs on its arches); 4. no benches or plants between; 5. the
loggia fronts have no order (no columns between the units).

**P4 / P8 (hall_full.jpg centre)** — reference: a wide white staircase in flights with landings, solid parapets with
balusters and a thick rail, square newels with lanterns at the foot, a balustraded gallery round the apse, round-arched
doorways under the gallery front between pilasters. Game: one straight 45° ramp of eight steps, eleven wide, a
balustrade on each stringer, two lamps at its foot, the gallery front with a balustrade and six doors in its face.
Differences: 1. no landings, no rhythm — a ramp; 2. no newels/columns framing it; 3. the gallery front is a plain wall
(no pilasters, the doors are holes); 4. the apse wall above the gallery is bare between the lancets (no niches, no lamps
in it); 5. the gallery's side edges are open.

**P5 (outside)** — game: a long white wall with buttresses at every pier line, tall windows with stained heads, the gate
with a stepped pediment, the clerestory, the glass vault. Differences: 1. no portal in the entrance end (the hall's
front has no door at all); 2. no cornice band under the glass roof, no plinth course: the wall ends flat; 3. the windows
have no tracery; 4. the ridge is empty (no lantern turrets); 5. the end walls have no round window.

**Light** — reference: high-key, warm white and gold. Game: the sky light under the glass is full (15), so the interior
is lit like the outside by the sky's ambient, which env.js makes cool blue-violet by day (`ambSky` [0.36, 0.44, 0.66]);
the sun's colour turns golden only when it is low (`warm` grows below sy 0.6: at 14:00 it is 0.42, at 16:00 0.8). Where
the façades face away from the sun they take the ambient alone — the cold. The fix within this front: warm block light
(the lanterns' colour, `blockTint` [1.0, 0.84, 0.64]) where people are — at every stall, under the arcades, round the
fountain — and the 16:00 picture (P1_gold) to show the golden hour. env.js is not touched.

## The plan (test-first: `test/hallcity_ornament.js` red, then the generator)

1. **The kit** (`src/shared/voxtown.js`, late, lazy, colours with a texture already):
   `vm_town_arch3` — a three-wide round-arched window of the references: wood-brown frame, mullion and transom, warm
   glass, a white stone archivolt with a keystone (3 × 3 cells); the word signs `vm_town_word_<key>` — a dark green
   board four wide with a gold frame and the shop's word in gold letters of our own 3 × 5 pixel font (umlauts too); the
   words are bilingual (`town.sign.<key>` in lang_de.js / lang_en.js, German first): the main thread reads them through
   VR.I18N, the worker gets the table with the world's `init` message (a word model is built again when the language of
   the table changes; without a table the worker follows the browser's language).
2. **The façades** (every shop house): pilasters (quartz pillars on a plinth block) at the bay edges u = 0, 4, 7, 11, one
   block proud; a cornice of upside-down stairs at every storey line and a crowning cornice; the upper floors' outer bays
   with the arched window (the archivolt's keystone repeated in a stepped hood of stairs one block out), the middle bay a
   pair of the narrow stained windows under a hood, a balcony with a balustrade on the third storey; the ground floor a
   wood storefront — two shop windows (riser, glass, sill) under the two striped awnings, the door in the middle under
   the word sign, lanterns on the corner pilasters; flower boxes and hanging vines on at least a third of the houses;
   the shop trades (Bäckerei, Konditorei, Teestube, Käse, Blumen, Schneider, Apotheke, Laternen, Buchladen, Werkstatt,
   Karten, Café) dress the shop behind the glass. The work kinds (bakery/market/library/cafe) stay: the keepers' roles
   and the folk budget do not change. Loggia houses: columns at the unit edges carrying the galleries, a plinth course,
   the workshop opening arched with a keystone.
3. **The grand staircase and the gallery**: thirteen wide, four flights of two steps with three landings (at 2, 4 and 6)
   arriving on the gallery (8) through an opening in its front; parapets with balusters every two blocks and a slab rail,
   newel columns with lanterns at the foot and at every landing, columns flanking it up to the gallery; the gallery's
   front with pilasters and arched niches (doors, lanterns), its open edges closed with balustrades; arched niches in the
   apse wall between the lancets, each with a lantern.
4. **The market aisle**: a stall every 7 blocks along both aisles (counter, four posts, a striped awning roof, goods on
   the counter, a barrel pair beside, a lantern hanging from a bracket over the walk), benches and potted plants in the
   gaps, wooden ribs under the aisle vault and a tie beam every 12 blocks, columns at the loggias' unit edges.
5. **The exterior**: a portal in the entrance end (a seven-wide pointed arch in a two-step surround, the giant window
   above it), tracery in the long walls' windows (a stone cross), a plinth course and a cornice band under the aisle
   roof's glass and at the clerestory's top, oculi in the aisle end walls, lantern turrets on the ridge every 24 blocks.
6. **Light**: lanterns at every stall, on the piers' aisle side, four on the fountain's rim; P1_gold at 16:00.
7. **People**: untouched (another front); the folk budget stays.
8. Browser: `test/trailer_town.mjs` gains P7 (a façade close-up at a shop) and P8 (the staircase from its foot) and
   P1_gold; BEFORE_DIST = the v042 build with t-town (`.tmp_tests/base_dist`) for the same-run Very Low comparison.

- **Light**: four lanterns on the fountain's rim; warm block light at every stall (the light engine: 11/8/4 in front of

## Round 2b — the continuation (9 Oct evening; the predecessor stopped by the harness at 17:34)

The brief's open items in order: the grand staircase and gallery (P4/P8), the market aisle (P3), the exterior (P5), the
warm lantern light. Round 2 had built a first version of each; the node preview of that build (townview, the game's own
generator, light, mesher and textures; no shadows) and run 1's browser pictures were the "game" column below.

**P4 / P8 (hall_full.jpg centre).** Reference: the stair climbs into a bright end — a huge luminous window over the
gallery; balustraded gallery with arched wooden doors under it, lanterns on posts along the balustrades, greenery hanging
from the gallery, big lanterns at the foot. Game (round 2): the flights and landings exist and read as steps in the browser
(shadows), but the end wall above the gallery is a grey brick wall with three narrow lancets (the eye goes nowhere), the
gallery's doors are niches three high, nothing marks the foot, no light or green on the way up. Differences by weight:
1. no focal window at the end; 2. the gallery front's doorways small, no cornice, no posts or lanterns on its rail;
3. the foot unmarked (no newel lamps); 4. no greenery. Plan: the middle lancet becomes a great window nine wide (two
mullions, a transom, a stained head of rays round a gold eye); newel pedestals with lanterns at the foot; doorways four
high with wooden doors, a hanging lantern and a keystone, a cornice under the balustrade, posts with lanterns over the
pilasters; vines from the cornice and potted plants on the landings.

**P3 (hall_full.jpg left).** Reference: timber stalls taller than a man under striped canopies, lanterns hanging inside
them, goods piled up. Game (round 2): a stall every 7 blocks, but low (the canopy at 2.5 blocks, under eye height), the
loggias' workshop arches black holes, the tie beams' lanterns hung at 19–20 blocks (out of the picture). Plan: a new
tall stall (`vm_town_stall_tall_*`, 3 × 4 × 2: posts and braces, a pitched striped canopy with a scalloped valance, a
lantern hanging over the counter — it emits); every workshop lit by a hanging lantern and given a striped awning and more
goods; the lanterns over the walk hang low on chains (F + 10, chains to the tie beam at F + 20).

**P5 (outside).** Game (round 2): the long wall's windows are plain openings (no frame), the side gate's portal is a flat
stepped pediment, the turrets are small (3 × 3) and the ridge has no crown. Plan: every tall window two blocks taller and
framed outside (pilaster jambs, an arched hood of stairs with a gold keystone, a sill); a portico two proud at each side
gate (columns on plinths, a gold entablature, a gabled pediment with a raking cornice and a finial, lanterns); turrets five
wide (glass base and cap so the sky light still falls through, a dome, a finial with a lantern); a glazed octagonal cupola
over the crossing (glass all through: the plaza keeps its sky light).

**Light.** env.js stays untouched; the warm comes from lanterns where the views look: newels at the stair's foot, the
gallery's doors and rail, the workshops, the stalls' own lanterns, the chains over the walk (test 9: the light engine's
rule on the plan's own blocks — warm block light ≥ 9 red at the stair's first steps, a landing, the gallery's doors, the
workshops).

Tests: `test/hallcity_ornament.js` sections 8 and 9 (red first: FAILED 30, then ALL OK); `icon_same.json` gets the
15 town models of rounds 2 and 2b appended (no older entry changes).
