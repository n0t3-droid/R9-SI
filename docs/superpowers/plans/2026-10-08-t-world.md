**Goal:** the Heartland's three trailer shots, Canyon flyover (A), Spire Citadel (B) and Sky Isles (C), look in the game the
way the owner's trailer frames look, with their ships flying as in the trailer. Method: `docs/trailer/README.md` (open the
frame, take the game's picture at the same place, list the differences, fix from the top, take it again).

**Spec:** `TRAILER.md` §1 (shot list), §2 (sky, light, palette), §3.5–3.7 (canyon, isles, citadel), §5 (ships).

## Shot A · Canyon flyover

**The frames** (`t1_01`, `t1_03`, `t3_01`, `t3_02`): an aerial push over ochre and umber mesas with green caps. Magenta and cyan
crystal clusters stand in the foreground. Lava seams glow orange between the mesas, and a cyan aether river runs in the valley.
Rail viaducts loop round the mesas, and a white-and-orange shuttle with a blue-white engine trail crosses the frame, with
fighters and contrails behind it. The ringed gas giant sits top right in a violet nebula, under a sunset sky.

**The game (after1):** A1 (the camera at 1.35 s) looks almost level, about 10° down, over a flat green plain that runs to the
horizon. There are no crystals, no ships and no planet in the frame. A2 (4.95 s) is the better picture: the lava pools and seams
light the terracotta walls and the aether glow reads, but one magenta crystal stands alone and no ship or planet is in frame.

**Ordered differences (most visible first):**
1. **No ships in any of the six pictures.** The shot scripts a shuttle and two interceptors (`director.js` TRAILER, shot A).
   The after2 run showed why: the ships had flown to their end during the settle, and their designs were freed. Fixed in
   `8b73863` (the designs stay cached while the shot runs) and `5f1f7bf` (the traffic stands still through the picture).
2. **Framing.** A1 looks at the horizon. The frame looks down at the mesas from above; the look-at point should move closer
   and lower so the mesas and crystals fill the lower half. (The second pass moved the camera; `after3` decides.)
3. **Sky.** No ringed gas giant in A1 or A2 in after1. The second pass pins the planets per shot (see below); the pale pink
   haze of the day sky is not tuned.
4. **Crystal scale.** The giant gems (new, 8–20 blocks tall) are generated on the mesa tops but not framed in A1.
5. **Smoke.** The vents (new) should rise as grey columns; none is visible in A2. The `air` diagnostic logs the director's ships;
   the vent counts are not in it yet.

**Plan:** (1) the ships in frame (done in the code; `after3` shows them); (2) the look-at toward the mesas if A1 still looks at the
horizon; (3) the day sky's haze and nebula (§2.1); (4) confirm the smoke columns in a picture; (5) the heat shimmer as
rising embers over the seams (already in `vents.js`), checked against `t3_02`.

## Shot B · Spire Citadel

**The frames** (`t1_05`, `t1_03`): a tall tan, fluted spire cluster fills the centre with cyan window slits and orange trim rings.
A blue sparkle beam rises from the spire in the night shots (not in these two). Foreground: a stone-arch viaduct on round piers, and a rocky mesa. Four or five fighters fly in loose
formation with white contrails and blue sparkle streams. A blue-green planet and a lilac gas giant stand in a deep violet sky with
stars.

**The game (after1):** B1 (1.2 s) shows the spire cluster with orange bands and cyan slits, close and in the centre, with pale
pink haze around it and no ship or planet in frame. B2 (4.4 s) pulls back: the cluster is small at centre-left, a rail viaduct
runs across the lower right and the haze hides the far spires.

**Ordered differences:**
1. **No ships** (as shot A). The gunships start left of the camera in the second pass; `after3` shows them.
2. **Sky and haze.** The frame's sky is deep violet with stars, the nebula veil and two planets. The game's is pale pink haze that
   flattens the spires. The second pass pins the lilac giant and the blue planet top left; the haze is not yet reduced.
3. **Scale.** In the frame the spire cluster and the arched viaduct fill the picture. In B2 the cluster is small and the arches are
   out of frame.
4. **Foot.** The frame shows giant crystals and lava streams at the foot. The generator has three giant crystals (azure 25,
   violet 16, verdant 12) and three lava streams down the flanks with steam vents; neither shows yet in the after1 pictures.
5. **Clouds.** The frame's clouds are white puffs drifting through the spires. The game's clouds are flat blocks.

**Plan:** (1) ships as shot A; (2) the day sky (planets, nebula veil, less haze); (3) move B's pull-back so the cluster and one
viaduct arch are in the lower half; (4) confirm the blinking tip beacons, the streams and the blue beam in `after3`; (5) clouds in the
spire band (§2.1).

## Shot C · Sky Isles

**The frames** (`t1_07`, `t1_09`): chunky floating islands with grass domes, layered earth and rock sides, round oaks and crystals on
the tops. Roads with yellow dashes loop over them on trestles, with a stone viaduct between two islands. Waterfalls and lava falls pour
off the rims, a hoodoo rock with a cave arch stands at left, and a small stone temple sits on one island. A shuttle with blue trails
flies in front, and the sky is sunset with clouds and planets.

**The game (after1):** C1 (1.2 s) is a hazy brown plain with one island carrying a tower and a few flat discs. The islands are small
because the camera starts about 250 blocks from the archipelago. C2 (5 s) is closer: a bridge and forest, orange lava glow on the
cliffs and a pale citadel far off. No shuttle is in either picture.

**Ordered differences:**
1. **No shuttle** (as shot A). The shuttle's route ends at (560, 165, -150); `after3` shows it in flight.
2. **Distance.** The islands fill less than a third of the frame in C1, where the frame has them filling the picture. The second
   pass moves the look-at to the centre isle (330, 150, 140).
3. **Islands.** The islands are flatter than the frame's (grass domes, layered sides).
4. **Hanging crystals and the viaduct** are generated (new) but not framed in after1.
5. **Sky and fog.** The frame's sunset is saturated; the game's fog reads grey-brown.

**Plan:** (1) ships as shot A; (2) the C1 camera closer to the islands, still behind the shuttle's path; (3) the isles' fog band
under the islands (§2.1); (4) the sunset grade.
