## 3 · The differences, ordered by how much they change the impression

1. Furniture is voxel carving (stair-stepped curves, block cushions); the owner wants real furniture → smooth meshes.
2. Palm crowns: an umbrella of thin, sparse feathers on one profile; the trailer's are domes of wide, dense fronds in tiers.
3. Palm colours: fronds dark green, trunks near black; the trailer's are bright green on tan.
4. Trunks straight and plain; the trailer's curve a little and show bark rings.
5. Crown centre bare (a small bulb); the trailer's is full (frond bases, young spear leaves).
6. People in front of the camera (a festival crowd) — a test problem, not the game's.

## 4 · The plan (and what was done)

- **Palm** (props.js): fronds on the golden angle in tiers (young up to 54°, old level and longer), sagging on a parabola;
  eight segments, a V-fold of three vertices across, 2.1 blocks wide in the middle; a denser leaflet cutout with ragged
  ends; two spear leaves; a fuller crownshaft; a 10-sided trunk that curves with the seeded lean over its top nine blocks,
  a collar, bark rings (uv.y banded in the shader); lighter greens, tan bark; the fronds let the light through.
  A software preview (scratchpad raster of the mesh, not a test) checked the shape before the browser run.
- **Furniture** (props.js, mesher.js, renderer.js, shaders.js): a model builder (rounded boxes with domed faces and piped
  seams, tapered legs, tubes, lathes, cutout leaves, a far mesh of boxes, a soft contact shadow) and 33 models from the
  voxel pieces' own numbers: three-seater, chaise, armchair, two dining tables, two dining chairs, double bed, tripod lamp,
  arc lamp, pendant, the kitchen units (lacquer or oak: cabinet, sink, hob, oven, tall unit, wall cabinet), island,
  bookcase, boxwood planter, monstera, olive tree, TV lowboard, wall TV, two coffee tables, nightstand. 55 furniture blocks
  map to them (VR.PROP_FURN, kind 16 + index; colours ride the instance). The mesher anchors part 0 with its facing and
  light when job.smoothProps (now a mask: 1 plants, 2 furniture) asks for the furniture; no block definition changes.
  The pass: near smooth meshes within 0.6 × vxLod (14–28 blocks), far boxes to 2 × vxLod, frustum-culled, one instanced
  draw per model and distance, then glass and contact shadows blended; the shadow pass draws the same lists. The shader
  has the terrain's light and F2's response (metal mirrors its surroundings, fresnel on smooth surfaces, matte fabric with
  sheen, wood grain, weave, marble veins), shades that glow through, glowing bulbs and the TV picture.
- **Tests** (node): props_palm.js (the tiers, the arching, the fan round the crown), props_furniture.js (every model:
  counts, no NaN, unit normals, outward winding, inside its footprint, materials and tint slots, far mesh; every listed
  block has a model of its voxel piece's size; the anchor's centre and turn match VM.footprint for all facings),
  props_list.js (the anchors by the mask), props_chunks.js; mesh_same / id_snapshot / lang stay green.
- **Browser** (test/trailer_props.mjs, three runs in all): the town's three places, the villa's two room views and the
  showroom's two, before / after in one world; the frame cost with the props off and on; the Very Low frame cost of
  this build against the build of ea240cf (OLD_DIST).
