# t-life — a living world without combat (9 Oct 2026)

The owner, playing the Hangar Hall: *„ich finde es doof, dass die Shuttles ingame verschwinden; ich mag es, dass es
vibrant und entertaining ist, immer etwas los ist, da geht was vor sich — das soll so sein. Denk dir noch mehr coole
Sachen aus."* And: *„wie man die Welt, die langweilig ist, mehr in eine schöne Umgebung macht — OHNE Combat."*

Branch `t-life` on v042 (vgen 9: the Weißstadt hall city, Nova Haven, real furniture, the trailer sky). Everything here is
a runtime actor (no block, no save, no world generation change): `gen_same`, `mesh_same`, `light_same` and
`id_snapshot` stay as they are.

## 2. Differences, ordered by how much they change the impression

1. Craft vanish in flashes (sky, apron, hall loop). → **they stay**: land, park for minutes, crews get out, they lift off
   and fly away to the horizon where they fade in the haze.
2. The apron and the hall pad are empty most of the time. → **a schedule**: the apron is occupied ≥ 85 % of every 10
   minutes, never empty for more than 20 s; the hall parks 45–90 s while a second craft holds over the canyon.
3. Combat by default. → **Friedlich** is the default of every new world; no raids, defenders, hostile mobs or bolts there.
4. Nothing happens near the player. → **the Regisseur des Lebens**: every 20–40 s one visible act within 60 blocks, chosen
   for the place and the hour, never the same twice in a row.
5. The hours look alike. → dawn convoy and fog, morning market, noon bells, afternoon plaza, dusk lantern lighter and
   chimneys, night meteors and a lit landing; the festival stays.

## 3. Design

### 3.1 Ships that stay — `src/main/padlife.js` (new) + traffic.js + hangarflight.js

**The apron schedule** (pure, `VR.PadLife.sched`): visits `n = 0, 1, …` on a pad keyed by the pad's seed; stay
`S_n = 60 + 120·h(seed, n)` s; touchdown `T_{n+1} = T_n + S_n + GAP`, `GAP = 10 s` (lift 3 s, the next craft slides over
from its holding point 3 s, settles 4 s). Worst case 60 / 70 = 85.7 % occupied, the gap never above 20 s. The clock is
the world's (`(day · 24000 + ticks) / 20`, then real seconds — like the trams), so every guest sees the same visit. A
visit's legs: approach from the horizon (45 s, from 700 blocks out, fading in) → holding point beside the pad (it waits
there while the previous craft is still parked) → slide over → settle → **parked** (engines cooling: the plume dies down
over 15 s, thin vapour, ticking metal; the ramp opens, a crew of 2–3 in pilot clothes walks down, talks, one checks the
gear, one the engine; a service drone circles; they walk back up, the ramp closes) → lift → turn → climb away to the
horizon (fading out past 450 blocks, removed past 700). No warp flash anywhere within 300 blocks.

Pads: the Gate Temple's apron (always), and every pad with a parked world ship (outposts, tech villages): there the
parked ship gets the ground crew and the drone (it never leaves: it is the player's), and visitors use the pad only when
it is free.

**The hall loop** (hangarflight.js): two craft (the hangar's own shuttle and a second one) share one schedule: stay on the
pad 45–90 s with the crew scene, then lift, turn, out through the mouth; the other one holds over the canyon (a circle in
the loop's teardrop) and comes in after the first has cleared the mouth. Range with hysteresis (on within 140 blocks, off
past 320) and the draw fades with distance, so nothing pops out.

**The sky** (traffic.js): flights run from horizon to horizon (A and B ~700 blocks out, passing 80–250 blocks from the
focus); they fade in and out with the distance (the hull drawn in the translucent pass with `u_alpha`); 1 / 2 / 3 flights
kept in the air (Very Low / Low / else) so 1–3 craft are always in the sky over a plaza; no flash within 300 blocks.

### 3.2 No combat
New worlds default to *Friedlich* (the Profi form, the old form, a guest's fallback). In a peaceful world: no raid (as
before), no defenders (`spawnDefenders` refuses), no dogfight bolts, the hostile mobs left from before are removed, and
`Entities.fireBolt` makes the player's own shots harmless light (no damage). The raid code stays behind the difficulty.

### 3.3 The Regisseur des Lebens — `src/main/lifedirector.js` (new)
* Slots: event `k` at `t_k = 30·k + j(seed, k)`, `j ∈ [−5, 5]` s → gaps of 20–40 s.
* Choice (pure): `LD.pick(seed, k, place, tier, ctxAt)` — the acts allowed at the place, the hour of `t_k`, the weather and
  the tier; even slots take their hashed pick, odd slots avoid the picks of both neighbours → never the same act twice in
  a row (when only one act fits, the odd slot stays quiet).
* Places (from the player's position): `hangar` (temple), `plaza`, `street`, `hall` (inside a Weißstadt), `hills`,
  `isles`, `water`, `wild`.
* Tiers: 0 Very Low (birds, lanterns, chimneys), 1 Low, 2 Balanced, 3 High; switch `lifeDir` (⚙ Bild · Dinge).
* Acts (runtime actors, reusing particles, airlife's flock code, traffic, folk rig, companions' models, entities, the
  festival's rockets, the audio's tones and Klang's pentatonic plan): birds across the sun, lantern lighter, chimney
  smoke, ship flyover with contrail, shuttle convoy at sunrise, cat on a wall, pigeons that burst up, kids chasing a
  ball, street musician (a short pentatonic tune), hover-cart along the avenue, dog drinking at the fountain, meteor
  (shower), hot-air balloon, sky ray over the isles, fog bank at dawn, fireworks on festival evenings, fisherman's catch.

### 3.4 The hours
Dawn (22500–1500): convoy, fog. Morning (1500–5000): the market's awnings roll out and the goods appear (runtime props at
the stalls), carts. Noon: bells (once, near a town). Afternoon: the plaza fills (kids, musician, pigeons, dog). Dusk
(11500–13800): lantern lighter, chimneys, music. Night: fireflies (airlife), meteors, the apron's visitor lands with its
landing lights. The festival every third evening stays (festival.js unchanged).

* `test/life_director.js` — slots 20–40 s apart, never the same act twice in a row, the choice a pure function, every act's
  trigger rule (place, hour, weather) and cost tier; the acts per hour (§3.4); Very Low runs only birds, lanterns, chimneys.
* `test/life.mjs` — the four pictures before/after, the counts, the Very Low and High costs (≤ 4 browser runs).
* `test/lang.js`, `id_snapshot.js`, `gen_same.js`, the node regression.

## 5. Costs and switches
Very Low: sky 1 flight (was up to 2), the apron's visitor without crew/drone/vapour, the hall loop off (`anim` off, as
before), the director with birds / lanterns / chimneys only. Low: + crew of 2, the drone, the tier-1 acts. Balanced+:
crew of 3, vapour and ticking, all acts but the fog bank's big particles on High only. Switches: `padLife` and `lifeDir`.
