# t-folk · Echte Menschen statt Klötzchen-Leute — smooth villagers with real proportions

The owner, looking at the villagers of the hall city: „natürlich keine Lego-Villagers, wir wollen schon Realismus".
Branch `t-folk` on v042 with t-form merged (src/shared/smoothvox.js: surface nets for any voxel grid).

## 1. What the references show, what the game shows

**References** (docs/refs/weissstadt/innenstadt_full.jpg, hall_full.jpg — looked at, nothing copied, the look is ours):
people with natural proportions (heads small against the body, long legs), soft rounded forms with no stair-steps,
robes to the ankle and knee-long coats with a gold hem, sleeves with cuffs, collars, hair with volume (curls, long
waves, buns), shopping bags and baskets in hand, children noticeably smaller with bigger heads relative to their body.

**The game** (test/shots/trailer_town_P1_final.png, the plaza at P1, High): chunky voxel figures — every surface
visibly stepped in 1/32-block voxels, a head a quarter of the figure, broad box torsos, arms like planks, painted
big eyes. Measured on the rig (folkmodel.js, model units 1/32 block, node, look 4242 'short' hair, no hat):

| figure | height | head (chin–crown) | heads tall | head width | shoulders | shoulders / head | hip / height |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| adult | 59 | 15 | **3.9** | 17 | 25 | 1.5 | 0.41 |
| child | 44 (× 0.82 drawn) | 16 | **2.75** | 17 | 23 | 1.35 | 0.30 |
| elder | 57 | 15 | 3.8 | 17 | 25 | 1.5 | 0.39 |

**The differences, by how much they change the impression**
1. Toy proportions: a head of 1/4 of the height (real: 1/7.5), short legs (0.41, real ≈ 0.5), narrow shoulders against
   the head (1.5 head widths). This is what reads as „Lego" even before the surface. → re-lay the rig (step 2).
2. Stair-stepped surfaces on every part (head, limbs, clothes). → surface nets per rig part (step 1).
3. Clothes without form: a box torso with painted lines; robes as stepped skirts; no collar, sleeves without cuffs that
   read as cloth; hair as a helmet of voxels. → collars, cuffs, hems with folds, six outfit families (step 3).
4. Stiff gait: legs swing, nothing else moves; the feet slide when the speed changes. → weight shift, counter-rotating
   shoulders, arm swing, head look, breathing idle, a step length matched to the speed (step 4).

## 2. Plan

Everything new behind one switch `smoothFolk` (Echte Menschen / Real people): 0 on Very Low and Low (they keep the
voxel people, half-res LOD as now), 1 from Balanced up. The proportions (step 2) are the model itself and change for
every preset — the voxel people of Very Low get the same real proportions (a smaller head is fewer voxels: never slower).
The trailer heroes (role 'hero': the blue and orange suits, the player's hero) keep their chunky trailer build and their
voxels — the trailer's art (t1_21/t1_22) — only the villagers change.

1. **Proportions (folkmodel.js, node test first: test/folk_real.js §1).** A new layout for villagers: adult 58 units
   (1.8 blocks) = 7.25 heads of 8 (skull 7 + hair), shoulders 15 wide against a head of 5 (7 with hair) ≈ 2.1 head
   widths, hip pivot at 29 (legs ≈ 0.5 of the height), knee 15, shoulder pivot 47, elbow 36, wrist at the crotch and
   fingertips at mid-thigh (≈ 0.38 H). Child (drawn × 0.82): 5 heads. Face at 1/32 a block: eyes 1 voxel (3 cm, as
   real), brows, a protruding nose, lips, ears; hats and glasses re-made at the head's scale. Test: measured ratios.
2. **Smooth people (src/shared/folksmooth.js shared + src/main/folksmooth.js game, worker job 'folkmesh').** Every rig
   part's grid through SV.nets at its own resolution (32 a block, step 1, creases at colour/material changes: cloth
   against skin, hair against skin, the belt), flattened, converted to the voxel models' 20-byte vertex (position,
   normal, colour × occlusion) + Uint16 indices. The worker keeps an LRU (≤ 24 MB) keyed by a hash of the part's grid;
   the main thread asks once per key and keeps one GPU mesh per key (shared by every villager whose part is the same).
   A villager shows its voxel self until all its smooth parts are there. Draw: the same rig (pivots, the pose), the
   parts gathered per mesh and drawn instanced (one draw per distinct part mesh) by a variant of the vox program made
   from the existing VOX_VS/FS sources with defines (per-instance matrix and light) — no edit of shaders.js; fallback
   one draw per part. Node tests (test/folk_real.js §2): a part meshed → closed, ≤ N triangles, the eye colour on the
   head mesh; the cache's hit rate on 40 villagers ≥ 50 %.
3. **Clothes and hair**: collar, sleeves with cuffs, hems with one or two voxel folds, coats and robes for the hall
   city; hair with volume (its crease against the skin kept by the colour regions). Six outfit families at least
   (work clothes with overalls, apron dress, tunic with belt, long coat, robe, suit jacket / waistcoat, kids' tee and
   shorts), colour sets from a palette; neighbours never identical (test §3).
4. **Movement (src/shared/folkanim.js, node test §4)**: walk with hip sway 2–3° and counter-rotating shoulders, arm swing
   out of phase with the legs, head toward the look target ≤ 40°, idle with breathing and a glance, sit pose for café
   chairs, the step length matched to the speed so a planted foot does not slide (foot ground speed ≈ 0 in stance).
5. **LOD**: smooth parts within 24 blocks, the half-res voxel LOD beyond, everyone voxel on Very Low / Low. The shadow
   map never draws people (they take the CPU heightmap shade): unchanged. Measured: ms/frame with 40 people in view on
   Balanced, voxel vs smooth (SwiftShader, relative), the mesh build time per villager, the memory of the meshes.
6. **Tests**: node test/folk_real.js (all of the above); browser test/folk_real.mjs — the hall city plaza at P1 (seed 53,
   vgen 9, CELL -2,-1 as trailer_town.mjs) and a village street, 5 villagers frozen at 3–6 blocks at eye height,
   shots test/shots/folk_real_{before,after}.png (the same villagers, the same time; before = smoothFolk off and the old
   rig from a BEFORE_DIST build), a close portrait at 2 blocks, the costs. Very Low measured (never slower).

Hard rules kept: no block ids, no generator change (gen_same/mesh_same/light_same untouched), no external requests,
every new text bilingual (g.set.smoothFolk), everything procedural and our own.
