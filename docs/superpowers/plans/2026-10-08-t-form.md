# t-form · Form trotz Voxel — smooth surfaces from any voxel model, shape tools in the Crafter

The owner (8 Oct): „Nutze oder implementiere alle nötigen Tools, die du im Editor benötigst, um Shape und Form trotz
Voxel verändern, manipulieren und meinen Wünschen entsprechend ändern und verbessern zu können."
Two deliveries, in this order: (A) Glatt-Modelle — a surface-nets mesh for every model piece that has no hand-built
smooth mesh (the game draws it in the furniture pass when „Glatte Möbel" is on); (B) Formen-Werkzeuge — lathe,
extrusion along a path, bevel, sub-voxel sphere / cylinder / cone in the Studio's Crafter, and a ✦ Glatt look on
every model card and in the Crafter's 3-D view.

## 1. The frames (shot G · white interior, and the rooms the furniture pass serves)

**What the trailer shows** (t1_26, t1_27, t1_29, t1_30, t2_28, t3_10): nothing in the rooms has a stair-stepped edge.
The white benches and plinths are soft rounded slabs (t1_29 left, t1_30 front), the pad in the middle is a white
rounded box with a recessed glowing blue top and a cyan ring (t1_30, t3_10), the ceiling coves are curved bands, the
glass stairs have glowing front edges, the plants in their pots are round. Every form reads as *made*, not as cubes:
radii of a few centimetres on every edge, materials that change with a clean crease (the shade against the stem, the
cushion against the frame), smooth shading across a curved surface.

**What the game shows** (the base v041 + t-smooth, pictures `t-smooth/test/shots/trailer_props_{living,dining}_after.png`
and `t-smooth2/test/shots/trailer_props2_hall_before.png`, taken by the siblings on the same build): the 81 hand-built
pieces of round I (sofas, chairs, tables, lamps, beds, kitchens …) are smooth; right next to them the other 299 model
pieces are still cubes of voxels — the fridge and the dishwasher in the open kitchen (living, left), the curtain at the
window (living, right), the sideboard, the dining bench, the pictures, the piano, the fireplace, the wardrobes, the
vanity, the bathroom pieces, every Modern Living piece (236), the Nova pad (t1_30's plinth: a 3 × 1 × 3 voxel box with
rounded corners in steps of 1/32), the garden pieces. In the mansion's hall (gen_modern.js) every piece is a VX block:
the urns, the gold pictures, the fountain, the reception desk, the chandeliers.

**The differences, by how much they change the impression**
1. Voxel stair-steps on every curved or bevelled piece that has no hand-built mesh (299 of 380 models) — the eye goes
   straight to them next to the smooth pieces. → (A) surface nets for all of them.
2. No crease between materials on a smoothed piece would melt a lamp shade into its stem. → creases kept where the
   material or the colour changes sharply (split normals, no smoothing across).
3. The player cannot make a round form in the Crafter except voxel by voxel (a vase, a pipe, a handrail, a bevelled
   counter). → (B) lathe, extrusion along a path, bevel, sub-voxel primitives.
4. The player cannot see what a model will look like smooth before it stands in the game. → ✦ Glatt on the cards and
   in the Crafter's view.

## 2. Plan (A) — Glatt-Modelle

* **`src/shared/smoothvox.js`** (all threads) — the mesher. A volume (packed VX voxels: colour, PRESENT, EMIS, GLASS,
  GLOSS, HOLO, material bits 29–31) → naive surface nets: one vertex per surface cell at the mean of its edge-crossing
  midpoints (a 256-entry table by corner mask), a quad per sign-changing voxel edge, split along the shorter diagonal.
  Opaque and glass are two passes (glass faces only against empty space, so what stands behind glass keeps its
  surface). Regions: every palette value gets a region (its material class — fabric, wood, metal, glass, light, matte,
  gloss, leaf, marble, leather — and a colour cluster, RGB distance > 50 starts a new one); a cell whose faces belong to
  several regions is a crease cell: its vertex is split per region (own normal, own colour), it smooths only along
  other crease cells. 2–3 Laplacian passes (λ 0.6) clamped to ±0.35 voxel of the original position. Normals from the
  faces (area weighted) per output vertex; colour = mean of the faces' voxel colours; a cheap AO (ten point samples of
  the occupancy along the normal and four tilted rays). Res-32 models are meshed at 16 per block (the dominant voxel of
  each 2³ cell, kept when ≥ 2 of 8 are set: thin parts survive), res 8 / 16 at their own resolution; a far mesh at 4
  per block. Adapters: `toFurn` (props.js' 14-float layout, centred on the footprint like the hand-built models, opaque
  first, then glass, a soft contact shadow, Uint16 indices) and `toStudio` (the Studio's 15-float model pipeline).
  An LRU cache (256 models, 24 MB cap) in every thread that meshes.
* **The game's path (lazy, in the worker, never per frame on the main thread)**
  - mesher.js — one additive line at the end of `propTables()`: `VR.SmoothVox.tables(PROPT, …)` gives every state of
    an eligible model (VM models of the furniture / object packs that are not in `VR.PROP_FURN`: not the windows,
    gutters, downpipes, garage door, the plants pack and the Nova tread, which join their neighbours) an *auto* kind
    (`4096 + index`, an Int32 copy of the kind table) and its anchor (part 0); its group stays 0 until its mesh is
    ready, so the mesher draws its voxels until then. A switched-off lamp (`lit` 0) has its own entry (the dark look).
  - worker_main.js — the `mesh` job applies the job's ready bits first (`SmoothVox.prep`) and reports the eligible
    models it met that are not ready (`svWant`); a new `smoothvox` job meshes one model (transferable arrays).
  - world.js — two additive lines: a mesh job carries the ready bits (`SmoothVox.tagJob`), a mesh answer's `svWant`
    asks for the meshes and remembers the section (`SmoothVox.want`); when a mesh arrives it is registered in props.js
    and the sections that wanted it are made dirty → they come back with smooth anchors.
  - props.js — a few additive lines at the end: `PR.registerAuto(kind, name, size, cell, mesh)` and `PR.furnDef`
    resolving kinds from `PR.AUTO0`; the hand-built tables are untouched (hand-built meshes win: their blocks never
    get an auto kind).
  - renderer.js — one token: `propSet`'s kind array is Int32 (auto kinds are > 255).
  - Very Low: „Glatte Möbel" is off there → no tag, no prep, no want, the mesher's tables never built: unchanged.
* **Tests** (node): `test/smoothvox.js` — the 16³ sphere is closed (every edge in two triangles), inside its voxel
  bounds, unit normals, no NaN, triangles ≤ 2 × surface cells; a two-material model keeps its crease; a 32-res chair
  stays in its footprint; all 380 catalogue models through the mesher ≤ 25 ms each; the game path (tables, ready bits,
  wants, the registry, the anchor's placement for every facing) with fakes; Very Low's mesh output unchanged.
  Browser: `test/smoothvox.mjs` — the villa's dining room and the mansion's hall with „Glatte Möbel" on, before (the
  auto pass held back) / after, the count of pieces drawn from surface nets vs hand-built, the Very Low frames.

## 3. Plan (B) — Formen-Werkzeuge

* **`src/studio/formtools.js`** — pure voxel functions (node-testable) + the UI: a ✦ Formen group in the Crafter's item
  panel: Drehkörper (a profile of 6–12 points dragged in a side panel, revolved round the model's vertical axis into the
  grid with the current material), Extrusion entlang Pfad (a polyline in the top view, a circle or rounded-box cross
  section of a chosen width), Abrunden (a bevel of radius 1–4 voxels on the convex edges inside the selection box —
  per axis-slice the convex corners are cut to a quarter circle, the radius limited to half the local thickness so thin
  parts survive), Kugel · Zylinder · Kegel with sub-voxel radius (coverage of the voxel centre against the exact
  surface). Every tool goes through `CR.vApply` (one undo step, the design stays voxel).
* ✦ Glatt: a toggle on every model card (studio.js' Möbel / Fassade cards and catalog.js' cards of models) and in the
  Crafter's 3-D view; the surface-nets mesh comes from the Studio's worker (`smoothvox` job, the same code) and is
  drawn with the Studio's model pipeline; the card's picture is rendered smooth.
* Tests: `test/formtools.js` (node: the lathe of a vase profile is rotationally symmetric, the extrusion fills the path
  with the right width, the bevel removes exactly the convex edge voxels, the primitives' volumes); browser
  `test/formtools.mjs` (de-DE: the Studio, the Crafter, a lathe vase, a bevelled box, ✦ Glatt on a sofa card).
