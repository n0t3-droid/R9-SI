# T-biomes · the endless world in the trailer's look · Plan

> **For agentic workers:** steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax. Node tests run through `bash test/wt_run.sh <name>.js 300`, browser tests through `bash test/wt_run.sh <name>.mjs` (background, polled). Branch `t-biomes`.

**Goal:** the world beyond the Heartland looks like the trailer's vista frames (`docs/trailer/frames/t1_01`, `t1_03`, `t3_01`, `t3_02`): ember canyons with lava, prism fields of giant crystals, terraced green hills, sky isles with falls, palm cities, rail corridors, saturated greens and cyan rivers. Everything is generated from the seed. Only worlds made at `vgen >= 6` get the new look, so older worlds stay byte for byte (`test/gen_same.js`, hash `3b54af9e`).

## 1 · What is built (vgen 6 only)

Source: `src/shared/gen_trailer.js` (new). Small additive edits: `gen_realm.js` (climate `terr` field, noise init in `islandCell`, `VR.RealmSpire` export), `gen_village.js` (`TECH_P6` palm-city shares), `game.js` (title world and new worlds at vgen 6).

Gate: `TR.on(g)` is `g.vgen >= 6 && singleBiome < 0 && !flat`. The Heartland (inside `HEART.R1` + 8) keeps its own look.

## 3 · Cost of the generation (far land, seed 1, 64 chunks, CPU time)

Generation reads no settings (no preset dependency), so this is the cost on Very Low as well. The render cost of the new mesh was not measured in a browser run.

| Run | vgen 5 cold / warm (ms per chunk) | vgen 6 cold / warm | ratio cold / warm |
|---|---|---|---|
| `trailer_gen.js` part 8, final run (alternating, min of two) | 8.0 / 3.0 | 7.6 / 3.6 | 0.95 / 1.20 |
| four further runs, the same loop (alternating, min of three rounds) | 6.7–7.4 / 2.5–2.8 | 7.1–8.0 / 2.8–3.0 | 0.99–1.07 / 1.04–1.13 |
| one run with the trail post pass switched off (`VR.Trail.post` = no-op) | 6.95 / 2.55 | 6.86 / 2.47 | 0.99 / 0.97 |

The clock is the process's CPU time (`process.cpuUsage`). Earlier wall-clock numbers on this 2-CPU box (other agents' browser and node runs active) swung between 0.76× and 1.47× from one run to the next, so they are not used here. The cold chunks are at parity within the noise (0.95–1.07×). The warm chunks are about 4–20 % slower in five runs (median about 13 %); with the post pass switched off one run gave 0.97×, so the post pass is the likely source. **Status: the cold cost does not get slower; the warm cost gets slower by about 13 %, so "Very Low must not get slower" is not met for warm chunks.** The first item of section 5 is this cost.

## 5 · Ordered differences and the plan

These are planned from the trailer frames (t1_01, t1_03, t3_01, t3_02) and from the top-down maps of the flight area (vgen 5 and vgen 6, 512 blocks around the spot; the maps are scratch renders, not the game). They are to be checked against the pictures before the next change.

1. **Lava cracks.** The frames show long glowing cracks across dark canyon floors. The generator has only vents (a throat of 7 blocks). Add crack lines in the ember canyons (a deterministic path per cell, lava on the floor). Test: a crack in an ember zone within 40 seeds.
2. **Giant crystals in view.** At the flight spot the top-down map shows small crystal speckles and one violet field; the top-down view cannot show tower heights, so the frames' foreground towers are unconfirmed until the picture is checked. Options: raise the spire share in prism fields, or plant spires on mesa tops in the ember zones. Check the count per view in the picture.
3. **Terraces.** The stepped green is visible in the map; the frames show more of it in the middle distance. Widen the zone (raise `smoothstep` bounds of `nTerr`) only after the frequency tests are updated.
4. **Rails and roads.** The corridors are kept clear, but no rail is in the view at this spot. The rails and trams are t-infra's; this branch only keeps the land open for them.
5. **Cost (Very Low).** Warm chunks are about 13 % more CPU than vgen 5 (section 3). The trail post pass is the likely source. Cheaper steps: skip the post pass for columns with no terrace, ember or prism climate; reuse the chunk's column tops instead of scanning from y 254 in `solidTop`; cache the corridor lines per chunk. Re-measure with `process.cpuUsage` after each step; the target is at most 1.0×.
6. **Floating isles.** The frames show isles left and right of the vista. None shows up in the top-down map of the view. The isle density is already tested (6.7 % of the far land); a view-based check is still missing.

## 6 · Known gaps

- Lava appears only as vents (small throats). The trailer's lava cracks (long glowing lines across the canyon floor) are not generated.
- No smoke from the vents (the chimney is a basalt column).
- Giant crystals are rare: prism fields are 7.9 % of the far land, and a spire needs a prism column at the spot.
- Realm spires, basalt pillars and outposts do not yet respect the corridors; roads and trams come with t-infra.
- The biome name in the F3 debug line is English (debug text only; the codex uses the translated key).
- Cold generation is 1.2–1.5× slower per chunk on this box (section 3).
- Render cost of the new mesh on Very Low was not measured in a browser.
