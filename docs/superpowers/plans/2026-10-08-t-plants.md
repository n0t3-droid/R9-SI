## 2 · The game's picture (before, v041 town at vgen 5)

The before run is `PHASE=before` (vgen 5). The plant code is behind `VR.Plants.on(g)`, and `test/gen_same.js` holds the vgen 5 terrain hash, so the before run is the v041 town. Pictures: `test/shots/trailer_<frame>_plants_before.png`, once the run has had a browser slot (see §5). From the code (checked on the pictures where they exist):

- Palms are `VG.palm`: a trunk of 4–9 blocks and eight short fronds in a crown block. The fronds are flat plates (`vx_palm_frond`), not drooping chains. Nothing moves.
- Hedges (`vx_hedge`), bushes (`vx_bush`, 8³ ball) and flower beds exist. No blossom bush, no tufts, no flower clusters.
- Round trees on the isles are `GEN.TREES.roundTree` (block trees). The Heartland's mesas have none of the trailer's dense crowns.
- Hills behind the city are smooth grass (`gen_hills.js`): no terraces, no crop rows.
- The VX pieces stand still: the mesher's sway bits (w5 5–6) are never set for VX quads.

## 3 · Differences, ordered by how much they change the picture

1. **Palm crowns.** Game: eight flat stub fronds in one block. Trailer: 8–12 long fronds that arch and droop, coconuts under the crown. → `vm_palm_crown` (7×2×7 blocks, res 8, ten rods with leaflet rows and three coconuts; 54 parts), placed through `VR.Plants.palm`.
2. **Wind.** Game: nothing moves. Trailer: crowns and leaves move, trunks stand. → VX sway: `voxarch.js` sets w5 bits 5–6 from the model part's sway level (`VR.Plants.partSway`); the terrain shader already moves sway > 0 vertices.
3. **Avenue and garden palms.** Game: `VG.palm` flat-frond palms at the street sides, in yards and in gardens. Trailer: tall ringed palms in a rhythm along the avenue. → vgen 7: the street palms (3 blocks from the road centre, every 9 road steps), the yard palms (back corner) and the garden palms are shared crowns. A crown overhangs the road or a neighbour's yard seven blocks up; it is never placed over a house, the plaza, another crown or a palm base.
4. **Plaza palms.** Game: four palms standing on the paving at the plaza corners. → vgen 7: the four corners at (±4, ±4) instead of (±3, ±3), in planters (`vx_tech_planter`), so no trunk stands on the paving and the 7-block crowns do not meet.
5. **Round trees.** Game: block trees, thin crowns. Trailer: round crowns of dense leaf balls, two sizes, orange-brown trunks. → `vm_tree_round_s` (3×4×3) and `vm_tree_round_l` (5×6×5), res 8; isles get them on 45 % of their tree spots (vgen 7).
6. **Lawns.** Game: bare grass between hedges. Trailer: grass tufts and flower clusters. → `vm_tuft_grass` and `vm_flower_cluster` on the garden lawns (about 14 % of the lot's cells, never on the palm's trunk cell); isles get the same on their tops.
7. **Hedges and forecourts.** Game: bushes and ferns stood on the paved forecourts. → vgen 7: forecourt planters (no plant on a plaza), the front hedge row is hedge (not bush).
8. **Terrace crops.** Game: smooth grass hills. Trailer: rows of green that read as rice terraces. → `vm_crop_rows` (res 16, dark soil and two greens) is built; the hook on the hills is **not** placed in this branch (see §5).
9. **Blossom bushes.** Game: plain 8³ bush. Trailer: round bushes with white and pink blossom. → `vm_bush_blossom` is built; not placed yet (see §5).

## 4 · The plan (what was built, and how it is checked)

- **Pieces** (`src/shared/voxplants.js`, `VM.define` with `late: true`, registered via `VR.BL.late`, after every older block, so no existing block id moves; `test/id_snapshot.js` shows 7 appended types). Each piece has a far box (VM's averaged box per part, `voxarch.js` lodFromQuads).
- **Quad budgets** (measured, res 8 for the big pieces, all parts at facing 0): crown 3 050 quads in 54 parts (largest part 239); small tree 1 010 in 20; large tree 3 525 in 48; tuft 146; flower cluster 455; blossom bush 629; crop 89. Budgets in `test/plants.js` are 6 000 / 1 800 / 5 000 / 300 / 600 / 1 200 / 300.
- **Sway**: a piece's parts from row `swayFrom` up get `sway` (crown 3, trees 2, lawn pieces 1–2); one level per column, so parts never move apart. The emitted quads carry it in w5 bits 5–6; the node test reads them back through `VX.meshBlock`.
- **Shared crowns are placed last** (`src/shared/gen_village.js`, the crown pass before the chunk buckets): every yard, garden and avenue palm is a candidate; a candidate is placed only if no op and no fill of the town reaches its trunk column or its 7×7 crown at those heights, and no accepted crown is within 6 blocks (the 7×7 footprints do not meet). So a crown is never cut by a later house's air, a street palm or a lamp, and never overlaps another.
- **Tests**:
  - `test/plants.js` (node, ALL OK): every piece exists and compiles, stays under its budget, sways as designed; the sway reaches the emitted quads; the shared palm places its trunk and whole crown; the plot of Nova Haven (81 chunks, x −64…79, z −192…−49, vgen 7, seed 4242) has **1 474 plant blocks** (≥ 150), **no plant on a street or a plaza** (0), **11 shared palms, all with whole crowns** (0 broken; the four plaza palms among them).
  - `test/gen_same.js` (vgen 5 hash 3b54af9e), `test/id_snapshot.js` (2 031 types, 7 appended), `test/model_names_de.js`, `test/lang.js`, `test/model_smoke.js` (378 models), `test/light_same.js`: ALL OK.
  - `test/mesh_same.js`: ALL OK after one rebaseline (see §6).
- **Browser**: `test/trailer_plants.mjs` frames the nine shots at dusk (tick 12 500), counts plant blocks within 34 blocks of each camera, writes the pictures for PHASE=before (vgen 5) and PHASE=after (vgen 7).
- **Mesh cost, measured** (node, the 3×3 chunks around Nova Haven's centre, seed 4242, classic look, vertices = stream words / 8):

## 5 · Gaps (honest)

- **Frame cost at Very Low: not measured.** The numbers above are mesh vertices, not frame times; the browser queue held the runs (see the run status in the report).
- **Pictures**: the before and after runs were queued behind other agents' browser runs; the pictures and the per-shot counts are reported when they exist. Until then §2's picture claims are from the code.
- Crop rows are built but not placed on the hills (`gen_hills.js` is t-city's terrace code; the hook belongs after its terraces merge).
- Blossom bushes are defined but not placed.
- Vines on walls are not built.
- Round trees on the mesas are not placed (the isles get them; the Heartland's mesas keep their trees).
- Trailer palms are about 8 blocks wide in the picture; the shared crown is 7 wide (fronds reach about 3 blocks out); the trunk height is 7–10.
- The default world is still vgen 5 in this branch (`game.js` keeps 5; `createWorld` takes `o.vgen`, which the browser test uses for vgen 7).

## 6 · Merge notes

- `src/shared/voxarch.js`: two additive lines (`swayB` before the emit loop; the `w5base` term).
- `test/mesh_same.js`: rebaselined once, with the reason in its comment. The cutout texture indices after the seven new models moved up by 7 (the texture word of 36 quads in four sections); geometry, colours and the other streams are identical, compared word by word. Any other branch that appends blocks before those cutout tiles will move the same hash, and needs the same look at the words.
- `src/shared/gen_village.js`: the plaza corner palms (planters), the avenue's street palm, the yard palm, the garden palm (all behind `VR.Plants.on(g)`); the crown pass and `crownCands` before the chunk buckets; the forecourt planters, the hedge row, the garden fern/planter routing, the garden lawn scatter. t-city changes the same palm sites (`VG.palmTall`): keep its vgen 6 branch and put `VR.Plants.on(g)` (vgen 7) in front of it.
- `src/shared/gen_isles.js`: the tree branch behind `VR.Plants.on(g)`.
- `src/main/game.js`: `createWorld` takes an optional `o.vgen` (default 5 as before).
- `build.js`, `test/load_shared.js`: `voxplants` after `voxmodern`.
- The default world stays vgen 5 until t-city's merge makes 6; vgen 7 needs the default bumped in `game.js` after both merge.
