## The crown pass (vgen 7)

- Candidates: the avenue (road 0), the palm roads (roads 1+), the yards, the gardens and the plazas hand their shared crowns
  to one pass at the end of the plan (gen_village.js). The plan records every palm in plan.palms and every drop in plan.crownDrops.
- Rules: the trunk stands on free land (no road, plaza, path or HOME) on the soil of its cell (climbed up to 8 blocks; stepped
  down over lower ground up to 3), with air up the trunk. The 7×7 crown holds only air or leaves at its two rows, so a crown may
  overhang a street, a lawn, a path or a hedge. A house wall, a roof or a light strip inside the crown moves the palm to the first
  spot that fits; the spot itself first, then up to 4 cells away (the frame wants 1–2; 4 is needed, see the numbers). Only when no
  spot fits does the trunk grow, up to 16 blocks.
- The state sets the pass reads come from the registry (VR.Plants.leafStates, soilStates); nothing is registered.
- Ten seeds (4242, 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 42, 99, 1234), palms placed (vgen 7 / the baseline):
  avenue 8/8 on every seed (vgen 5 has 8); gardens 1/1; plazas 4/4; yards equal to vgen 6 on every seed; palm roads equal to
  vgen 5 on nine seeds and one short on seed 2 (road 1 at (−45, −107): its trunk cell is a lot's entrance path, every cell within
  4 cells is a path, a lot or a road). That drop is the only one of the ten seeds.
- Moves on the ten seeds (532 palms): the avenue and the palm roads (373) stand on their own spot 167 times, 1 cell away 120,
  2 cells 31, 3 cells 25, 4 cells 30; the yards (149) 37, 31, 37, 40, 4; the gardens (10) 4, 2, 1, 3. With the search limited to
  two cells, five palms would be dropped on these seeds (three avenue palms: two on seed 2, one on seed 99; one yard on seed 1234).

## Vgen 5 is not the baseline for the yards

Vgen 5 and vgen 6 offer different yard spots: the detail layer (vgen 6) changes the house storeys, so the yard candidates
differ (seed 2: vgen 5 has 12 yards, vgen 6 has 9). The test compares the yards with vgen 6. Avenue, palm road, garden and plaza
counts are compared with vgen 5 (their candidates do not depend on the storeys).
