# T-smooth2: real furniture, round II (plan, 9 Oct 2026)

Branch t-smooth2, worktree /home/claude/vr/.worktrees/t-smooth2, base v042 (6fae587). Round I (t-smooth, plan
2026-10-08-t-smooth.md) made the palms and 64 furniture models smooth; its §8 lists what stayed voxel. This round closes
those gaps so that a furnished room shows no voxel piece: curtains, fridges, the dishwasher, range hoods, art and
mirrors, desks, wardrobes, the villages' chandeliers and lanterns, the corner sofas, the mansions' pieces; the rug runs on
under the furniture standing on it; the water's planar reflection draws the props; the smooth furniture follows the
Klassisch look in its material response. Very Low keeps the voxel pieces and builds none of it.

## 4 · The plan (and what was done)

- **Models (src/main/props2.js, new, after props.js in build.js MAIN).** Built with round I's builder (PR.FB; the
  helpers exported as PR.H) from the voxel pieces' own numbers, 16 units a block, the front at −z: 110 model keys for
  100 block names — curtains (a sheet in soft vertical folds on a rod with rings, linen or grey), fridges (a rounded
  steel box, the freezer seam, bar handles, a dark vent; the French door one), the dishwasher (steel round a black glass
  front), range hoods (chimney and canopy, a light strip; the island hood), art (a thin frame round a canvas painted in
  the furniture atlas: warm, cool, gold, abstract, landscape, photo — the block's own picture), mirrors (MAT.mirror = 12,
  floor and round), desks (a thin top on slab legs or a trestle, a drawer box; the tech and school desks), wardrobes (a
  tall box, two door seams, handles, a plinth; lacquer or walnut; the villages' two-high ones), the villages' crystal
  chandelier, lanterns (a hanging one gets a chain), pendants, sconce, floor lamp, the corner sofas (they join the rows
  — PROP_JOIN), and the mansion's pieces from gen_modern.js (bookcases, vanity, wall cabinets, WC, the counter run with
  sink and hob, coffee tables, the tub, benches, the long dining tables and their chairs, the TV wall, stools, urns,
  tech planters, potted plants, the fountain), the villa's rest (sideboard, dresser, benches, shelves, office chair,
  vanity, WC, bath, shower, single and bunk beds, fireplace, piano, lounger, coat rack, laundry, towel rail, toy box,
  washer, sculpture, palm pots) and the garden's (garden chair and table, outdoor sofa, fire pit, parasol, path light),
  and Villa Nova's light pad (3 × 3: the plinth on its cyan line, the recessed blue top, the ring emblem). A census of
  the seed-4242 town (a scratch probe over the generated chunks) named the commonest room pieces still voxel after
  that: every village house's stove and counter (gen_village.js), the shelf nook, the tech houses' lockers — added too
  (the cast-iron stove with the fire behind its door, the wooden counter with a stone top, the locker's louvred doors;
  the nook takes the bookcase model in oak).
  Each model passes props_furniture.js's per-model checks (vertices in bounds and in the footprint, unit normals, known
  materials, no NaN, faces outward); props_furniture2.js checks what each kind must have.
- **Mesher (src/shared/mesher.js).** The 100 names are appended to PROP_FURN (round I's kinds keep their numbers; kinds
  16…196 < 256); joins for the corner sofas, the counters, the long tables, the wall cabinets, the tubs and the TV walls;
  PROP_STACK 4: a lantern with something above it hangs. **The rug under the furniture:** with the furniture group
  asked for, a furniture cell between rug cells (along x or z, looking past up to two pieces; a second pass between
  rugs and first-pass cells) gets a rug anchor too, and the rugs round it join it — no bordered hole. Air stays air (a
  hole a player dug stays a hole). Nothing changes without job.smoothProps & 2 (mesh_same).
- **Reflection (src/main/renderer.js).** reflectPass draws the props with the mirrored camera: tier 2 (High, Ultra) as
  in the main pass, tier 1 their far forms, no translucent pass; renderer.noReflProps switches it off (the cost test);
  stats.reflProps counts the draws. Very Low (water 0) never reflects.
- **Klassisch (src/main/shaders.js FURN_FS).** u_blockVar (VR.LOOK.vary) in the material response only: the detail
  snapped to texels, duller gloss, rougher sheen; the vertex shader is untouched (no change of shape). The mirror branch
  (m == 12): a room gradient indoors, the sky outdoors, a sun glint.
- **Test cameras (test/trailer_props2.mjs).** living2 moved above the TV (run 1 stood beside the floor lamp); the pool
  camera moved over the water's west end (run 1's never found the water). Their "before" is the voxel picture of the
  same run.

## 8 · Known gaps

- **The house, not furniture:** Villa Nova's benches (white slabs), glass stairs, ceiling light frame and glass walls;
  balustrades, columns, windows; ceiling spots and LED coves.
- **Still voxel elsewhere:** the supermarket's fittings (about 30 shop kinds), holo screens and the holo table, the farm's
  hay and troughs, crates, barrels, the wheelbarrow, the garage's wall shelf (vx_wall_shelf), the roof terraces'
  vx_lounger, the BBQ, the pergola; the gardens' bushes, flower beds and round topiary trees (plants, not this round).
- **Rugs at a section edge:** the rule looks up to three cells along a row of pieces; the mesher's padding is two, so a
  piece standing at a section's edge with its rug farther away can lose the rug under it in one of the two sections.
- **One painting per palette:** the voxel art varies its motif per block (position hash); the smooth canvas is one
  print per family (warm, cool, gold) plus the villas' abstract and landscape.
- **Draws:** a furnished room on High draws about 600 (round I: about 390, the voxel pieces about 250) — one instanced
  draw per model and form (near, far, glass). A shared far mesh for all furniture would halve the far draws.
- **Steel under the alien sky:** the fridges' and hoods' steel takes the lavender sky in its reflection, as the voxel
  steel did.
- gen_modern.js puts a bed into the mansion's dining wing (seen in mdining; the generator's, not this round's).
