# Trailer heroes and crowd, round 2 · Implementation Plan

**Goal:** three places of the trailer as the frames show them. The Hangar Hall with the two heroes at the console row,
blasters up, facing the camera (t1_16). The walkout: the heroes come out of the hall toward the camera, the city behind
them (t1_21). The Palm City street at dusk: 8 to 16 people in view on purposeful walks, a few pairs, kids running, two
or three on the benches, never a mob (t2_23). The heroes look up close like the trailer: chunky-cute, a cap helmet with
a cyan visor line, a glowing chest plate, shoulder pads, white and orange/blue panels. The Very Low cost is measured
properly: five alternating rounds, the median is the number.

**Spec:** `TRAILER.md` §1 (shots E and F) and §5 (heroes); the fidelity brief `docs/trailer/README.md`. Frames:
`docs/trailer/frames/t1_16.jpg`, `t1_21.jpg`, `t2_23.jpg` (640 × 348).

## Method

- **Camera:** eyes 1.6 blocks above the feet, vertical FOV 70, picture 1296 × 704 (the trailer's aspect, 1.84). The
  heroes' size is the projected height of the feet to 1.9 blocks over the frame height (`heroes[].frac` in the METRIC
  lines). The frames give 0.36 for t1_16 and t1_21 (about 3.7 blocks), 0.135 for t2_23 (about 10 blocks).
- **Places:** hall, camera 4.5 blocks from the heroes (predicted frac 0.30), pitch 11 degrees. Walkout, camera 4 blocks
  inside the door, the heroes walk the last 5 blocks toward it (predicted 0.34 at the end). Street, camera 6 blocks from
  the heroes' end of their walk (predicted 0.23).
- **Chunks:** the world streams chunks around the player, and the camera is teleported there. A spot is shot only after
  its columns are loaded (a 5 × 5 grid of points, 24 blocks apart at most) and the mesh pool is idle with nothing
  pending on three checks in a row (`around`). The first hall picture had its left half missing (grass and sky where the
  left wall and floor belong), before this wait existed.
- **Census:** the villagers inside the frame and the draw distance. Outdoor people are counted, indoor ones (home,
  sleep, meals, school, learn, office, cafe) are drawn but not counted. The census counts all outdoor people in view,
  the draw cap applies to the nearest ones.
- **Browser runs:** three in all. The first (an after run) stopped after the hall shot on a metric bug in the test's frame projection (fixed: the page keeps its own size). The second is the after run of this branch (`.tmp_tests/dist_after`, built from the committed sources; ALL OK). The third is the before run of the base build (`.tmp_tests/dist_before`), which waited in the browser queue for hours.

## Ordered differences (before → after)

1. **Hall (t1_16), the heroes' pose:** the base holds the blaster at the hip (arm 0.99 rad). The 'aim' act raises it to
   the chest, barrel ahead (arm 1.25 rad, forearm 0.25 rad, eased in over 85 % of the way per recomputed pose).
2. **Hall, the helmet:** the shell reaches the brow (y ≥ eye + 4 instead of + 2) and the visor is one cyan emissive line
   across the brow. The dark glass row under it is gone, so the eyes show below the cap.
3. **Hall, the chest:** a 5 × 5 glowing plate with a white centre, a cyan-white panel and an accent frame, in place of
   the 3 × 3 row of cyan dots.
4. **Hall, the shoulders:** 9 × 9 pads, one voxel bigger in every direction, with a white inset on the outer edge.
   The torso is one voxel taller for heroes (bodyH 19).
5. **Walkout (t1_21):** the same model, and the heroes walk the last 5 blocks toward the camera, the blasters up while
   they walk. The picture is taken after the walk, so the heroes stand and wave there (see the known gaps).
6. **Street (t2_23), the people:** the base's dusk (10600 to 12300) sends about half of the adults to the plaza or a
   park and every child to play, all at the same time. Now each person walks home at its own time between 11100 and 12400 (the streets empty gradually, about
   54 % home by 11800). One person in eight goes to the plaza (C.plazaPerson, 12.0 % measured). At most three sit on
   the benches, at most four children play their games, the others roam running. Adults walk to road points at least 12
   blocks away, pause 2 to 4 seconds, and about a third pair up. The draw cap is 8 people in view for Very Low, 12 for
   Low, 16 for the others; the nearest are drawn first.
7. **Street, the heroes:** the same suits walk the road with the blasters up (aim) at 1.9 blocks per second.

## Very Low and cost

Potato preset, the street camera, five alternating rounds per state; the median is the number (the rounds in brackets):

- **After:** no heroes 3.1 ms [4.3, 2.8, 2.7, 3.1, 4.3]; two heroes 4.0 ms [4.0, 2.2, 2.8, 4.3, 4.3]. The crowd's draw with the heroes 0.10 ms; the crowd's update 0.004 ms.
- **Before:** no heroes 4.4 ms [4.9, 4.4, 3.5, 8.6, 3.3]; two heroes 5.7 ms [4.0, 6.7, 14.9, 5.7, 4.1]. The crowd's draw with the heroes 0.10 ms; the update 0.007 ms.

The two heroes cost about 1 ms a frame on this machine in both builds (1.3 ms before, 0.9 ms after, by the medians), and the after is not slower by the medians. This is not a speed-up claim: the rounds spread by up to 11 ms, the two builds ran at different times on a 2-CPU machine shared with other agents' browser runs, and the no-heroes median dropped too (4.4 to 3.1). No villager is in view at potato's draw distance (32 blocks) from the street camera, so the crowd's cost at Very Low is not in these numbers.

## Known gaps

- **The test's first evening is a festival night.** The Lantern Festival gathers the village on the plaza every third evening (the village near the spawn holds it on day 0), so the walkout and street shots show the festival in both builds. The dusk walk home, the benches and the games are not in the pictures; the dusk budget is pinned by `test/t_heroes2.js` only. The browser budget is spent, so a non-festival street needs the next round.
- **The street shot does not meet t2_23.** About 120 villagers are in the frustum (the census does not check occlusion), and the 16 nearest are drawn. A village ranger on patrol stands close to the camera and appears to hide the orange hero in this run; the patrol route is random.
- **The hint toast stays in all six pictures** ("B = build hand"). The test hides `#toasts`, but the hint is another overlay element.
- **The walkout heroes stand.** They reach their end point 4 blocks from the camera and wave (state 'wave'), so the picture shows them still, not mid-stride as in the frame.
- **The hall heroes wave too** (the player is within 7 blocks), one hand up, which the frame does not show.
- **The far end of the hall differs between the two pictures.** The before shows the back door open to the sky and the city; the after shows a closed end wall with lights. Not investigated: the camera is the same in both runs.
- The street's heroes stand about 6 blocks from the camera at the end of their walk. The frame has them about 10 blocks
  away (0.135 of the frame height), so in our picture they are larger. The camera came from the brief's 4 to 6 blocks,
  not from the frame's measured size.
- The boy at the left edge of t2_23 and the small figure at the right are not reproduced: the walk, the wave and the
  girl are the next round's people.
- The people are not in the frame's places: the census counts what the game puts in view, not the frame's composition.
- The pairs use `Math.random()` for the choice to pair (the homes, the plaza and the seats are seeded per person), so the
  pairs differ between sessions.
- Every look with role 'hero' gets the taller torso, the chest plate and the shoulder pads, not only the trailer's two
  heroes: the player's suit presets and other players' hero looks in multiplayer. The helmet and visor are only on Blau
  and Orange. The test shows the crew only; the other places are not checked in a browser.
