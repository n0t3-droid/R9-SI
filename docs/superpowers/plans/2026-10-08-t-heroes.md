# Trailer heroes and people · Implementation Plan

**Goal:** the two trailer heroes — Blau (blue armour, white and orange panels) and Orange (orange armour, white and blue
panels) — with helmets, cyan visors, shoulder pads and blasters, chunky-cute as in the frames. The player can wear them
(the character creator's presets Blau and Orange), and the two stand, walk and wave as crew in the Hangar Hall and the
city. The people of the Palm City walk the light-line paths at dusk. Animation is smooth and budgeted: far people are
posed less often and nothing allocates per frame. Very Low must not get slower.

**Spec:** `TRAILER.md` §1 (shots E and F) and §5 (heroes), the fidelity brief `docs/trailer/README.md`. Frames:
`docs/trailer/frames/`.

## Ordered diffs

1. **No trailer suits.** The presets were "Nova Blue" and "Solar Orange": the old palette, no helmet, no white panels.
   → Places 0 and 6 of `HERO_SUITS` become **Blau** and **Orange** (the order is kept: the saved choice is an index).
   A helmeted suit has a shell over the top and back, a cyan visor line on dark glass, a crest in the accent colour.
   The chest has a white panel around the glowing core; the knees have white guards (`folkmodel.js`, `villagers.js`).
2. **Proportions.** Legs 22 (was 24), shoulders 8 wide (was 7), head 14 high (was 13): chunkier, two blocks tall.
3. **Crew stand still.** → the `hold` clip: the blaster ready at the hip, breathing, glances left and right. The free
   left hand waves (`wave_l`) once when the player comes within 7 blocks (25 s cooldown). A standing hero turns its body
   and head to the player when they are within 14 blocks, eased.
4. **Walk out without a stride.** → a confident stride (`FA.walk` with k = 1.2), a blend in and out of the walk (no
   snap), the blaster arm kept up while striding. Heroes walk to a place at a given speed and face their way of travel.
5. **Villagers ignore the light-line paths.** The pathfinder gave road cells a bonus, but the tech city's paths
   (`vx_floor_plaza`, its cyan `vx_floor_plaza_line` edges) were not on the list. They are now.
6. **No budget.** Villager poses were computed for every agent every frame, and the model key was built from the look
   every frame. → Distance bands (every frame within 24 blocks, every 2nd within 48, every 4th within 96, every 8th
   beyond), a cap per preset (24 Very Low, 40 Low, 96 others), the model cached per agent and LOD, and the pose clock
   clamp 0.1 → 0.5 s so a waiting villager does not lose its time.

## Very Low and cost

- Very Low is `potato` (`folk: 32`: villagers are drawn within 32 blocks). Its budget cap is 24 recomputations a frame.
- The switch: `settings.anim === false` freezes the heroes in their pose (`crowd.js` returns before stepping). No preset
  sets it and this branch has no menu for it, so Very Low animates the heroes too, and the measure includes them.
- Measured in the browser test on the street view, Very Low, 1296 × 704, swiftshader, 2 CPUs: `verylow_frame` (ms per
  frame, no crew: the base has none, so this is the comparison), `verylow_frame_heroes` (the same view with the two
  heroes placed in it: the worst case), `verylow_crowd_update` (ms per `C.update` with the two heroes), and
  `verylow_people` (the villagers in view on Very Low). See Results.

## Known gaps

- **Placement.** The crew in the real game is placed by the Gate Temple's hall (`t-temple`, `TEMPLE.crew`, `hall.js`);
  this branch's test places them with `VR.Crowd.addHero`. The merge needs that call from the hall.
- **People in view at dusk.** The brief asks for 8–16 people in view per preset. The number of people is the village
  plan of the Palm City (`t-city`), not this branch. The budget scales to more; the Results say what is in view now.
- **Routes into the villas** are the villagers' existing routines (home, work, plaza); no new routes were added.
- **The heroes on the road (t2_23).** The street picture shows the people only, as the brief asks; the two heroes that
  walk the light-grey road in the frame are not placed there. The Very Low measure places them at the plaza.
- The suits' colours are judged by eye against the frames; the blasters are the existing trailer blaster.
- **Commit 78603d0** (the road list) did not build: a comment sat inside the array literal. Fixed in 6fbcfc4. Merge both.
