# T-beauty · beauty from every angle — plan and findings

Branch `t-beauty` (base `a00fd82`, v042: Nova Haven vgen 8, Nahtlos by default, the trailer sky, echte Möbel II, the
far world). The owner: „alles muss auch außen Sinn machen, die Cities und modernen Häuser und Hügel und einfach alles
muss cinematic in jedem Blickwinkel sein, beauty ist Priorität". The trailer frames (`docs/trailer/frames`, read first:
t1_22, t1_24, t3_05, t3_07–t3_09 for the city and the hills; t3_01/t3_02 for the vistas) show a world that holds up
from the back as well as from the front: stepped villas articulated on every face, curved streets that always lead
somewhere, terraced green hills under palms, nothing floating, nothing cut.

## Method

1. **The audit by pictures** — `test/beauty_audit.mjs` (one Playwright file, the pattern of `trailer_city.mjs`): a new
   world with the default generator (seed 4242), the High preset as a player gets it (only the dynamic resolution held
   off so each picture is full size), times of day 08:00 (2000), 12:00 (6000), 18:30 (12500), 22:00 (16000). 26
   designed cameras and two seeded random cameras per site (14), 40 cameras and 14 extra times of day = 54 pictures:
   `test/shots/beauty_<site>_<n>[_<time>]_<tag>.png`, the cameras and timings in `test/shots/beauty_<tag>.json`.

   The harness: the camera is teleported (as `trailer_city.mjs` does), the area loaded from above, the ground read, the
   camera posed. Run 1 (aborted) showed the High preset's 14-chunk radius does not settle at the preset's streaming rate
   in the harness (7 000 sections unmeshed after 100 s a camera: 12 mesh jobs a frame, 600 frames a camera); since run 2
   the settle raises `renderer.q.upload` (the loading pace only) while it waits and puts the preset's value back for
   every picture and every cost frame.
2. **Mechanical scans** of the generated blocks (node, scratch tools, then the rules in `test/beauty_rules.js`): top-down
   maps of the Heartland, ruin and floating-block scans, the open street ends, the blank wall runs of the villa family.
3. **Fixes in the generators, test first**, all behind `g.vgen >= 9` (new worlds; old worlds keep their chunks — t-town's
   Weißstadt is vgen 9 too, both ask `vgen >= 9`), then the after run of the same cameras.

## The fixes (vgen 9)

1. **No random ruins in the designed land** (`gen_overworld.js` features: no ruined gate, well, camp or tower on the
   surface inside `HEART.R0`; dungeons and geodes stay underground; beyond the Heartland the random world keeps them).
2. **Turning circles** (`gen_city.js`, `NH.TURN`): every road end that meets no other road and not the plaza swells into
   a paved circle of radius 7 — the kerb line lights its rim (the same rule that lights every road edge), the light grey
   kerb round it, a raised bed (light grey kerb, dark soil, ferns, flower clusters) with a tall palm in the middle, a lamp
   on each side, palms on its far verge; level with the road where the road comes in (the hill road climbed a block at
   its end). The circle stays inside the city's disc (its centre slides back along the road when it would not).
3. **No blank wall** (`gen_nova.js` buildComp): the builder notes every opening; a run of plain wall longer than four at
   the window rows gets a window band of two or three with its lintel, until none is left. The family as vgen 8 builds it
   is byte for byte as before.

## Tests

- `test/beauty_rules.js` (node, red first): 1 the ruins (a vgen-8 world keeps its 10 known gates, vgen 9 has none there
  nor in 108 chunks spread over the designed land, the random world beyond keeps its own), 2 every street end joins a
  street, the plaza or a turning circle (paved, rim lit, kerbed, a planted island), 3 every side of every storey of every
  composition (and mirror) keeps a window within four blocks at vgen 9, the vgen-8 family hashes unchanged.
