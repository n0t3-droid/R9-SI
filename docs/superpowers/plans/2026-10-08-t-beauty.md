# T-beauty · beauty from every angle — plan and findings

Branch `t-beauty` (base `a00fd82`, v042: Nova Haven vgen 8, Nahtlos by default, the trailer sky, echte Möbel II, the
far world). The owner: „alles muss auch außen Sinn machen, die Cities und modernen Häuser und Hügel und einfach alles
muss cinematic in jedem Blickwinkel sein, beauty ist Priorität". The trailer frames (`docs/trailer/frames`, read first:
t1_22, t1_24, t3_05, t3_07–t3_09 for the city and the hills; t3_01/t3_02 for the vistas) show a world that holds up
from the back as well as from the front: stepped villas articulated on every face, curved streets that always lead
somewhere, terraced green hills under palms, nothing floating, nothing cut.

## Method

1. **The audit by pictures** — `test/beauty_audit.mjs` (one Playwright file, the pattern of `trailer_city.mjs`): a new
   world with the default generator (seed 4242), the High preset as a player gets it (only the dynamic resolution held
   off so each picture is full size), times of day 08:00 (2000), 12:00 (6000), 18:30 (12500), 22:00 (16000). 26
   designed cameras and two seeded random cameras per site (14), 40 cameras and 14 extra times of day = 54 pictures:
   `test/shots/beauty_<site>_<n>[_<time>]_<tag>.png`, the cameras and timings in `test/shots/beauty_<tag>.json`.

   The harness: the camera is teleported (as `trailer_city.mjs` does), the area loaded from above, the ground read, the
   camera posed. Run 1 (aborted) showed the High preset's 14-chunk radius does not settle at the preset's streaming rate
   in the harness (7 000 sections unmeshed after 100 s a camera: 12 mesh jobs a frame, 600 frames a camera); since run 2
   the settle raises `renderer.q.upload` (the loading pace only) while it waits and puts the preset's value back for
   every picture and every cost frame.
2. **Mechanical scans** of the generated blocks (node, scratch tools, then the rules in `test/beauty_rules.js`): top-down
   maps of the Heartland, ruin and floating-block scans, the open street ends, the blank wall runs of the villa family.
3. **Fixes in the generators, test first**, all behind `g.vgen >= 9` (new worlds; old worlds keep their chunks — t-town's
   Weißstadt is vgen 9 too, both ask `vgen >= 9`), then the after run of the same cameras.
4. **Continuation 2** (the machine restarted at 18:10 and stopped the after run of 18:04 before its first picture):
   - **the camera rule** — run 2's `far_1` stood inside the station's hull and `villa_1` in front of a garden mound (a
     black and a green blur). Every eye is now made clear before its picture: nothing solid within 0.9 of the eye at its
     height (walls, rock, a crown, a trunk — every VX/VM piece is solid, flowers and grass are not), the cell 0.6 under
     it free (the eye above the ground), the view ray free of anything solid for 2.5 blocks and of opaque blocks and
     leaves for 6; else the eye rises a block at a time (to 24), else it steps back along the view two blocks at a time
     (to 12) and rises again; a fixed camera keeps its look-at point. A dry run of the rule on the generated blocks
     (node, no browser) moves only `villa_1` — its spot is a small round tree's (up 3) — at vgen 8 and 9 alike.
   - **the settle** — run 2's settles never ended (1 100 … 1 400 sections stayed dirty within the view with every
     column shown and the pool idle: re-meshes the world leaves for later), so every camera ran to its maximum (60
     rounds, ~100 s). The dirty count is now the sections with blocks in the inner view (two columns in from its rim),
     and a settle whose view is all shown ends when that count has not fallen for four rounds.
   - **one after run** (run c2): `BEFORE_ONLY=villa_1,far_1` re-shoots the two moved cameras on the build before (the
     base `a00fd82`, vgen 8; its other 50 pictures are run 2's), then the branch's build (frozen in
     `.tmp_tests/c2_dist`) shoots all 40 cameras, then `COST=1` measures Very Low against the build before.

## The pictures (before, run 2) — what breaks the illusion

What the trailer shows and what the game showed, per viewpoint (the trailer frames first, then run 2's pictures of the
vgen-8 world, `test/shots/beauty_*_before.png`):

- **(a) hills** — t3_07 · t3_08 · t3_09: rounded green hills cut into fine rows like rice terraces, round trees and tall
  palms on them, stepped white villas with turquoise glass on the crests, a starry dusk. The game (`hills_1` golden /
  noon / night, `hills_2`, `hills_3`, `hills_r1/r2`): the same composition from above — terraces ringing round hills,
  villas on every second tread, the city below, the temple and the canyon behind — but the risers are 4–5 blocks of
  moss that read as dark walls at the golden hour and at night, the floating isles over the horizon hang as **grey
  cones of stone** (`hills_1`, `hills_2`), an **obsidian ruin** stands in the meadow east of the city (`hills_2`), the
  hill road's end cuts into the terraces (`hills_3`: a paved bulb with palms over a brown cut).
- **(b) street** — t1_22 · t1_24 · t3_05: curved light-grey streets with cyan lines on both edges that always lead on,
  palms in rows, lawns and flower beds. The game (`street_1/3/4`, `street_r1/r2`): very close to the frames at eye
  height; but the crescent's two ends and Palm Row's two ends **stop in the lawn** (`street_4`: the line runs out into
  grass, an obsidian ruin behind), and Palm Row's ends **climb onto a bank** two blocks over the lawn (`street_2`: a
  wall of dirt and kerb across the street, a moss wall beside it).
- **(c) backs** — the trailer never shows a back; the owner asks that every side makes sense. The game (`backs_1/2/4/5`,
  `backs_r1/r2`): glass ground floors, stepped storeys with light bands — but **blank walls** on four of the five
  compositions (`backs_1`: the corner villa's top box shows a bare grey face), and at the classic's glass back two
  **dark stepped boxes of moss** (the garden mounds, `backs_3`).
- **(d) villa** — Villa Nova from its garden and its roof terrace. The game: `villa_1` stood in front of a mound (a green
  blur, the camera's fault), `villa_2/3` see the temple and the hills over the roof's balustrade (its top just above
  run 2's eye at 95.6), `villa_r2` the moss mound again at night.
- **(e) temple** — t1_10 · t1_11 · t3_03: the slate temple on its flagstone plaza, round trees in rows. The game
  (`temple_1` … `temple_4`, `temple_r1/r2`): the temple as designed, but the plaza's eight trees are **dark oak-leaf
  blobs** (`temple_1` at the golden hour: black stepped cubes beside the city's round trees), and a ruin beside it.
- **(f) water** — t1_07 · t1_09: the isles' falls pour off the rims and **dissolve into mist** high above the land. The
  game (`water_1`, `water_r1`, `far_3`): every fall runs 85–101 blocks to the ground as a **straight blue pole** (six to
  ten in a picture), and on the lake's east shore **yellow sulfur crust floats** over the meadow (`water_2`).
- **(g) far** — t3_01 · t3_02: a dense, colourful, layered world to the horizon. The game (`far_2/3/4`, `far_r1/r2`):
  the meadows beyond the hills scattered with **obsidian ruins** (`far_2`: about ten in one picture, `far_r1`: two,
  `far_r2`: one), the isles as grey cones and blue poles, Nahtlos's single steps as dark contour lines on the wide
  lawns; `far_1` stood inside the station's hull (the camera's fault).

## The fixes (vgen 9)

1. **No random ruins in the designed land** (`gen_overworld.js` features: no ruined gate, well, camp or tower on the
   surface inside `HEART.R0`; dungeons and geodes stay underground; beyond the Heartland the random world keeps them).
2. **Turning circles** (`gen_city.js`, `NH.TURN`): every road end that meets no other road and not the plaza swells into
   a paved circle of radius 7 — the kerb line lights its rim (the same rule that lights every road edge), the light grey
   kerb round it, a raised bed (light grey kerb, dark soil, ferns, flower clusters) with a tall palm in the middle, a lamp
   on each side, palms on its far verge; level with the road where the road comes in (the hill road climbed a block at
   its end). The circle stays inside the city's disc (its centre slides back along the road when it would not).
3. **No blank wall** (`gen_nova.js` buildComp): the builder notes every opening; a run of plain wall longer than four at
   the window rows gets a window band of two or three with its lintel, until none is left. The family as vgen 8 builds it
   is byte for byte as before.
4. **Nothing hovers** (`gen_overworld.js`, `gen_hills.js`, `gen_realm.js`): no sulfur spring in the designed land; the
   Pod Hills' path lamps on a bank of the path's earth, the pods' bushes on their own ground; a fall's spring pool takes
   the grass and flowers off its cell.
5. **The plaza's trees and the classic's mounds** (`gen_temple.js`, `gen_nova.js`): the Gate Plaza's eight trees and the
   terraces' two are the city's round tree in an octagonal raised bed; the classic's two garden mounds rise in steps of
   one with grass on every top, so Nahtlos draws one soft slope.
6. **The isles' layered sides** (`gen_realm.js`, `lod.js`, `far.js`): under four of soil six to ten bands of ochre, umber
   and terracotta, grey rock below, moss among the tuff at the dangling tips (TRAILER.md §3.6); the far rings colour an
   isle's sides warm (`LOD.isleSide`), not grey stone.
7. **Level street ends** (`gen_city.js`, `gen_hills.js` `treadT8`): Palm Row's ends lie level with the lawn (vgen 8 climbed
   to 84 over the lawn at 82), no street above the terraces' base; the hill road's circle slides back off the treads.
8. **The falls dissolve into mist** (`gen_realm.js`, `vents.js`): a fall's curtain ends 18 … 35 below the rim, each of its
   cells at its own step (0, 2, 4 or 6 further: a ragged end); the chunk of its lead cell (the first of its 2 × 2 cells
   off the rim) lists a `mist` spawn at the foot, and `vents.js` puffs soft white spray there (a lava fall smokes and
   sparks) — High a puff every 0.25 s a fall within 320 blocks (about 20 alive a fall), Low every 0.9 s, Very Low none.
   The curtain stays as generated until a block beside it changes (`sim.js` flows fluids on neighbour updates only); a
   saved, edited chunk loads without its spawns, as the canyon's vents do.
