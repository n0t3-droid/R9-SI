# 🎬 t-cinema — the game plays its own trailer · Design + Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax; test first, commit small.

**Goal:** the game plays the three R9-SI trailers shot for shot: the camera paths, timings and framings of the shots
A–G, the game's own shuttles flying their formation at the right moments, the heroes where the trailer has them, the
letterbox, a title card at the start and at the end, the HUD hidden, the golden hour per shot, the music running, and
Esc leaves at any time back to where the player was. Entry: a 🎬 Trailer button on the start screen (Einfach and Profi,
next to „Andere Welt“), the same in the pause menu, and `/trailer`. Works in a fresh world (the Heartland at the
origin) and on Very Low without stutter.

## 4 · The differences, ordered by how much they change the impression

*Provisional until the BEFORE pictures are in (items 6 and 7 wait for them). Each item was checked against the v0.41
source (`git show a1e4d51:src/main/director.js`, the last commit before t-cinema).*

1. **No white house and no closing card.** v0.41 ends in the palm city: its last shot is F, with `R9-SI` as a caption at
   6 s of that shot. G, G2 and the closing card are missing.
2. **No heroes.** v0.41 has no actors: the hall and the walk have no people, and people are the trailer's most
   recognisable subjects.
3. **The shuttles are not on their marks.** A scripted ship's design is requested at its shot's first frame and the
   ship joins the traffic when the design is built, so the flight can start late. The random sky traffic is not held
   back and flies through the shots.
4. **The camera stops at every key.** v0.41 eases each segment in and out (`ease()` between each pair of keys), so the
   camera halts at every key. The new tracks pass through the keys at speed (C1, Catmull-Rom tangents).
5. **No title card while a cut loads.** v0.41 also waits for the chunks (`ready()` and 0.4 s, at most 6 s of game
   time), but it shows black, not a card, and it places the camera only when the shot starts, so no area is preloaded.
6. **Framings of A–D** (to check in the pictures): v0.41 starts A 720 blocks out on z (the new A: 470), B's camera
   starts 200 high (the new one: 118) and D's starts at z 175 (the new one: 112).
7. **Time of day** (to check in the pictures): E is 12000 in v0.41 and 12300 now; F is 11600 and 12700 now.
8. **Very Low:** v0.41 has no measure, and its `camAt` returns new arrays every frame. The new spline writes into
   preallocated buffers, and the ride is measured (section 7).

## 5 · The plan

### 5.1 The camera (src/shared/cine.js, `VR.Cine`)
* Cubic Hermite tracks in time with Catmull-Rom tangents (`(p[i+1] − p[i−1]) / (t[i+1] − t[i−1])`), zero tangents at the
  two ends of a shot: C1 across the keys, every shot starts and ends at rest, the speed bounded (test: 74 blocks/s max).
* The look target is a second track; yaw and pitch come from the difference. Evaluation writes into preallocated
  buffers (`Track.eval(t, out)`), so the ride allocates nothing per frame.
* The follow-cam (C) takes its pose from the scripted shuttle each frame (behind, above, looking ahead and down).
* The shot list is the data: id, length, field of view, golden-hour time, camera keys, scripted ships, heroes.

### 5.2 The director (src/main/director.js)
* The state machine: `card` (title; preloads the next real shot) → `load` (black, waits for the chunks: `ready()` and
  at least 0.4 s, real-time guard 12 s) → `run` (spline, fade in 0.5 s, fade out 0.4 s) → next.
* Scripted ships: their designs are made ahead (`prepare`); at a shot's first frame they join the traffic
  (`VR.Traffic.ships`) with `t0` as the delay inside the shot; a hovering shuttle (E) is `quiet` (no arrival flash).
* Random sky traffic is held back while the trailer runs (its countdown and its ships), and restored after.
* The heroes: `VR.Folk.heroLook` + `heroPose` + the villagers' rig (`ent.drawModel`), drawn in the opaque pass.
* Time of day per shot (`env.setTime`, the freeze flag on), HUD hidden, letterbox from the existing `#cine` bars.
* Esc (checked first in `update`) stops it and restores the player's pose, the time, the HUD and the traffic. From the
  start screen the temporary trailer world is left again (`quitToTitle`) and removed (`deleteWorld`).

### 5.3 Entry points
* Start screen (`src/main/start.js`): `🎬 Trailer` after „Andere Welt“ in both modes; it makes a temporary world
  (`tmp: true`, the Heartland seed 4242, Zen) and starts the ride when the world is in (`g.trailerAfterLoad`). Temporary
  worlds are never listed; a left-over one is removed on the start screen.
* Pause menu: `🎬 Trailer` in the way-on group (plays in the world you are in).
* `/trailer` and `/cinema` (already there): the same `play('trailer')`.
* Autosave is skipped during the ride (the camera must not be saved as the player's place).

### 5.4 Tests
* `test/trailer_path.js` (node, ALL OK): smootherstep (0/1, zero speed and acceleration at the ends, monotone,
  derivative), exact keys, C1 at the inner keys (1.5e-5), bounded speed, rest at the start of every shot, pitch range,
  the shot list (title cards, A–G in order, durations within ±12 % of the first trailer, 50–60 s total, starts = sums),
  every ship and actor valid, the follow-cam target, the golden hour, the preload of shot A, the reveal conversion.
* `test/trailer_director.js` (node, ALL OK, 33 checks): the director's state machine over a virtual clock — the cards,
  the loads, the cuts, the design cache (the pre-fix director fails it), Esc, and the temporary world.
* `test/trailer_cinema.mjs` (browser, one file, one browser: the v0.41 pictures first, then P1–P3):
  - BEFORE the six-shot director of v0.41 at 0.8/2.4/3.8 s of each of its shots (`cinema_before_*`).
  - P1 the start screen's 🎬 Trailer at High: the order of the shots, every shot's length (±0.12 s), the time of day per
    shot, the HUD hidden and the letterbox on in every frame, every cut with no column pending, the title cards read
    R9-SI, the ships of every shot that has them and the heroes of every shot that has them, no random sky traffic,
    the frame times (no frame above 3× the median), the start screen back and the temporary world gone. Pictures
    `test/shots/cinema_<shot>[_<n>].png` at the key seconds: title 1.2 s of the card, A 1.0 s, B 2.0, C 1.0/2.0,

## 6 · Known gaps (for the other fronts)
* **The white house (G, G2)** does not exist yet: the camera keys of G and G2 frame the palm city's street at
  (−60, 84.6, −150…−190), not a white house with glass stairs, a cyan pad and a white interior. The search for a glass
  villa with those parts in the generator timed out in node. The house belongs to the t-city / t-world front (the villa
  skins).
* **G2 opens on G's pose**: both cuts start at (−60, 84.6, −150) looking at (−60, 84.4, −180) (placeholder keys), so the
  second cut's glass-hall passage begins on the same frame as G.
* **The Hangar Hall's pad shuttle and the heroes** are the director's own (scripted); the consoles' blinking and the
  holo sweeps are the t-temple front's.
* **Portal veil motion** and the energy field of the second cut's portal view come from the temple front.
* **Clouds and the nebula by day** are the t-clouds / sky fronts; the ride only sets the time of day.
* **Weather during the ride**: the director freezes the time of day (`zenFreezeTime`), not the weather. Whatever the
  weather is when the ride starts keeps running through it; the pictures will show whether that is clear.
* **Very Low** is measured here as the game's own work per frame, not the graphics card's.
* **Frame times** cover the loop only. The starship designs are built in the worker and uploaded to the GPU in message
  callbacks between frames; the ride requests all of them when it starts, so they finish during the first title card,
  which the logic measure does not include.
