## What the frames show

The sky in every frame that has one is the same family: a nebula veil (magenta, violet, cyan) over the upper third,
a ringed lilac gas giant on the right with its ring seen at a slant, one or two grey moons, a blue-lilac planet, and a
sun low at the right horizon with a warm glow. Clouds are rare: they are white puffy cumulus only in the Sky Isles frames.

- t1_03 (vista, canyon rim, shuttle in the air): veil across the top; the lilac giant top right, ring at a slant; a grey
  moon at the top-right edge and a small one top left; the sun low right behind the shuttle; haze between the mesas.
- t1_01 (canyon flyover): the same sky; the giant behind the shuttle; two grey moons; sunset colour at the horizon.
- t3_01 (canyon, sun low right): giant top right with a wide ring; a floating island far top left; no clouds.
- t3_02 (citadel spires, the frame the brief calls the citadel): the spire cluster against a clear sky; giant top right;
  sun glow behind the towers. No cumulus wraps the spires in this frame.
- t1_07 (Sky Isles panorama): white cumulus at the top-left and top-right corners; two large planets, one banded
  blue-lilac; the sun glow at the right; mist sheets between the isles and along the horizon.
- t1_09 (Sky Isles follow-cam): a large banded blue-lilac planet top left; a grey moon with a ring top right; cumulus
  puffs top left; warm sunset right; mist under the islands.
- t1_11 (gate temple portal), t1_12 and t2_14 (Hangar Hall): no sky of note (a moon at the top-right corner in t1_11).

Frame mapping, for the record: the brief maps some frames to other content. Actual content: t1_11 is the gate temple,
t1_12 and t2_14 are the Hangar Hall, t1_09 and t1_07 are the Sky Isles, t3_01 and t3_02 are the canyon and the citadel.
There is no city-at-night frame in this set (t2_14 is the hangar); the city shot is therefore a check of the night sky
and the clouds against the sky of t1_11 and t1_01, not against a city frame.

## The four shots

Each shot is taken at the game's camera (vertical fov 72 at 720 px) with the cover at 0.2 (clear weather), the high
preset, and the time of day fixed.

### 1. vista (noon, tod 6000) against t1_03

Frame: the lilac giant top right with its ring at a slant; veil across the top; sun low at the right.
Game (before, picture): a milky haze over the whole frame; a deck of boxy white clouds (the largest top right); the giant
is a dark violet disc with a faint ring; the sun a pale glow at the right; a small moon top left.
Ordered differences:
1. The giant reads dark by day. Frame: lilac bands, lit everywhere.
2. The clouds are the flat boxy deck. Frame: none in this shot; the cumulus belongs at y 206-215 in the sky.
3. The veil by day is faint. Frame: magenta and cyan veil across the upper third.
4. The moons are faint.
Plan (done): the giant's shade side keeps its bands (0.3), the day factor 0.75; the veil base 0.4 with the veil colour
1.3 by day; the planet radii from skybodies.js (giant 7.0, blue 4.9, moons 1.2-1.8 degrees); cirrus streaks on balanced
and above (0.12); puffy cumulus sprites on balanced and above.
Viewer sees (after): a ringed lilac giant with bands and a clear slanted ring, top centre-right; a grey moon at the top
right and a small one top left; puffy white cumulus in four clusters across the top; a faint violet veil at the top.
Still short of the frame: the veil is much weaker than the frame's magenta, and the cumulus is not in the frame.

### 2. citadel (dusk, tod 11700) against t3_02

Frame: the spires against a clear sky; giant top right; sun glow behind the towers.
Game (before, picture): the cube deck at y 250 passes through the tallest spire and cuts it (a gap in the tower at about
y 230-265); boxy clouds at the top corners; dusk pink with violet above.
Ordered differences:
1. The deck cuts the spire where it crosses the deck plane. Brief: no deck on balanced and above; sprites wrap the spires.
2. No puffy cumulus. Brief: cumulus at y 200-215 wrapping the spires; the frame shows none.
3. The giant is outside this view (gap).
Plan (done): ten anchor sprites round the spire cluster (clouds.js ANCHOR, world space, y 207-212, radius 24-34), the
nearest fading when the camera is inside one (v_near); the cumulus field around the camera at cover 0.2.
Viewer sees (after): the spire is whole; soft white puffs wrap the towers at mid height, the nearest one large at the
right of the tallest spire; the sun glow low left behind a fog band; the high-tier god-ray streaks (pre-existing) across
the upper sky.

### 3. isles (sunset, tod 12000) against t1_07 and t1_09

Frame: the Sky Isles with white cumulus at the top corners, mist between the isles, a banded planet, sunset glow.
Game (before, picture): the boxy pink deck above the isles; no mist; the sun bloom at the top centre; the ground flat.
Ordered differences:
1. No mist. Frame: white mist sheets between the isles and along the horizon.
2. Cumulus shape. Frame: white puffy cumulus with lit tops and a lilac shade; the deck is flat and boxy.
3. No planet in this view (gap: the frame's two planets are outside the shot).
Plan (done): fog banks at y 104-114 within 300 blocks of the isles' centre (world space, FOG constants in clouds.js),
soft silhouettes from five taps on the atlas; the cumulus at y 206-215 as billboards (white tops, lilac shade, sunset
tint on the shade); cirrus streaks at 0.12; the sun's rim light from the atlas normals.
Viewer sees (after): puffy clusters at the top left, top centre and top right, white with a lilac shade; a sun bloom
with rays at the top centre; a soft white mist sheet across the lower half, between the isles and the foreground.
Still short of the frame: the foreground mist is heavier than the frame's, and it has straight-edged steps (see gaps).

### 4. city (midnight, tod 18000), the night sky over the palm city

Frame: no city-at-night frame in the set (see the mapping note). The night sky of t1_11 and t1_01 is the reference: stars,
the veil at full strength, the planets lit by the moon and the stars' glints.
Game (before, picture): stars and a pink-violet nebula; the boxy deck across the top; the city lit.
Ordered differences:
1. The night clouds are the boxy deck. Plan: moon-lit puffy cumulus on balanced and above (shade and tops use the moon).
2. The veil at night is already at strength (u_night owns the sky); unchanged apart from the LITE path on Very Low.
3. No giant or moon in this view.
Viewer sees (after): moon-lit puffy clusters across the upper half over the same stars and nebula; the clouds are blue-grey
in their shade and white on their tops.

### Very Low (potato), vista

Before (picture): the cube deck, a dark violet giant with no bands, the milky haze.
After (picture): the same cube deck (kept on Very Low), the giant lilac and banded with its ring, the moons, and a faint
LITE violet veil at the top centre. The nebula on Very Low has two octaves and no dust layer.

## Tiers

- Very Low (potato, lite): the flat cloud deck (cube layer, unchanged), the sky with the two-octave LITE nebula (no
  dust layer, about four noise evaluations instead of sixteen), no cirrus, no sprites.
- Low: the cube deck, with the full nebula.
- Balanced and high: sprites for the cumulus (up to 260 per frame, nearest first; a cell at cover 0.2 gives about
  120-150), the fog banks and anchors (about 35), the cirrus; high adds the shafts (existing god rays, unchanged).
- Ultra: as high.

## Measurements

Node (CPU, clouds.js): field() about 0.55 ms per call (cover 0.2); atlas() about 22-26 ms once (the first balanced frame).
Sprites per frame at the four shots: vista 123 cumulus + 35 fog/anchors; citadel 152 + 35; isles 104 + 35; city 147 + 35.
Browser test feature check (after): clouds, sky bodies and puffs on balanced all present (179 puffs drawn at the check).

Very Low frame time, browser (SwiftShader on a shared 2-CPU machine, the vista, the frame loop with gl.finish, four
blocks of three frames, the median of the middle two blocks):
- before: 2.8 ms (blocks 2.5, 2.7, 2.9, 7.6)
- after: 2.4 ms (blocks 2.0, 2.3, 2.5, 14.2)
Each run has one block loaded by other work on the machine, so the 0.4 ms difference is not a measured saving: Very Low
cost is unchanged in practice (the cube deck is kept; the LITE sky takes about four noise evaluations instead of sixteen).

Browser test results: before FAILED 3 (the three feature checks, cloud module, sky-body module, puffs on balanced: expected
on the base); after ALL OK. No page or WebGL errors in either run.

## Known gaps

- Vista, lower left: a translucent haze patch (about x 0-240, y 565-676) with a straight lower edge at y 676 over the
  grass. A fade at the quad's own border was tried offline and removed: it did not touch the mist's interior steps, and
  the offline harness draws no terrain, so the patch could not be reproduced there.
- Mist and puffs: the atlas's boxy lobes leave straight-edged steps in the isles' mist (left side, about y 500-600) and
  flat tops on some puffs. Next steps: a rounder atlas, and a wider mist blur (the five taps are 0.02 apart).
- The veil by day is weaker than the frame's magenta (the brief asks for a veil at about 45 percent by day).
- Planets: the giant and the banded planets are outside the citadel, isles and city views; only the vista shows the giant.
- Sun path: the game's noon sun stands at 25 degrees; the frames' sun is low (5-12 degrees). The eternal-dusk path is
  env.js's and is not changed beyond the body directions.
- The citadel's cumulus wrap is the brief's spec; the frame t3_02 has a clear sky.
- The foreground mist in the isles is heavier than the frame's.
- High-tier god rays are pre-existing (renderer); they show as streaks in the citadel and isles pictures.
- No city-at-night frame in the set; the city shot is a sky check.
- The browser pictures include the game's post pass (grade, bloom); the offline previews do not.
