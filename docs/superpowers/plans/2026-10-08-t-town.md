# Weißstadt, the hall city (t-town) — the owner's town of 9 Oct

> **For agentic workers:** the pictures come from `test/trailer_town.mjs` (a showcase: before/after pairs at the same
> world cameras, the villagers in view, the Very Low cost; `regress: skip`); the numbers from `test/hallcity.js` (node,
> the gate, ALL OK). Everything new is vgen 9 (`g.vgen >= 9`): worlds made before keep their chunks (`test/gen_same.js`
> keeps its reference). References: `docs/refs/weissstadt/` (README, `hall_full.jpg`, `innenstadt_full.jpg`,
> `video/v3_*.jpg`, the segment crops) — design references only, nothing copied, our own names everywhere.

**Goal (the owner's words, 9 Oct):** "wenn ich in die Villages gehe, sind die Einwohner dort extrem viele, ist ja gut,
aber dafür sind die Dörfer zu klein, zu eng, und deren Häuser nicht wie hier die Bilder plus die Segmente … keine
Lego-Villagers, wir wollen schon Realismus." Corrections an hour later: the town of the pictures stands INSIDE a vast
white vaulted hall ("die City befindet sich aber draußen und nicht drinnen wie in den Bildern"), and **no crystals
anywhere** ("ich mag die nicht, ob groß oder klein") — where the references show crystals the game uses lanterns, gold
trims, glass and light.

## The differences, by how much they change the impression

1. **Outdoors vs indoors**: there is no hall — the town must stand inside a vast white vaulted hall (96 × 160, 34–40
   high), bright inside.
2. **Scale and air**: three-block dirt lanes and a 13-block square vs a 7-wide cobblestone street with paved fronts, a
   round plaza 25 across, 5-wide lanes; houses of 6–8 blocks vs town houses 11–12 wide and 12 deep, two or three
   storeys.
3. **The houses' language**: pastel plaster cottages with shutters vs warm white stone, wood-brown quoins, a gold band,
   arched windows, storefronts with striped awnings and signs, balconies with balustrades, flower boxes, vines, timber
   gables.
4. **The square**: a well vs a three-tier fountain in a round basin, café tables round it.
5. **The crowd**: every bed a villager on a small village vs a population that follows the town's walkable area.
6. **Street furniture**: lampposts every nine blocks vs black glass-head lamps every 8, hanging lanterns, potted
   plants, market stalls under awnings, barrels and crates in the aisles.
7. **The people's clothes**: role colours vs the references' palette (robes and coats, gold trims), bags and baskets.

## Fixes after run 3 (this session)

1. **No floating island over a hall city** (vgen 9; gen_hallcity.js `VG.hallSkyClear` wraps `islandCell`, cached per
   island cell; far.js and lod.js read the same cells). Older worlds keep their islands; gen_same, light_same, mesh_same,
   lod_same unchanged. The vault's panels reach the springing (|u| ≤ 26, the half dome's d ≤ 26), as the plan said. Proof:
   test/hallcity.js — no island over the footprint (181 columns before), the vault over the plaza and the apse lit from
   below (1076 cells, the lowest 12), the apse's gallery ≥ 10 everywhere; hallcity_cost.js — the blocks' streams of a
   hall column 178 % of a village column's (193 % before; near 54 %).
2. **The street's cap counts the people on their way in** (folkcrowd.js `Crowd.onStreet`): only who has arrived indoors
   (asleep, or standing or sitting at the indoor act) is free of the cap. The hall city's evening: 45 of 149 drawn on
   High, 37 on Very Low (all 149 before; test/town_folk.js 6). Every town, old worlds too (the drawn set only).
3. **The people nobody sees cost less** (villages.js `pick`, `unseenWalk`; folklife.js `separate`): a person not drawn
   and not beside the player lives at the far rate (every 4th frame, the 8th on Very Low); a walk nobody sees — the
   person and its goal away from the player, the goal out of view or beyond the folk distance — searches no path, the
   person arrives when the walk would be done; the personal-space pass skips it and makes no arrays per frame; no speech
   bubble floats over a person the cap did not draw. test/town_sim.js (node, the P1 camera, the real update loop on the
   generated blocks): the hall city on Very Low 22.9 agent updates and 0.39 path searches a frame (41.9 and 0.91 before),
   0.34 ms a frame against the v042 village's 0.44 ms with the code before (1.03 ms before the fix); its people still sit
   on the terrace and reach their goals (26 and 44; 20 and 35 before).
4. test/trailer_city.js: new worlds are made with vgen 8 **or later** (the node regression of run 3 failed there only
   because vgen 9 is the default now).
5. test/trailer_town.mjs: the people in view with their acts and who is drawn (on the street / indoors); the Very Low cost
   broken down (the villagers' simulation, their draw, the world's render, the rest) and measured at a control camera C0
   outside the town looking away from it in both builds.
