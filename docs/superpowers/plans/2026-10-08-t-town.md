**Goal (the owner's words, 9 Oct):** "wenn ich in die Villages gehe, sind die Einwohner dort extrem viele, ist ja gut,
aber dafür sind die Dörfer zu klein, zu eng, und deren Häuser nicht wie hier die Bilder plus die Segmente … keine
Lego-Villagers, wir wollen schon Realismus." Corrections an hour later: the town of the pictures stands INSIDE a vast
white vaulted hall ("die City befindet sich aber draußen und nicht drinnen wie in den Bildern"), and **no crystals
anywhere** ("ich mag die nicht, ob groß oder klein") — where the references show crystals the game uses lanterns, gold
trims, glass and light.

## The references (what I see)

- **innenstadt_full.jpg / v3_01 … v3_06** (the street at eye height): a wide pale cobblestone street runs from the
  viewer to a round plaza with a three-tier stone fountain in a round basin (about 12 m across); left and right
  three-storey shop houses of warm white stone with wood-brown frames and quoins: ground floors are storefronts (big
  wood-framed glass, warm lit goods behind), striped awnings over them (cream/brown, one white/teal), a dark board sign
  above each awning, upper floors with arched windows, balustraded balconies, flower boxes and long vines hanging over
  the façades, dark slate gables with timber; black street lamps with glass lantern heads every few houses; café tables
  and chairs in front of the shops on both sides, people sitting, people walking with shopping bags and baskets. Over
  everything the vault of a vast white hall: piers, round and pointed arches, tall arched windows with stained-glass
  tops, a giant arched window at the end of the nave — the light floods in, the whole picture is high-key, warm white
  and gold.
- **hall_full.jpg** (the hall itself): a white-stone nave with piers, gilded and wood-brown accents on the arches,
  galleries with white balustrades at two levels, a grand central staircase with balustrades climbing to a gallery in
  the apse, round-arched doorways under it, market stalls under striped awnings in the foreground (wood counters,
  baskets, flowers, lanterns), barrels and crates, potted plants, vines on the piers, a white marble floor. (The
  crystal clusters of the picture are left out on the owner's word.)
- **The segment crops** (`hall/`, `innenstadt/`): arched windows (white frames, light glass, stained tops), the
  balustrade, the grand staircase, hanging lanterns (black iron, warm glass), the market stall (posts, a striped
  canopy with a scalloped edge), potted plants, stained glass (blue/gold/rose), striped awnings, vines, wooden barrels,
  beams, crates, tables; the storefront windows, the shop sign (dark board, gold letters), the street lamp (black,
  tall, a four-sided glass head), the central fountain (three tiers), cobblestones, flower boxes, shopping bags.
- **The people:** robes and coats in white, cream, blue-grey and brown with gold trims, shopping bags and baskets,
  sitting at café tables. (The pictures' figures are toy-like; ours keep `folkmodel.js`'s realistic proportions.)

## The game before (v042, vgen 8) — `test/shots/trailer_town_<shot>_before.png`

Seed 53, the meadow village of cell (−2, −1) at (−944, −296): a v2 village of `gen_village.js` — a square plaza of 13–15
blocks with a well, radial dirt lanes three blocks wide, cottages of 6–8 × 6–8 blocks on lots with 2–3 block margins,
lamps every nine blocks, farms outside. Every bed is a villager: about 3.5 per home, and at lunch one in eight goes to
the 13-block plaza. (Run 1's pictures: see "Runs" below.)

## The differences, by how much they change the impression

1. **Outdoors vs indoors**: there is no hall — the town must stand inside a vast white vaulted hall (96 × 160, 34–40
   high), bright inside.
2. **Scale and air**: three-block dirt lanes and a 13-block square vs a 7-wide cobblestone street with paved fronts, a
   round plaza 25 across, 5-wide lanes; houses of 6–8 blocks vs town houses 11–12 wide and 12 deep, two or three
   storeys.
3. **The houses' language**: pastel plaster cottages with shutters vs warm white stone, wood-brown quoins, a gold band,
   arched windows, storefronts with striped awnings and signs, balconies with balustrades, flower boxes, vines, timber
   gables.
4. **The square**: a well vs a three-tier fountain in a round basin, café tables round it.
5. **The crowd**: every bed a villager on a small village vs a population that follows the town's walkable area.
6. **Street furniture**: lampposts every nine blocks vs black glass-head lamps every 8, hanging lanterns, potted
   plants, market stalls under awnings, barrels and crates in the aisles.
7. **The people's clothes**: role colours vs the references' palette (robes and coats, gold trims), bags and baskets.

## The plan (built test-first)

1. `test/trailer_town.mjs` first (the cameras P0–P6 in the hall's frame, 14:00 and dusk, the villagers in view, the Very
   Low cost) — run 1: the BEFORE pictures of v042's village.
2. `test/hallcity.js` (node, red first): the site of the meadow/cherry climates becomes a hall city at vgen 9 and stays a
   village at vgen 8; the hall's geometry (the vault hollow, the windows glass, the vault's glass band, the stair climbs
   to the gallery, the street 7 wide, the plaza radius, lamps every 7–9 blocks, lots and houses ≥ 1.6× the old
   villages'), sky light through the glass on the street, the villager budget, the longest plan step under 60 ms, no
   crystal block anywhere in the plan.
3. `src/shared/gen_hallcity.js`: `VG.hallCity(g, site)` — a plan generator like `gen_city.js`'s Nova Haven (chunk
   buckets, yields between steps, the plan cached per site by `VG.planAdvance`). The hall: a nave with two aisles, round
   arches on piers, a clerestory with tall stained-glass windows, a pointed vault with a glass band along its crown (the
   light engine's sky light goes straight down through glass: the street is in full daylight), a half-round apse with
   the grand staircase and a balustraded gallery, a giant window in the entrance end, two gates in the long walls. The
   town: the main street along the nave, the round plaza with the fountain at the crossing (the cross street runs to the
   gates), shop houses in rows with side lanes and back lanes, café tables round the plaza, market stalls in the aisles,
   lamps every 8, hanging lanterns along the nave.
4. `src/shared/voxtown.js`: the kit pieces no older block serves, as late VX/VM models (`VR.BL.late`, append-only ids):
   the stained-glass arched window (three colourways), the black street lamp with a glass head, the striped awnings
   (two colourways), the shop sign (pictograms, no words), the market stall, the fountain tiers, the gold band.
   Reused: barrels, crates, lanterns, flower boxes, café tables and chairs, balustrades, beams, stairs.
5. The villagers (`villages.js`, `villagers.js`, `folkmodel.js`, `folklife.js`): the roster of a vgen-9 town is capped
   at 1 per 9 blocks of walkable street and plaza plus 2 per home; the Weißstadt outfits (robes, coats, gold trims),
   shopping bags and baskets, café sitting at the plaza tables.
6. Runs 2 and 3 of the browser test; the differences read against the references and fixed from the top.
7. vgen 9 the default of `createWorld` (game.js).
