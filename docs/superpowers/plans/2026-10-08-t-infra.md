# T-infra · the trailer's moving infrastructure — plan (2026-10-08)

Spec: `TRAILER.md` §1 (shots A–F), `docs/trailer/README.md` (the method, the shot list), the stills `docs/trailer/frames/t1_05.jpg`,
`t1_03.jpg`, `t1_01.jpg`, `t1_07.jpg`, `t1_21.jpg`, `t3_05.jpg`. Owner's complaint: the infrastructure, the animations and the
shuttles were "started and then left half done". This slice takes the moving things: the rail viaducts with their trams, the
trams on every rail line the world places, the headlights and the rail hum at dusk, and riding a tram.

Method (as in the brief): (1) read the frame, (2) frame the same place in the game at dusk (`TIME=12100`) with
`test/trailer_infra.mjs` and take the game's picture, (3) write down the differences in order of how much they change the
impression, (4) fix from the top, (5) take the picture again and compare.

Pictures (local, gitignored): `test/shots/trailer_infra_before_<shot>.png` (HEAD's build, `PHASE=before`) and
`test/shots/trailer_infra_after_<shot>.png` (this branch, `PHASE=after`). Shots: `viaduct_dusk`, `station_dusk`, `street_dusk`,
`viaduct_tram_at_station`, `viaduct_tram_passing`, `ride_chase`, `ride_inside`, `viaduct_dusk_potato`.

## Shot by shot

### 1. Viaduct · t1_05 (the Spire Citadel, right half; B and A)

**The frame.** A tall fluted tan spire with cyan window slits and orange trim rings stands left of centre; smaller spires
and a squat watch tower with a cyan-lit top sit at the left. The sky is deep blue-violet with a nebula band, a blue-green
planet, a banded pink gas giant and moons. Four shuttles (white hull, orange stripes, blue canopy) fly with cyan-white
engine trails and contrails. At the lower right a stone-arch viaduct (pale arches on round piers) carries a rail deck toward
the spire base; ochre mesas and green valley below, lava glowing at the right edge, a steam plume from the base. The arches and
the deck read clearly; no tram is readable in this still.

**The game before** (`test/shots/trailer_infra_before_viaduct_dusk.png`): not yet taken: the run is queued behind other agents' browser runs. What HEAD has, from `gen_citadel.js`: a 5-wide deck on 3×3 piers with round arches, glass railings and an amber lamp on every pier; no rail track on the deck, and no tram line for the viaducts.

**Ordered differences (before → after):**
1. No rail on the citadel viaducts. The generator (vgen 5) lays the deck, the piers, the arches, the glass railings and the lamps, and no track. The
   spec's deck is 5 wide: rail track plus walkway. → `gen_citadel.js` lays a rail block per deck column at vgen 6, with ramp
   shapes at the steps (`railShape`, `trackBlock`); a duplicate column keeps the later deck's height (`CIT.viaductLine`).
2. No tram on it, so no line in the rail network. → `CIT.trackLines` is a provider (`VR.RailGen.providers`, `gen_rails.js`)
   and the viaducts become tram lines (`heart-viaduct-*`). Links of the village network that would cross the citadel zone are
   rejected at vgen 6 (`RG.build`, 52 blocks around `VR.Heart.CITADEL`), so the lines do not cross there.
3. No light at dusk: the tram's headlights are emissive voxels with no glint. → `atmosphere()` in `trams.js`: two lamps per
   car, each with a glint ahead, every 0.05 s, at night (`env.night` ≥ 0.12) within 140 blocks. A cost tier of its own: the
   glints need the particles setting above 0, so Very Low (particles 0) has none.
4. No rail hum. → `g.audio.loop('hum', …)` (bandpass 180 Hz, Q 2.5, amb bus), level by the nearest car within 70 blocks.

**Checked in the test:** the night value at the dusk frame is printed as `res.night` in the run's JSON line (the glints need
≥ 0.12); `test/tram_sched.js` section (e) checks the glints and the hum on the node side.

### 2. Station · t1_01 / t1_03 (the Canyon Loop; the Gate Plaza's rail)

**The frame.** t1_03: a grey rail winds in S-curves around ochre and grey mesas at the left; a lava river (orange) cuts the lower
left, a cyan river runs below; magenta and cyan crystal clusters stand on the rock; a stone lookout tower with a red beacon sits
at the right; white steam rises from a vent; a shuttle with an orange engine glow flies above, a ringed gas giant at the top right.
t1_01: the same canyon with the shuttle close at the left and the rail loops around the mesas in the centre.

**The game before** (`test/shots/trailer_infra_before_station_dusk.png`): not yet taken (queued). What HEAD has: the Canyon Loop point list with 60 breaks (measured in node), so the tram jumps on the curves.

**Ordered differences:**
1. The Canyon Loop (the sky rail that already carries a tram) had gaps and duplicates in its point list (60 breaks), so the tram
   jumped on the curves. → `CAN.describe` (`gen_canyon.js`) is 8-connected and deduplicated: gaps are interpolated, a duplicate
   column keeps its later height.
2. Headlights at dusk (as in 1.3).
3. The station itself is not changed: the platform is the existing plaza landing; no new platform geometry in this slice.

### 4. Isles · t1_07 / t1_09 (the trestle bridges between the floating islands; C)

**The frame.** Chunky floating islands with grass tops and layered earth sides; rail tracks run on the islands and across the

**The game before:** the isle bridges (trestles with planks and posts) carry no rail and no tram.

**Ordered differences:** 1. the bridges carry no rail (→ `gen_isles.js` `bridgeTrack`: rail at deck height +1 with ramp shapes at

### 5. Ride · the player boards a tram (no frame: the spec's "shuttles and trams" beat)

**The game before:** right-click boards any car, including one that is moving; the rider is carried away with no hint, and Shift
gets off only at a station.

**Ordered differences:**
1. A moving tram can't be boarded: the hint asks to wait (`g.tram.wait`, de/en).
2. A click while riding a moving tram only says so (`g.tram.moving`).
3. Right-click at a station gets off onto the platform beside the car (`exitSpot`: lateral 2.2 / 2.6 blocks).
4. Shift while it moves asks for the roof; a second Shift within 2.5 s climbs there (`exitSpot` roof +3.3).
5. A tram removed from the network, or a dead player, ends the ride or the boarding.

**Node tests** (`test/tram_sched.js`): the timetable is deterministic; dwell 10 s at each station; the run out and back take the
same time; the two cars stay 6.5 blocks apart; on the real network (14 lines, one cycle of 217 s, sampled every second) no two
trams share a block; the boarding state machine (all five rules); the atmosphere (no glints by day; 8 glints at night for two
cars; the hum 0.064 at 20 blocks, 0.039 at 40, 0 at 90). `test/tram_net.js` checks the line network: connected, stations at
town, plinth, plaza, viaduct landing and island top; bridge supports within 2 blocks of the columns; the citadel track in the
generated chunks; no citadel track at vgen 5; the only crossing is the known one.

## Measured cost (Very Low = the `potato` preset)

Node, CPU only (no GPU; the real network of the Heartland at vgen 6: 14 lines, 3 in reach of the player, 6 cars), after a
3000-call warm-up, `Trams.update(1/30)`:
- Very Low (particles 0): 0.0018–0.0024 ms per call by day, 0.0015–0.0017 ms at night (no glints; the hum loop only).
- balanced (particles 2): 0.0016 ms by day; 0.0017 ms at night with about 4.5 glint spawns per call.

The browser numbers (`res.perfHigh`, `res.perfPotato` in the JSON line of `trailer_infra.mjs`, measured in the page with the
GPU and the other workers on the same two CPUs) are pending: the run is queued behind other agents' browser runs.
