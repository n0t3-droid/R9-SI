# F-Far · die echte Ferne (three rings) — plan (9 Oct 2026)

Branch f-far, based on v042 (F8 fade-in, soft far mask, streaming budget; the 5 km horizon; the worker pool). Every edit
to the files of other fronts is additive and kept in its own methods (merge notes at the end).

## The complaint and the goal

The owner, verbatim: „die Welt weit sichtbar ja und das mit LOD aber selbst wenn ich alles auf max stelle sehe ich die
Welt-Ferne nicht, die sind nur als LOD sichtbar". Today the real world ends at renderDist (16 chunks ≈ 256 m on Ultra,
14 on High); beyond it only the flat horizon tiles stand. The horizon tiles' top faces are all horizontal: every slope
gets the same sunlight, so hills read as a smeared texture. Goal: on High and Ultra the land out to ~1 km is made of
real shapes — hills with lit and shaded sides, the city's masses, the citadel's spires, the canyon's mesas — while
Very Low stays exactly as it is.

## Design: three rings

| ring | what | where | built by | drawn by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | the chunks (full detail) | renderDist (High 14 / Ultra 16; slider up to 24 on High, 32 on Ultra) | worker gen + mesh (as today) | terrain programs (as today) |
| 1 | „Formen": coarse meshes | from the edge of ring 0 out to 64 chunks (1 km) | worker job `form` (VR.Far.form) | LOD program with `FORM` |
| 2 | the horizon tiles | beyond (to 5 km) | worker job `lod` (as today) | LOD program (`FAR1` while ring 1 is on) |

Interpretation of the slider rule: ring 0's top is the preset's `rdMax` — 24 on Very Low, Low, Balanced and High
(unchanged), 32 on Ultra. Ring 1's radius is the preset's `far` — 0 on Very Low, Low and Balanced, 64 on High and Ultra.

### Ring 1, the forms (src/shared/far.js, worker side)
- A form tile is 4 × 4 chunk columns (64 × 64 blocks). Cells: 2 × 2 blocks for tiles within 32 chunks of the camera,
  4 × 4 beyond (both ≈ 4 px a cell at 1280 × 720).
- Terrain: a smooth height field from the generator's cheap pass (`g.surfaceY` at the cell corners: the climate corners and
  the density columns, no chunk), coloured with the horizon's own surface look (lod.js, factored out unchanged: `LOD.look`)
  — so ring 1 and ring 2 share colours. Its normal comes from the geometry in the shader (face 6): slopes toward the sun
  are lit, slopes away are shaded. A vertex shade darkens hollows (a cheap ambient occlusion from the neighbours).
- Water, lava and aether as flat planes (greedy-merged), the bed under deep water is not drawn.
- Shapes: trees as crowns (the biome's cover on a 4-block grid, as the near horizon tiles); buildings and set pieces as
  slabs per cell from their block ops — the village and town plans (Nova Haven), the Gate Temple, the Pod Hills, the
  Mountain Station, the canyon's sky rail and towers, the Sky Isles' roads and bridges — top colour = the top block,
  side colour = the commonest block of the cell; citadels from `CitadelGen.lodBoxes` (the spires), floating islands from
  `islandAt`. Each structure's skyline is computed once per worker and cached.
- Skirts hang 12 blocks from the tile border (no cracks between 2- and 4-block tiles, ring 1 and ring 2).
- Vertex: the horizon's 12-byte layout (int16 xyz in 1/8 block, face|kind, aux, rgb + shade); indices 16 bit.

### Ring bookkeeping (VR.Far.RingSet, pure, node-tested)
- `ringOf(d, rd, r1)`: d = column distance in chunks (centre to the camera's column) → 0 if d ≤ rd + 0.5 (exactly the
  renderer's visible radius), 1 if d ≤ r1 + 0.5, else 2.
- A tile is wanted when it has a column at d ≥ rd − 2 (its chunks may leave ring 0 any moment: the form is there before
  the chunk goes) and a column at d ≤ r1 + 0.5; it is kept (hysteresis) while d ≥ rd − 4 and ≤ r1 + 2.5; else freed.
  The band is rescanned only when the camera's column, rd or r1 changes; the scan reuses its arrays (no per-frame
  allocation); requests go nearest first, a few in flight per worker.
- No column is drawn by two rings: the far mask (RG8, 136 × 136 columns around the camera while ring 1 is on) holds per
  column R = the chunk's fade (ring 0, as F8) and G = the form tile's fade. Ring 1 draws a pixel iff coverR ≤ t < G,
  ring 2 iff t ≥ max(coverR, coverG), ring 0 iff t < R (t = the 4x4 dither threshold, cover = min(bilinear, nearest)).
  Every t is drawn by one ring at least; in a settled column (fades 0 or 1) by exactly one.
- Promotion (ring 1 → 0): the chunk's 300 ms fade-in dithers over the form; the tile is freed once all its columns are
  well inside ring 0. Demotion (0 → 1): the form is already there (wanted from rd − 2), the chunk's column leaves the mask.

### Streaming and memory
- Lanes: ring-1 jobs ride the far lane with the horizon tiles, after the coarse tiles (priority 0..1 vs ring 2's −2.4…),
  so ring 2 comes at once, ring 1 within seconds, ring 0 as today; none waits for another.
- Uploads: ring-1 meshes (and ring-2 tiles while ring 1 is on) go through the world's upload queue (F8 budget); a form
  fades in over 300 ms (G), so nothing pops.
- While ring 1 is on, the horizon quadtree stops at level 2 (512-block tiles): ring 1 replaces the 4- and 8-block tiles
  (and their 1-block villages) inside 1 km, which frees their memory and worker time.
- Budget: ring 1 + ring 2 share the horizon's byte cap of the level (`capB`, 192 MB on the 5 km horizon that High and
  Ultra set today); ring 1 stops asking when ring 1 + ring 2 reach it and evicts its farthest tiles first.

### Governor and presets
- `PRESETS.far` (0, 0, 0, 64, 64) and `PRESETS.rdMax` (24, 24, 24, 24, 32); saved settings without them follow their
  preset (custom: ring 1 when renderDist ≥ 14). The governor's ladders touch neither rd, nor the horizon, nor ring 1.
- The render-distance tip explains the rings (German first).

## Tests first
- test/lod_same.js: the horizon tiles' bytes are unchanged by the refactor (hash of several tiles, recorded before).
- test/far_rings.js: ringOf at distances; band want/keep; promotion and demotion when the camera moves; no column in two
  rings (the mask rule for every threshold); no allocation in a steady update; budget arithmetic (bytes, cap, eviction).
- test/far_forms.js: a flat column → a flat field; a house lot → a slab with its roof colour; a tree → a crown; a mesa →
  steep sides; determinism (same bytes twice); cost per chunk column vs a full chunk (≤ 1/10 of generation time) and
  triangles (≤ 1/8); bytes per tile and the whole ring against the budget.
- test/far.mjs (browser): High before (v042 dist) and after, pictures far_high_{before,after}.png, ring-1 columns, time to
  the first ring-1 frame, bytes per ring, frame ms; Very Low: no ring 1, frame ms within ±5 % of before.
