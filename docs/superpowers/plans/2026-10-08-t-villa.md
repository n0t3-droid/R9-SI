# T-villa: THE house from the trailer — Villa Nova (plan, 8 Oct 2026)

Branch t-villa, worktree /home/claude/vr/.worktrees/t-villa, base v042. The owner: "you have an editor and cannot build
houses like in the trailer? that is a joke". The deliverable is ONE villa that a viewer recognises as the house of
t1_26 / t3_05 / t2_23, and ONE white interior (t1_29, t3_10, t2_25, t2_28, t1_30), as a catalogue house of the villa
catalogue (gen_plan.js family `trailer`, skin `nova`) that the blueprint path (🖐 Bauhand, /haus, the Studio's house list)
places like every other house, and that the city generator (t-city) can call instead of its towers.

## 1 · The frames, read as a builder

### Outside (t1_26, t3_05, t2_23, t1_24, t3_06)

- **Masses.** Three storeys, stepped: a wide ground floor, a first floor set back two to three blocks at the front and
  the sides (the ground floor's roof is a terrace), a small top floor set back again (roof garden around it). Every
  level ends in a thick flat slab (about one and a half blocks of fascia), the terrace slabs cantilever past the walls
  below (t2_23: the ground-floor slab projects over the bays).
- **Box bays.** The walls are not flat: rectangular boxes stand out of every facade (half a block to a block), each with
  ONE tall window (t1_26: a 2 × 3 window in the left box, a narrow 1 × 2.5 one in the next). Between the boxes the wall
  steps back and carries horizontal glass bands (t3_05 left: long low windows).
- **Windows.** Turquoise / cyan glass that glows from inside (it reads like a lit screen, not like clear glass), set in a
  THICK glowing orange frame (about a fifth of the window's width), with a dark grey reveal between frame and glass.
  The entrance (t3_05, centre) is a tall narrow glass door in the same orange frame.
- **Stone.** Grey stone (light grey in the light, mid grey in shadow) with fine horizontal joints, large clean blocks; the
  slab fascias are a shade lighter than the walls.
- **Light lines.** Orange light lines at the base of the boxes and under every overhang (t2_23: the upper boxes glow
  orange underneath), the glass glows turquoise. (The coordinator's brief adds cyan lines along the slab edges; the
  frames show cyan mostly on the paths — both are built: cyan on the slab fascias, orange under the overhangs.)
- **Balustrades.** Classic white-grey balusters with a rail on every terrace edge and on the roof.
- **Roof garden.** A big round clipped bush on the roof (t2_23, t1_24), planters, a small roof structure.
- **Garden.** Lawn, low hedges, round topiary bushes (t1_26: a light-green ball by the entrance, t3_05: a round tree on the
  lawn), flowers in purple and white, fan palms and cycads low, tall palms with real fronds, a curved light-grey path
  with cyan light lines along both edges, planters.

### Inside (t1_29, t1_30, t3_10, t2_28, t1_27, t2_25)

- **White gloss floor** with reflections, white walls, a flat white ceiling with round downlights in a grid and a
  rectangular cove (a recessed rectangle with a glowing cyan-white outline) in the middle; a curved white soffit with a
  cyan LED line on its lower edge (t1_29 right).
- **Glass wall** to the garden, floor to ceiling, thin vertical mullions every three to four metres, the garden (lawn,
  palms, hills) behind it.
- **Two floating glass stairs**, one left, one right, rising from near the glass wall toward the camera into the ceiling:
  glass treads with glowing cyan front edges, a grey spine, glass balustrades with a thin rail.
- **The pad**: a white plinth (about 3 × 1 × 3 m) with a recessed glowing blue top showing a round ring emblem; white
  blocks (benches) beside it; cyan light at the floor around the white pieces (t1_29, t1_30).
- **Glass partitions** with glass doors between the entrance and the hall (t3_10, t1_27), white round columns, plants.

## 4 · The plan

**Blocks (prefer existing):** walls `vollton_light_grey` (clean grey stone; the Klassisch outline gives every block its
joint), fascias `vollton_white` with `vx_tech_white_strip` (white fascia with a cyan line), terraces `vx_balustrade`,
orange lines `vx_led_cove` (under an overhang edge) and `vx_led_strip` (at a box's foot), interior floor
`vollton_white_gloss`, garden glass `panorama_white` with `vx_beam_white` mullions, spots `vx_spot`, cove ring
`vx_tech_white_strip`, path `vx_floor_plaza` + `vx_floor_plaza_line` (the line block curves round its corners by itself),
hedges `vx_hedge`, round bushes `vm_bush_blossom` / `vm_tree_round_s`, palms `VR.Plants.palm` (vx_palm_trunk +
vm_palm_crown), planters `vx_tech_planter` / `vm_planter`, plants `vm_plant_palm_concrete`, sofas `vx_sofa_white`, doors
`door_glass_white`, the portal `vx_portal_frame_white` where it helps.

**New blocks (late, appended — none of the existing ones serves), file `src/shared/voxnova.js`:**
- `vx_nova_window` — a 16³ VX piece of kind `novawin` that JOINS its neighbours in both looks: any rectangle of these
  blocks is one window with ONE thick glowing orange frame (3/16) at its outer edge, a dark reveal and glowing turquoise
  glass — so a 2 × 3 bay window, a 4 × 1 band and the glazed screen around the entrance door are the same block (the frame
  also closes against a door). Smooth (res 16) surfaces, no voxel tile.
- `vm_nova_tread` — VX32, 2 × 1 × 1: two glass treads per cell (rise ½), glowing cyan front edges, a grey spine.
  (Built as 2 × 3 × 1 in the end: the spine read as floating grey cubes, the frames show glass cheeks — the two rows over
  the treads carry a slanted glass pane with a steel handrail on both sides, and the panes of the pieces join into one
  straight stringer; only the tread row glows.)
- `vm_nova_pad` — VX32, 3 × 1 × 3: a white rounded plinth with a recessed glowing blue top, a cyan ring emblem and a cyan
  light line round its foot.
The colours reuse existing VX colours, so no new texture name is registered (mesh_same keeps its hashes).

**The house (`src/shared/gen_nova.js`, `PLAN.buildNova`), lot 25 × 25 (u across the street front, v into the lot):**
- Ground floor u 2–22 × v 6–20 (21 × 15), 5 blocks clear; slab zone 2 blocks (fascia y 6–7) cantilevering 2 blocks over the
  front bays; first floor u 4–20 × v 9–20 (set back 3 front, 2 sides), 4 clear; slab y 12–13; top floor u 7–17 × v 13–20,
  roof y 18–19, balustrades on every terrace and on the roof, a roof garden (lawn, round tree, planters) around the top
  floor, a white dome on the roof.
- Front: two box bays (protruding 1) with 2 × 3 nova windows, an entrance screen (1 × 4 sidelights + transom around a
  glass door) in the middle, band windows; first-floor bays with orange underglow; cyan strip on every fascia, orange
  coves under every overhang edge, orange strips at the foot of every bay.
- Inside: the white hall (floor `vollton_white_gloss`, ceiling with spots and a cove ring), the glass wall to the back
  garden (single storey, mullions every 4), the pad at the centre with a cyan floor ring and white benches, the two floating
  stairs along the side walls rising from the glass toward the front into openings in the ceiling, a glass partition with
  a door between the foyer and the hall, plants; upstairs a lounge and a bedroom with terrace doors; the top floor a studio.
  (Changed while building: in t1_29 / t1_30 the stairs are seen ACROSS the glass wall, against the garden — they now
  stand two blocks deep in front of the glass wall and climb from the side walls toward the middle, a V; the pad and the
  cove sit in front of them, the garden door in the middle of the glass wall; a third glass stair leads from the gallery
  to the top floor.)
- Garden: lawn, hedge line along the street with the path's gap, the curved path (plaza + cyan lines both sides) from the
  street to the entrance, two round bushes by the entrance, a round tree on the lawn, planters, palms (front corners and the
  back garden), flowers and ferns in beds along the facade, a paved strip in front of the glass wall.

## 6 · For the city generator (t-city merges it later)

- **Catalogue entry**: `VR.VillageGen.plan2.CATALOGUE[PLAN.TRAILER]` — `PLAN.TRAILER === 11`, name `'Villa Nova'`,
  family `'trailer'`, the only skin `'nova'`, `special: true`, its own builder (`build: PLAN.buildNova`, gen_nova.js).
  The blueprint item is `blueprint_11`. `PLAN.pick` never hands it to a town lot (only to a lot of exactly its size).
- **Lot**: `PLAN.NOVA.LOT` = `[25, 31]` (u across the street front 0…24, v into the lot 0…30). The house stands at
  u 2–22, v 6–20; the roof garden's tree tops out at F + 19, the roof balustrade at F + 20 (`info.roofTop` = F + 22).
- **Street side**: v = 0 (the lot's −v edge); the path leaves the street at u 5–8 and the entrance door is at
  u = `PLAN.NOVA.ENTRY` (12), v 5. With `VG.makeLot(put, ox, oz, r)` the lot turns like every catalogue lot (r = 0:
  the street on the −z side; facings and axes turn with it).
- **Call**: `PLAN.build(L, { w: 25, d: 31, F, r, ox, oz, kind: 'home' }, { index: PLAN.TRAILER, rng, ground: (u, v) => y,
  beds: 3 })` (or `yield* PLAN.buildSteps(...)` inside a generator) → the villa builder's info record (door, outside,
  inside, beds, seats, lights, roofTop, plan). The builder levels the lot itself: lawn at F − 1, earth down to
  `ground(u, v)`, air above up to F + 24.
