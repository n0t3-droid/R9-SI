# t-look: the game's picture against the trailer frames

Branch `t-look` (base b702a76, v041). Measured with `test/look_stats.js` (the frames and the shots, 640 × 348, the same
numbers) and `test/trailer_look.mjs` (one browser session: before, sweep, after). The grade's knobs live in `VR.Grade`
(`src/main/env.js`); the defaults are the trailer look. Status: the look test ends with FAILED 5 (listed under before and after, and in the gaps). The grade is shipped as measured; the bands were not loosened to pass.

## The ordered differences

Largest first. Each line names the shots it affects; numbers are the game before (High unless noted).

1. **Brightness.** All four High shots are over their band: vista L 159 and citadel L 143 against the sky kind's
   89-121, meadow L 172 and meadowlow L 158 against the lawn kind's 110-129. On Very Low the sky shots are too bright
   too (vista 158, citadel 140), the lawns are in band.
2. **Washed-out sky on the sky shots.** vista S 0.27 and citadel S 0.29 against 0.37-0.49; S90 0.48 and 0.43 against
   0.66-0.85. The frames' violet-blue sky is far more saturated than the game's.
3. **Warm sky on the lawn shots.** meadow skyRB +9.5 (High), +15.6 (meadowlow High) and citadel +6.0 against the
   frames' -52..-25; meadow skyS 0.16-0.20 against 0.33-0.45. The game's horizon is orange-white where the trailer's
   is lilac.
4. **Too little contrast.** Spread vista 118 (frames 171-196), meadow 104 and meadowlow 114 (lawn 140-170).
5. **No highlights at all.** White 0.0% on every High shot; the sky frames carry 0.6-1.9% (stars, the planet's lit rim,
   the sun glow).
6. **Very Low lawns over-saturated.** meadow S 0.47 and meadowlow S 0.52 against 0.29-0.41; ground gndS 0.68 against
   0.23-0.43. The lite terrain paints the lawn a flat green, which no grade can bring into band (see the gaps).

## The plan (what changed, where)

The grade is one object, `VR.Grade` (`src/main/env.js`). Its defaults are the look; `palette: 0` restores the v0.41 day sky
(the sweep's palette0 row); the test's before is the v0.41 build itself. Values below: the v0.41 value (as it was in `b702a76`) and the t-look default.

| knob | v0.41 | t-look | what it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| palette | 0 (old constants) | 1 | violet-blue day sky, lilac horizon, violet fog veil (env.js) |
| expo | 0.94 (all times) | 0.75 day; night keeps 0.94 | exposure of the composite |
| sat | 1.17 | 1.12 | saturation after the luma mix (rain still -0.2) |
| contrast | none | 0.3 | S-curve in the composite, after the clamp (shaders.js `u_contrast`) |
| vignette | 0.55 | 0.3 | corner darkening (renderer.js reads `E.vignette`) |
| fogDens | 0.00042 | 0.00016 | aerial haze over the far land |
| nebula | 1 | 0.55 | the nebula band in the sky (`u_nebula`) |
| sunDisc | 16 | 8 | the sun's disc in HDR (`u_sunDisc`) |
| bloomThr / bloomStr | 0.95 / 0.075 | 1.15 / 0.10 | glow of emissives; night still adds 0.04 to the strength |
| rays | 0.55 | 0.3 | god-ray strength (renderer.js reads `E.rays`) |
| sunWarm | 1 | 0.5 | how orange the low sun is (its light on the lawns) |
| sunsetK | 1 | 0.3 | golden-hour glow of the horizon and the warm cast of the sky |
| skyK, sunK, gridK | 1, 1, 0.12 | 1, 1, 0.12 | sky brightness, sun colour, Klassisch block-edge grid (kept: terrain) |

Where: `env.js` (GRADE, the violet palette, the golden-hour and warm-sun terms, the exposure, saturation, bloom and
the E fields), `shaders.js` (uniforms `u_nebula`, `u_sunDisc`, `u_gridK`; the contrast S-curve in the composite),
`renderer.js` (the uniforms from `E`, the rays and vignette from `E`, `u_contrast`). Very Low runs the same
composite, so it gets the same grade; its extra cost is the clamp and the S-curve, no pass.

## Known gaps

- **Very Low lawns (the main gap).** The lite terrain paints the lawn a flat pure green. Its lowest channel is at 0, so
  S is 1 on those pixels: S90 1.00 (ceiling 0.83), gndS 0.93 (ceiling 0.57), and meadowlow Very Low L 89.5 (floor 94.3).
  No global grade fixes this without a Very-Low-only desaturation, which would break "the same grade". The fix is the
  lite terrain's colour. That is terrain work, which this branch leaves alone.
- **High meadows with the sun in view.** White 2.0-2.6% against a ceiling of 1.6%; skyS 0.22-0.23 against a floor of
  0.233. The sun disc at 8 is the least that keeps the vista's sun; 16 scored better in the sweep but adds sun in view.
- **meadowlow High lawn saturation.** gndS 0.62 against 0.57. Lowering sat for the lawns would cost the vista (sat 1.12
  was the vista's pick).
- **Vista High spread** 145 against a floor of 146.5, which is 1.5 short.
- **The hint toast** "B = build hand" appears in the shots. `#hint` is outside `#hud`; hiding it in `shot()` did not
  work. It is a screenshot artefact only; the statistics were taken from the same pictures, so it may count.
- **Not measured:** night, rain and the other weather; the frames are day and dusk only, and the night exposure keeps
  0.94 as in v0.41.
- **Not run:** the full browser regression and the classic_v2 grid dips (8% or more). The coordinator runs those on the
  merged branch, together with the other fronts.
- **Merge notes:** `env.js`, `shaders.js` and `renderer.js` are shared with the other fronts; the look test expects the
  defaults in `env.js`.

Node regression on the final defaults: 53 tests, 1 failed under load (`modern_pack.js`); run alone it passes (ALL OK).
`looks_core.js` flaked the same way earlier (a timing budget under load) and passed alone.
