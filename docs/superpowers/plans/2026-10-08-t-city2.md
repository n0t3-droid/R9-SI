# Palm City Nova Haven (t-city2) — trailer shot F: the city itself

> **For agentic workers:** the pictures come from `test/trailer_city.mjs` (a showcase: before/after pairs, the Very Low
> cost; `regress: skip`); the numbers from `test/trailer_city.js` (node, the gate, ALL OK). Everything new is vgen 8
> (`g.vgen >= 8`): worlds made before keep their chunks (vgen ≤ 7; `test/gen_same.js` keeps its reference, and
> `test/trailer_city.js` pins the vgen 6/7 home-city plans and the vgen 7 chunks of the city and the hills).

**Goal:** the trailer's opening street. Behind the Gate Temple's back door the game shows the city of the frames:
Villa Nova lots along gently curved roads with continuous cyan light lines on both kerbs and on every path to a door,
villas that vary (one design language, five compositions and their mirror images), lawns, hedges, topiary balls,
flower beds, tall palms along the roads, lamps with cyan heads, and on the hill behind the city terraced green fields
with villas on every second terrace — at dusk with stars and the ringed giant.

## The game before (v042, vgen 7)

`gen_heart.js` fixes the home city at (0, −140), radius 100; `gen_village.js` plans it like every tech village: a
square plaza at the centre, six radial roads (the first straight to the temple's back door), tech towers (white panel
boxes, two to four storeys, window bands) on small lots, palms every nine blocks. The temple's hex corridor (z −41 …
−49) opens onto the end of the straight road. The hills behind are smooth, with pod houses and ribbon paths.
(Pictures: `test/shots/trailer_city_<shot>_before.png` — filled in from run 1 below.)

## Differences, ordered by how much they change the impression

1. **The houses**: tech towers on small lots against big stepped villas with box bays, glass bands, slab light lines
   and roof gardens with the big tree — the frames' architecture is Villa Nova's, and it varies.
2. **The street**: a straight radial lane against a gently curving road that sweeps across the view, cyan lines on
   both kerbs, raised kerbs, planted verges, paths with lines to every door.
3. **The hills**: smooth slopes against terraced fields with contour light lines, stairs and villas.
4. **The planting between the lots**: hedges, clipped balls, flower beds, palms in rows along the roads.
5. **The light**: dusk with stars and the giant (the frames' time — the test shoots at 13000 = 19:00 and at noon).

## Design and what was built

### Where Nova Haven is, and the hand-over
The home site keeps its place (0, −140); at vgen 8 its radius is 130 (`gen_heart.js`, one expression). `planSteps`
hands a vgen-8 home city to `VG.novaHaven` (`gen_village.js`, one line); every older world plans as before.

### The family (`gen_nova.js`, `PLAN.NOVA_FAMILY`)
One language — grey stone storeys stepping back, slab edges with the cyan line and the orange underglow, box bays each
with one big turquoise window in a thick orange frame, glass bands with lintels, white balusters, a roof garden with
the big round tree — in five compositions from parameters (storey rectangles, an L of rectangles, overhangs, bays,
bands, the entry or a glass front, the hall's pad and floating stairs, the roof garden): `classic` (t-villa's Villa
Nova, built as before), `long` (t1_22: wide two-storey, the long glass band, the roof garden on the first floor),
`tower` (t1_24: three tiers stacked at one side), `corner` (t2_23: an L with a box bay wrapping the wing's corner),
`bungalow` (t2_25 / t3_06: one storey with a glass front and a pavilion box on the roof). `o.novaMirror` builds the
mirror image (blocks turned 1↔3, hinges swapped, every model laid at its mirrored footprint). The info record carries
the house rectangle, the bays, the slab edges and where the garden path meets the street.
One fix to the classic: its palm crowns were laid by part 0, two blocks into the crown's grid, so every crown hung two
blocks off its trunk; they stand centred now (the classic's reference hashes moved for that alone;
`test/trailer_villa.js` looks for the trunk under the crown's middle).

### The city (`gen_city.js`, `VG.novaHaven`)
- **The arrival plaza** at the corridor's city end (a half disc, r 10.5): the corridor's axis kept clear, the holo
  table with its cyan ring and benches on the east side, two palms in planters on the west, lamps, the bell.
- **The crescent**: an arc of radius 211 whose apex touches the plaza — the road that sweeps across the view from the
  corridor. **Palm Row**: the same arc 90 blocks north (the villas of both stand back to back). **The avenue**: a gentle
  S from the plaza across both to the hill foot. No straight run longer than 24 blocks (the least turn in any 24
  blocks: 6.5°).
- **Roads**: 7 wide, light grey (`vx_floor_plaza`), a cyan light line (`vx_floor_plaza_line`) on both kerbs — the edge
  of the paved area, its diagonal steps joined so the line piece draws one unbroken curve — a raised grey kerb
  (`vollton_mid_grey_slab`), planted verges.
- **Lots**: Villa Nova lots (25 × 31) on the level land along the roads, oriented to the road, laid out from each
  arc's apex outward (so the first lots hug the avenue): twelve villas. The frames' houses at the corridor's view (the
  long villa, the corner villa, the tower house, the bungalow), the rest by the seed, never twice in a row, half of
  them mirrored.
- **Paths**: every lot's garden path continues straight to the kerb: cyan lines on both edges joined to the kerb line,
  an amber line across the mouth (`vx_floor_plaza_line_amber`, the orange accent at the junction).
- **Verges**: palms every 6–9 blocks on both sides (seeded, 8–12 tall), tech lamps with cyan heads every 12, flower
  clusters along the kerb, clipped balls and blossom bushes, tufts in the lawns.
- The plan record is the villages': homes (door, beds, seats, lights), the temple base as work, the plaza, lamps,
  roads, chunks; villagers, the far world and the spawn read it as before. A lot's first clearing of a cell becomes one
  fill per column (a twentieth of the ops).

### The terraces (`gen_hills.js`, vgen 8)
The hill face north of the city (x ±170, z −150 … −285) is cut into terraces: each column's natural height lowered to
the level below it (levels 82, 86, 91, 95 … — risers of 4 and 5), grass treads, moss risers (green from the side), a
cyan light line along every terrace edge, small round trees on the treads; a Villa Nova of the family on every second
terrace where the hill is gentle enough (its lot cut level into the slope, up to 13 blocks; the moss runs 14 deep so
the cut is green); three stairways from the hill foot over every riser, paved across the treads with light lines on
both sides. The cut leaves every cell the city laid out (`plan.nova.owns`); the face's edges blend into the hills; the
old pods and ribbon paths keep out of it.
