## The differences after run 1, ordered by how much they change the impression — and what was done

1. **The first pictures themselves** (F1, F2): two harness faults, not the game's. The settling steps draw nothing, so
   (a) the detailed VX pieces (crowns, furniture) waited for their per-frame upload and the averaged boxes stood in,
   and (b) the streaming queue and the columns' 300 ms fade-in were not finished, so ring 1's far forms (dark boxes)
   still stood in for the near columns. `trailer_city.mjs`: every section within the detail distance gets its pieces
   before a picture, and "settled" now means no upload waiting and no column fading in. The villagers within 12 blocks
   of the camera are left out (at dusk they gather round the player on the plaza and wave into the lens).
2. **Rows of warm blobs along every kerb at dusk**: f-light's lamp halos (F6) took every emissive cell near the eye,
   and the kerb light lines took all 48. `particles.js`: halos for point lights only (no light lines, strips, slab
   edges, Villa Nova pieces, the temple's slits, recessed ceiling spots); a cool light (the tech lamps' cyan heads) in
   its own colour; warm lamps exactly as in v0.42.
3. **The avenue read straight** (a ±3-block wobble on a 7-wide road): it now forks — t3_05's and t2_23's Y — into two
   branches that curve round a planted island to Palm Row (each turns 81°, 43 blocks apart at the widest); a hill
   road continues from Palm Row to the hills.
4. **The terrace face read as a stepped wall**: round hills — ten cosine-bell domes in the Heartland's own terrain at
   vgen 8, the terrace cut runs round each in rings, a stairway up every hill's south face, villas on the hills.
5. **The kerbs a dark navy wall at dusk**: light grey (the frames' #D2D9E1).
6. **The verges flat**: the fan palms stand in raised beds (dark soil in a light grey kerb), and the island has a
   raised bed at its tip with the round clipped tree (t3_05) and two fan-palm beds.
7. **The palms short** (8–12): 10–14, over the villas' first floor (t1_22: 12–16).
8. **The dusk framing**: 13000 (19:00) keeps the set; F1 and F3 also at 13700 (the blue hour: stars, lit lines).

## Design and what was built

### Where Nova Haven is, and the hand-over
The home site keeps its place (0, −140); at vgen 8 its radius is 130 (`gen_heart.js`, one expression). `planSteps`
hands a vgen-8 home city to `VG.novaHaven` (`gen_village.js`, one line); every older world plans as before.

### The family (`gen_nova.js`, `PLAN.NOVA_FAMILY`)
One language — grey stone storeys stepping back, slab edges with the cyan line and the orange underglow, box bays each
with one big turquoise window in a thick orange frame, glass bands with lintels, white balusters, a roof garden with
the big round tree — in five compositions from parameters: `classic` (t-villa's Villa Nova, built as before), `long`
(t1_22), `tower` (t1_24), `corner` (t2_23), `bungalow` (t2_25 / t3_06). `o.novaMirror` builds the mirror image. The
info record carries the house rectangle, the bays, the slab edges and where the garden path meets the street. One fix
to the classic: its palm crowns hung two blocks off their trunks; they stand centred now.

### The city (`gen_city.js`, `VG.novaHaven`)
- **The arrival plaza** at the corridor's city end (a half disc, r 10.5): the corridor's axis clear, the holo table with
  its cyan ring and benches on the east side, two palms in planters on the west, lamps, the bell.
- **The roads** (`NH.ROADS`): the crescent (an arc of radius 211 whose apex touches the plaza), Palm Row (the same arc
  90 blocks north), the avenue from the plaza across the crescent between the frames' villas to the fork, the **west
  and east branches** (Catmull-Rom, from the avenue's end round the island to Palm Row), the hill road from Palm Row to
  the hills. 7 wide, light grey, a cyan light line on both kerbs (the edge of the paved area, its diagonal steps
  joined, so the line piece draws one unbroken curve, round the island too), a raised **light grey** kerb.
- **The island** (`NH.ISLAND`): the lawn between the branches, reserved from the lots, then planted like the verges;
  at its tip — kept free of palms so the fork shows the bed — a raised bed (an ellipse 7 × 11: a light grey kerb one
  block high round podzol, ferns and flower clusters, the small round tree in the middle); two small raised beds with a
  fan palm further along.
- **Lots**: Villa Nova lots (25 × 31) on the level land along the crescent and Palm Row, oriented to the road, laid out
  from each arc's apex outward: eleven villas; the frames' four at the corridor's view (the long villa, the corner
  villa, the tower house, the bungalow), the rest dealt from the family shuffled by the seed, half mirrored.
- **Paths**: every lot's garden path to the kerb with cyan edge lines joined to the kerb line, amber across the mouth.
- **Verges**: tall palms every 6–9 blocks on both sides (10–14 tall), tech lamps with cyan heads every 12; a planted
  strip along the kerb (ferns, flower clusters, clipped balls), blossom bushes behind; in every second gap between the
  tall palms a fan palm (a crown on a trunk of two or three) **in a raised bed**; small round trees on the wider lawns.
  Every trunk and every crown's anchor is laid last.

### The terraces (`gen_hills.js`, vgen 8)
- **The round hills**: `HILLS.domesT8` — ten domes on a jittered grid of the face (x ±170, z −150 … −285), radius 25–32,
  16–23 blocks high (a cosine bell, the highest counting where two meet), faded in from the city's edge and at the
  face's sides — are added to `HEART.design`'s height (a wrapper, vgen 8 only), so the generator, `surfaceY`, the far
  forms and the horizon all see the same hills.
- **The cut** (unchanged in kind): each column lowered to the terrace level below it (levels 82, 86, 91, 95 … — risers
  of 4 and 5), green treads in rows along the hill's own contours (grass and a darker moss row by turns), moss risers,
  a cyan light line along every terrace edge, small round trees on the treads; the cut leaves every cell the city laid
  out (`plan.nova.owns`).
- **Villas** of the family on the even levels where the slope allows (eight now, on levels 2, 4, 6, 8), their lot fill
  in moss (green from the side) where the lot stands out over the slope.
- **Stairways**: up the south face of every hill, wide flights over every riser to its top, on the lower tread or —
  where that is too narrow — cut into the upper one; paved across the treads with light lines on both sides.

### The halos (`particles.js`)
`Particles.haloStates()` (a Uint8Array by state, read from the registry once) and `Particles.haloColor(s, out)`;
`HALO_AREA` names the area lights. No allocation per frame (the colour array is reused).
