## Design

### Where Nova Haven is
`gen_heart.js`: the home city site is fixed at (0, −140), radius 100 (`HEART.CITY`), the Gate Temple at (0, −10) with
its back door at z −40 (`HEART.TEMPLE.back`); the 7-wide hex corridor runs from the back door north to its city end
at z −49 (a veil in an octagon of orange light bars, `gen_temple.js`). The Pod Hills rise north of the city (z −205 …
−430, `gen_hills.js`). The city is planned by `gen_village.js` (`planSteps`, style `tech`, `HOME`): a plaza at the site
centre, six radial roads (the first straight to the temple), tech towers on small lots.

### vgen 8: the city as a designed composition (`gen_city.js`, `VG.novaHaven`)
`planSteps` hands a home city of a vgen-8 world to `VG.novaHaven` (one line); every older world plans as before.
The planner returns the same plan record (homes, work, plaza, lamps, roads, chunks …), so villagers, the far world and
the spawn keep working.

- **The arrival plaza** at the corridor's city end: a paved disc (r 10) with the cyan rings, lamps, benches, planters.
- **The ring road** round the hero lot: an oval (centre (0, −92)) whose south arc passes the plaza; the hero villa
  (the long two-storey villa with the big tree, on the temple's axis) stands inside it, its garden path running
  across the ring road's verge to the plaza.
- **The avenue** leaves the ring at its north point and winds north in S-bends to the city's north edge (no straight
  run longer than 24 blocks), where it meets the hill stair.
- **Roads**: 7 wide, light-grey paving; the cells at both kerbs are cyan light lines (`vx_floor_plaza_line`), a grey
  kerb row, then the verge. Paths from the kerb to every door: 5 wide, lines on both edges joined to the kerb line at
  T-junctions, an amber threshold (`vx_floor_plaza_line_amber`) across every path mouth (the orange accent).
- **Lots**: Villa Nova lots (25 × 31) on both sides of every road, oriented to the road, the composition drawn from the
  family by seed (the hero lots near the plaza are fixed: the long villa on the axis, the corner villa and the tower
  house on the west side).
- **Between the lots**: lawn verges with box hedges, round topiary balls, flower beds, tall palms along the road every
  6–9 blocks (seeded), tech lamps with cyan heads.

### The Villa Nova family (`gen_nova.js`)
One language (grey stone storeys, slab edges with the cyan line and the orange underglow, box bays each with one big
turquoise window in a thick orange frame, glass bands, white balusters, a roof garden with the big round tree), five
compositions from parameters (tiers, box bays, band lengths), each also mirrored:
`classic` (the Villa Nova of t-villa), `long` (wide two-storey, long glass band), `tower` (three tiers, narrower and
taller), `corner` (L-shaped with a corner box bay), `bungalow` (one storey with a glass front and a roof garden).

### The terraces (`gen_hills.js`, vgen 8)
The hill face north of the city is quantised into terraces 4–5 blocks high: grass on the treads, green (moss) risers,
a cyan light line along every terrace edge, crop rows on the narrow treads, a Villa Nova variant on every second
terrace, a stair from each tread to the next.
