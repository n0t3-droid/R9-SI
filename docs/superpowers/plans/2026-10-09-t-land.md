# t-land — the land between (vgen 10)

The owner, playing: „die Welt ist langweilig … wie man sie mehr in eine schöne Umgebung macht, ohne Combat“.
The landmarks hold (Nova Haven, the villas, the Gate Temple, the hall city, the lava canyon); everything between them
is one empty green plain. This front fills the Heartland's land between the sites with a landscape: paths that
connect, woods and groves, farms with fields, a river with a waterfall, a lake with beaches, a rocky ridge with a
lookout, and animals at their places — procedural, ours, deterministic from the seed, for NEW worlds only (vgen 10).

The coordinator's reading holds: the emptiness between the sites is the boredom. The game's generator explains it:
the Heartland's `sky_meadow` biome has no entry in TREE_DENS or FLORA (gen_overworld.js), so its land gets no tree,
no grass tuft and no flower; HEART.design keeps it at y 80–85 everywhere (82 + 5·n1); the horizon (lod.js TREES, far.js)
draws no canopy there either.

## 2. Design — VR.Land (src/shared/gen_land.js, after gen_station; vgen ≥ 10)

* **Versioning.** Everything is gated on `g.vgen >= 10` (LAND.VGEN). New worlds get vgen 10 (game.js createWorld). A
  vgen-9 world keeps every chunk (only modified columns are saved — storage.js — so a changed vgen-9 generator would
  grow woods next to a player's saved columns). gen_same.js (vgen 5/1) keeps its reference; a new test pins vgen-9
  Heartland chunks against the hash taken before this front (test/land_v9_same.js).
* **Terrain shapes in HEART.design** (so the chunks, the far ring and the horizon all see them):
  the **river valley** (a meandering centreline from the Pod Hills' east shoulder to the isles' lake: bed below sea
  level, gravel/sand banks, valley sides ~1:2.2 up to the land; a steep cirque at its head for the waterfall),
  the **lake** under the Sky Isles (a warped ellipse, beaches), the **rocky ridge** in the west (a crest ~30 high).
  D.river marks the channel so the biome is `river` (sand bed, the horizon draws water).
* **The land plan** (once per world per worker, ~0.2 s, no village/site calls — VG.probe safe): the sites
  (temple plaza W/E, Nova Haven's crescent ends, the Pod Hills' flanks, the station's foot, the citadel's foot, the
  canyon rim, the lake's north beach, three farmsteads, the lookout, the lighthouse, the fishing hut), the path network
  (MST + loops; A* on a 16-block grid over the designed height with slope, water and zone costs; Chaikin-smoothed; a
  1-block profile with a slope limit), bridges, lanterns/benches/milestones, the farm layouts, the set pieces, a
  2-block keep-out raster (paths, sites, fields) that the woods and the meadows read.
* **Per chunk** (a HEART.builder, last): stamp the plan's ops of the chunk (only over natural blocks: grass, dirt,
  stone, sand, gravel, plants, leaves, air — never over a designed module's block), plant the woods (a jittered grid
  of candidates over the chunk ± 6, each decided by global functions only — noise, hash, plan — so a tree is whole
  across chunk borders; GEN.TREES through the chunk's ColumnWriter), undergrowth, meadow grass and flower drifts,
  boulders, pond bowls, banks; write the spawns (`fauna` anchors, `mist` at the waterfall's pool).
* **Horizon**: lod.js and far.js ask `VR.Land.lodTrees(g, X, Z)` where the biome table has no trees (vgen ≥ 10 only):
  the woods are drawn as canopies far away. Measured on Very Low.
* **Game side**: entities.js reads the `fauna` anchors (deer at wood edges at dawn and dusk, the farm's herd in its
  pasture, ducks on the ponds as a floating prop, fish… see §4), vents.js the `mist` spawn (spray at the fall's foot,
  off on Very Low).

## 3. The pieces and their node tests (test/land.js — every rule a check, ALL OK / FAILED n)

1. **Paths that connect** — gravel/coarse-dirt paths 3 wide (flagstone near the temple and the farms), following the
   terrain; a lantern every 12 blocks, a bench and a milestone every 40, a bridge (wood deck on posts, stone arch
   over the river) wherever the path crosses water or a gully. Test: every site reachable from the temple on path
   cells (flood fill over the plan's cells, the city's own streets and the plaza count as joined at their anchors);
   no step between neighbouring path cells steeper than 1 block; every path cell over water is a bridge deck.
2. **Woods and groves** — broadleaf woods (oak, fancy oak, birch, round tree), conifer stands on the ridge (spruce,
   birch), blossom groves (cherry, round tree), poplar rows along some paths; undergrowth (fern, large fern, bush,
   mushrooms, leaf litter, moss), fallen logs, mossy boulders; orchards by the farms; hedgerows along paths and
   field edges; reeds and lily pads at ponds; tall grass and flower drifts (poppy, oxeye daisy, cornflower, allium,
   dandelion, azure bluet) in patches; clearings with a campfire ring and two tents. Test: forest cover 15–35 % of the
   open designed land; no trunk on a path or site cell; flower drifts clustered (index of dispersion of flowers in
   8×8 bins ≫ 1, nearest-neighbour ratio < 0.6).
3. **Fields and farms** — three farmsteads, each with 2–4 fields (wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroots, sunflowers,
   allium as lavender rows, a vineyard of trellis rows on a sunny slope), a fence round every field, a scarecrow, a
   barn, a windmill (its sails on a pivot cell, `windmill` spawn for a later turning), a well, a fenced pasture with
   a trough. Test: every field's crop cells lie flat (|Δy| ≤ 1 between neighbours), every field is fenced on its
   rim (gate allowed), every farm has a barn.
4. **Water** — the river (above), a waterfall at its head into a pool with mist and a small cave behind the fall,
   ponds (perched bowls) with lily pads, reeds and ducks, the lake's beaches, a lighthouse on a lake point, a fishing
   hut with a pier. Test: the river is continuous (water at every centreline sample, every bed sample ≤ the one
   upstream), its banks sand or gravel, the lighthouse stands on the shore (water within 4 blocks, its foot dry).
5. **Rocks and relief** — boulders and outcrops (stone, andesite, mossy cobble with moss carpet) on the slopes, the
   rocky ridge with a lookout tower (stairs inside, a platform with a railing and a lantern), a stone shrine with a
   lantern at a path junction, mushroom rings in the woods, the waterfall cave. No crystal anywhere (GEN.noCry: the
   test scans every generated chunk of the land for crystal blocks).
6. **Animals at places** — `fauna` spawns: deer at wood edges (dawn, dusk), sheep in the farms' pastures, ducks on the
   ponds (a floating duck prop, vm_duck, appended late — no existing block serves), fish at the pier (bubbles/jumps
   in the particle tier). Test: the anchors exist where the plan says (pasture, pond, wood edge), entities' anchor
   spawning picks the right kind (node test on the pure part).
